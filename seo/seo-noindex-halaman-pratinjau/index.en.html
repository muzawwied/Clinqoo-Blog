Put noindex on Clincoo Preview Pages, Do Not Let Drafts Hit Search Results

Preview and staging URLs should not be indexed. A robots noindex meta keeps only production pages in Google.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo projects often share a preview URL that matches production, plus draft pages. If that link is public, search engines can index unfinished copy.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, add meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" on the preview template or staging host. Do not put noindex on production pages you want found.

robots.txt alone is not enough for a URL that is already known. Meta robots and an X-Robots-Tag header are clearer for a single page.

Ask AI only to insert the meta in the draft head. Paste the existing head; do not regenerate the whole site.

After deploy to app.clincoo.buzz, view source and confirm public pages stay index, follow. Indexed drafts damage the brand title in search.
