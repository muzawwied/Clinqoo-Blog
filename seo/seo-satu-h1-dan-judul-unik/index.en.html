How to Keep One H1 and a Unique Page Title

Search engines use the title and h1 to tell URLs apart. On editor.clincoo.buzz write a title that names the page job, then one h1 with the same words.

Do not duplicate the template title

About, pricing, and blog pages must not share one title. Put the section name before the Clincoo brand.

One h1, lower headings beneath it

Use h1 once in the main content. Subsections use h2. Check the preview on app.clincoo.buzz with the document outline.

Match the title promise and the first paragraph

If the title promises a deploy guide, the first paragraph must be that. Keep sample titles on blog.clincoo.buzz.
