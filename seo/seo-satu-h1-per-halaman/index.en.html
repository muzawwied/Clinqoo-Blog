One H1 per Page on a Clincoo Site

A clear main heading helps readers and search engines. Do not duplicate H1 in the hero and the navbar.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Templates often put the logo in an H1 and repeat another H1 in the hero. Two main titles then compete.

Pick one H1 that states the page topic. Keep the logo in a link or a span. Use H2 and H3 in order for subheads.

If the AI tidies headings, limit the job to one file. Ask for a change list: which tags drop from H1 to H2.

Check preview: a reader should see the page title without guessing. Search engines follow the same structure.

Clincoo ships the markup as it is. Heading hierarchy is your job before release.
