Use a Safe OG Image Ratio When Sharing a Clincoo Page

A 1:1 image gets cropped on social cards. Prepare a 1200x630 asset for sharing.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A square logo on editor.clincoo.buzz looks tiny or cropped in Facebook and X previews.

Upload a 1200x630 PNG or JPG. Set og:image to an absolute https URL.

Do not ask AI for stock photos. Ask only for the og:image tag and dimensions.

Test a social debugger, then store the asset on app.clincoo.buzz and a note on blog.clincoo.buzz.
