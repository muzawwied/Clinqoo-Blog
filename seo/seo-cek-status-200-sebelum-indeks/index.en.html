Check for HTTP 200 before Sending a Clincoo URL to the Index

A 404 or a redirect chain wastes crawl budget. Confirm the final document returns 200.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the Network tab of the editor.clincoo.buzz preview, the main document must be 200, not 404 after a slug change.

If a redirect exists, keep one 301 hop to the canonical URL, then 200.

Paste the status line to AI, not the whole HAR. Ask for one route fix.

Repeat on app.clincoo.buzz and link the status checklist on blog.clincoo.buzz.
