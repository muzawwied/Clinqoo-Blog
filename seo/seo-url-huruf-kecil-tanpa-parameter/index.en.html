How to Keep Public URLs Lowercase and Free of Tracking Parameters

The URLs /Harga and /harga?utm=x are often treated as two pages. In editor.clincoo.buzz internal links use lowercase, hyphens, and no tracking parameters.

One canonical form

Pick /harga/ or /harga, then use that form in every link and canonical. Do not mix capitals in the menu. utm may appear on outbound campaign links, not on internal hrefs.

Canonical points at the clean form

If a visitor arrives with a tracking query, canonical still points at the URL without the query. Do not put utm in canonical.

Check the preview

On app.clincoo.buzz click the menu and read the hrefs. They should be lowercase and query-free. Keep the pattern on blog.clincoo.buzz.
