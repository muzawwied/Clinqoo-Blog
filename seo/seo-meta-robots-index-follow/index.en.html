Set Meta Robots to Index Follow on Public Clincoo Pages

A public page that keeps noindex never shows in search. Check the robots meta before release.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a draft template often carries noindex onto a page that is already public.

View Source on the public URL. Confirm the robots meta is index, follow, not noindex.

Do not ask AI to delete every meta tag. Ask only to change robots on public app.clincoo.buzz routes.

Document the preview exception on blog.clincoo.buzz so drafts stay noindex.
