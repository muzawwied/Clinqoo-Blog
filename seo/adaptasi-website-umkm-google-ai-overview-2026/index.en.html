How Indonesian SMEs Should Adapt Websites to Google AI Overviews in 2026

Zero-click is rising and AI Overviews appear on most Indonesian queries. Here is how an SME site can still get cited and clicked.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

If organic traffic feels quieter even while rankings look intact, visitors may already be satisfied on the Google results page. That is not always a quality failure; the answer is often summarized in an AI Overview before anyone clicks. Intura’s July 2026 study of 1,000 Bahasa Indonesia keywords found that 93 percent of queries produced an AI Overview, with near-total coverage in computers and health. Local SEO commentary citing Ahrefs has also placed Indonesia among the markets with the highest AI Overview coverage in the sample. The ten-blue-links playbook from five years ago is no longer enough for an SME website.

Understand the search change before you rebuild the whole site. At I/O 2026 Google described Search as AI search through and through: AI Mode passed one billion monthly active users, AI Overviews passed 2.5 billion, and the search box now accepts text, images, files, and Chrome tabs. Generative answers still include links, and Google says it has added more inline links and site previews so people have a reason to keep reading. For a business owner the metrics that matter are not clicks alone: whether the brand is named in the summary, whether your URL appears as a source, and whether the people who do arrive actually chat or buy.

First practical step: write pages that fully answer one question instead of stacking slogans. AI Overviews prefer clear blocks—a short definition, numbered steps, explicit price or timing, and first-hand detail that cannot be copied from a competitor. If you sell office catering in Bekasi, do not stop at “delicious quality menus.” Publish the package, minimum portions, delivery radius, order cutoff, and one delivery story that actually happened. Citation research in Indonesia often finds YouTube among the most-cited sources; thin, generic site copy loses to first-person material. FAQ pages still help when the questions match what customers type and the answers are specific to your shop.

Second: clean up the technical signals that make a page trustworthy as a source. Titles, headings, and the opening paragraph should name the topic in human language, not stuffed keywords. Add relevant schema—LocalBusiness, FAQPage, or Product—only when the data is true. Speed up the mobile version; most Indonesian searches happen on phones, and a long AI Overview makes people impatient with a heavy site. Since June 2026 Google has been testing Search Generative AI performance reports in Search Console: impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode, which URLs appeared, country, and device. If that report is in your account, separate generative impressions from classic clicks so a traffic dip is not misread as a penalty.

Third: design the page as if the visitor already has half an answer. They have read the AI summary; they now need proof, price, original photos, and a button that does not require thinking. Put the promise and CTA in the first screen. Show reviews with names and cities. Offer a simple package comparison table. If you assemble the site in a builder such as Clincoo, test the phone preview before chasing desktop effects. Google is also testing a Search Console control so owners can choose whether their content grounds AI answers. Decide calmly: for product catalogs and useful articles it is usually better to remain a source than to hide, as long as the facts on the page would not embarrass you if quoted verbatim.

Three mistakes keep SMEs out of citations. First, “ultimate guides” that clone a competitor’s outline with no original data; language models can summarize ten generic versions without naming anyone. Second, locking the important facts inside images or PDFs so the model cannot extract a sentence. Third, chasing broad volume (“how to start an online business”) when the queries that produce chats are narrow and commercial (“catering price 50 pax Bekasi today”). Zero-click is highest on definition queries; click opportunity lives in queries that need location, stock, schedule, or a decision. Shift the content calendar there, then measure WhatsApp chats and form submits, not sessions alone.

Adapting an SME website to Google AI Overviews in 2026 is not about buying an expensive SEO suite. It is about pages that can be cited safely, clean technical signals, and an offer that still makes sense after someone has read the AI summary. Start this week with three URLs: the homepage, one flagship service, and one article that answers the question customers ask most. Update the facts, add local proof, check Search Console, and republish. Search will keep summarizing. The sites that last are the ones that still give people a reason to click after that summary appears.
