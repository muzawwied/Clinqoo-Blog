Fill Image Alt Text on a Clincoo Site

An image with no alt is empty for screen readers and search. One descriptive phrase is enough.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates sometimes leave img tags with no alt or alt="image". That does not describe what is visible.

Write alt as a short sentence: subject and context. Do not stuff keywords into alt.

Purely decorative images may use an intentional empty alt (alt=""). Product, team, and hero photos need text.

Ask the AI to add alt only on the page file you have open. Check the result in preview.

Honest alt helps access and SEO together. Clincoo does not fill alt automatically at deploy.
