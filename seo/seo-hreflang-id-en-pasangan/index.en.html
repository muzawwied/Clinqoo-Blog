Pair hreflang id and en on Clincoo Articles

Language versions without reciprocal links make search engines pick the wrong URL.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Every bilingual article on editor.clincoo.buzz needs rel alternate hreflang id and en that point at each other.

Add x-default to the version visitors open most. Do not point it at a 404.

Ask AI for two link tags, not a head rewrite. Check View Source on both files.

Preview the pair on app.clincoo.buzz. Official examples live on blog.clincoo.buzz.
