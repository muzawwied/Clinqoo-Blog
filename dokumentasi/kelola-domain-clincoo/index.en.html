Manage Clincoo Domains and DNS: Verification, Records, SSL, to Cache

2026-10-05

The Domain page at app.clincoo.buzz/domain is the single place to manage your custom domain: verify ownership through nameservers, manage DNS records, monitor SSL certificates, and control caching. Once your domain is active, your Clincoo site can run on your own domain over HTTPS.

Quick flow

  1. Add the domain — enter your domain name on the Domain page.
  2. Verify ownership — change the nameservers at your registrar to the pair Clincoo assigns.
  3. Manage — control DNS records, zone, SSL/TLS, and cache from the available tabs.

Adding a domain

Open the Domain page, click Tambah Domain (Add Domain), and enter the domain name (for example example.com) without https:// and without a trailing slash. The domain appears in your list with its verification status.

Verifying via nameservers

Clincoo assigns a pair of nameservers for your domain. Change the nameservers at your registrar, then return to the verification page and press the check button. Propagation usually takes minutes, at most 24 hours — pressing the check button repeatedly does not speed it up. Until the nameservers point correctly, your DNS records are not active.

Managing DNS records

Once the domain is verified, the DNS tab lists all your records. You can add, edit, and delete records at any time.

TypePurpose
APoints a domain or subdomain to an IPv4 address.
AAAAPoints to an IPv6 address.
CNAMEMakes a subdomain follow another domain.
MXControls where the domain's email is delivered.
TXTStores text for third-party service verification, such as ownership claims.

Every record has a TTL — automatic, or a specific number of seconds if you want to control how fast changes spread. Records also have a traffic status: routed through the Clincoo network (with filtering) or DNS-only.

SSL/TLS and cache

The SSL/TLS tab monitors your domain's certificate status, while the Cache tab helps make sure visitors always get the latest version of your site. The Zone tab holds advanced settings for active domains.

Tips

  1. Copy the nameserver pair exactly as shown — one wrong character breaks verification.
  2. After adding a new record, wait a moment before testing; the automatic TTL is usually fast enough.
  3. A domain you no longer use can be removed from its detail page — move your site first.

FAQ

How long does nameserver verification take?

Usually minutes, at most 24 hours depending on your registrar. If the check keeps failing, make sure the nameservers are entered exactly, then wait and try again.

Are DNS records active immediately after being added?

New records are saved right away, but only work fully once your domain's nameservers are verified and pointed to Clincoo.

Can I use my domain without changing nameservers?

Full DNS management from the Domain page requires nameserver verification. For an alternative way to connect a domain to your deployed site, see the How to Set Up a Custom Domain article.

How do I delete a domain?

Open the domain's details on the Domain page and choose Hapus (Delete). Make sure no important site is still connected before deleting.
