Record Why a Design Decision Exists in a Clincoo Project

One paragraph of rationale stops the AI and new teammates from reviving a rejected option.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Without notes, three weeks later someone asks why the hero has no slider. The answer “it did not fit on a phone” lived only in a deleted chat.

Add a short file at the project root, for example DECISIONS.md. Each entry: ISO date, page, chosen option, and one sentence of why. Not an essay.

When you ask the AI to change a layout, paste the related entry. Reject suggestions that collide with a still-valid decision. If a decision is stale, strike it and add a new date.

This record also helps a pre-deploy review. A code diff is easier to read when you know why a class must not be deleted.

Clincoo stores plain text files. Decision notes belong in the project, not in a chat that disappears.
