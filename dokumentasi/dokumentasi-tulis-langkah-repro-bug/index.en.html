Write Repeatable Clincoo Bug Reproduction Steps

A 'it's broken' report without steps makes AI and teammates guess. Order the actions, URL, and visible result.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Bugs on editor.clincoo.buzz often cannot be repeated because the note only says 'the form failed'. Without steps, AI invents a fix.

Number the steps: open the URL, click what, fill which field, then what you see versus what you expected. One change per step.

Include the page slug and whether it is preview or app.clincoo.buzz. Do not mix two flows in one report.

Paste those steps to the AI assistant. Do not ask it to 'fix everything' before the repro works once.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz uses the same pattern: reproduce first, then change code.
