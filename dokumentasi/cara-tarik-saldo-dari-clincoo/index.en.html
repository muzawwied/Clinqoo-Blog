How to Withdraw Your Clincoo Earnings

Earnings from ClincooPay QRIS payments land in your project balance on app.clincoo.buzz and can be withdrawn anytime from the same page. This guide walks through the official withdrawal flow: adding a destination, OTP verification, and reading the withdrawal history.

Before you withdraw

  • Payments must be active in your project and your available balance must cover it — the minimum withdrawal is Rp 10,000, fees included.
  • Have access to the project owner email: the withdrawal verification code is sent there.

Add a withdrawal destination

In your project Settings, open Payments and choose a withdrawal destination. Pick the e-wallet type and enter a phone number that is truly yours — the e-wallet account holder name must match the project owner. For safety, Clincoo may ask for email OTP verification when a new destination looks suspicious, such as frequent changes or a new network.

Submit the withdrawal with OTP

  1. Enter the amount you want to cash out.
  2. Clincoo sends a 6-digit OTP to the project owner email. The code is valid for 10 minutes with at most 5 attempts.
  3. Enter the code to submit. The amount plus the service fee is immediately held from your available balance.

The service fee is deducted automatically and the final figure is always shown before you confirm. If the balance falls short, lower the amount instead of clicking the same button twice.

Read the withdrawal history

Every withdrawal is recorded with its status: Pending while processed, Selesai once the funds are sent, or Ditolak. A rejected withdrawal returns the amount and fee to the project balance. Status changes are also emailed to the project owner.

If a withdrawal stays pending for long, start with Common Problems before submitting a new one. Technical details of the withdrawal API are in the ClincooPay API reference.
