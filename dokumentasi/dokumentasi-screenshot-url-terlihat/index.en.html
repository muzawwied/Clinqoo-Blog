Take Clincoo Screenshots with the URL Visible

A crop without the address bar drops the slug and query. Include the full URL when you ask for help.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A screenshot cropped to a button forces people to guess the page. Query strings and hashes often cause bugs on app.clincoo.buzz.

Capture the full window or keep the address bar visible. Paste the raw URL in the note, not only the image.

Redact private data before upload. Do not send tokens in the query to a public chat.

Give AI the URL plus the screenshot. Do not ask AI to guess the route from button pixels.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz and editor.clincoo.buzz use different slugs; a visible URL ends that guesswork.
