Clincoo Plans and Pricing

Clincoo uses a freemium model: a free plan generous enough to genuinely get started, and paid plans as your needs grow. Current prices always show on the Subscription page in your account — this article explains the structure and limits of each plan.

Starter (free)

Free forever, designed so you can truly build and publish sites without paying: 3 active projects, 50 AI messages per month, 5 deploys per month, 1 collaborator per project, and 1 custom domain with automatic HTTPS.

Pro

For regular work: the deploy quota rises to 25x per month, 5 collaborators per project, and larger quotas across the rest of the features.

Business

For teams and production needs: unlimited deploys, 20 collaborators per project, and full support for heavier operations.

Upgrading and canceling

Everything happens on the Subscription page in your account — or tap the crown icon on a premium feature to see plan options. Upgrades activate as soon as payment is confirmed; canceling takes effect at the end of the current billing period, with no penalty, and sites you have already deployed stay online.

Premium features and the crown marker

Certain features — such as additional domains beyond your plan quota — carry a crown icon marking them as premium. The marker lets you see at a glance what requires a higher plan without trial and error.

If you hit a payment or quota snag, check Common Issues and Solutions. Quick answers live in the FAQ.
