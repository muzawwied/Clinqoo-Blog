Deploy Your Site and Use Your Own Domain

Publishing a site is the best moment of any project. This article explains how deploying works in Clincoo, the quota rules, and how to use your own domain.

How to deploy

Inside a project, open Build & Deployment and hit deploy. Clincoo builds your site from the project files and publishes it to the internet over the Cloudflare network. Every project gets its own online address you can share with anyone.

Deploy quota

Deploys are limited per plan to keep the infrastructure healthy: Starter gets 5 web deploys per month, Pro up to 25 per month, and Business unlimited. For a stable site you do not need to redeploy after every edit — use live preview first, then deploy when it is truly ready.

Custom domain

The default address is a fine start, but your own domain makes a site look serious. The custom domain feature lives in the project settings menu: enter your domain, set the DNS records as instructed, and Clincoo connects it to your site with automatic HTTPS. The crown icon in the menu marks premium features — the free plan includes one domain, and more are part of the paid plans.

Tips before deploying

  • Check live preview at phone and desktop screen sizes.
  • Make sure all links and images use relative paths so nothing breaks after publishing.
  • Name your main file index.html so the site opens automatically.

Once deployed, your site address is live and ready to share. If anything goes wrong, see Common Problems and How to Fix Them in the Help Center.
