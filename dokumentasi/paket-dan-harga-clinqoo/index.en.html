Clincoo Plans and Pricing: Starter, Pro, and Business

Clincoo has three plans designed around your journey: start free, move up as needs grow. This article summarizes all three honestly, with no hidden fine print.

Starter — Free

  • Up to 3 active projects
  • 1 collaborator per project
  • AI chat in every project (50 messages per month)
  • 3 ready-to-use project templates
  • Scheduled tasks and notifications
  • 5 web deploys per month

Pro — Rp49,000 per month

  • Up to 10 active projects
  • Up to 5 collaborators per project
  • All Starter features: AI chat, templates, scheduled tasks
  • AI chat up to 500 messages per month (max 50 per day)
  • Web deploys up to 25x per month
  • Import GitHub repositories into your workspace

Business — Rp129,000 per month

  • All Pro features
  • Up to 50 active projects
  • Up to 20 collaborators per project
  • Advanced access control and project security
  • Unlimited web deploys

Upgrading, downgrading, and billing

Plans are managed from the Subscription page in your account. You can upgrade anytime, and premium features unlock as soon as payment is confirmed. When a premium feature is locked behind the crown icon, tapping it shows the full plan list. Monthly or yearly billing is available.

Pick the plan that matches real needs: most people start on Starter, then move to Pro when their projects or team outgrow the free limits. No subscription is needed just to try — every core feature can be explored on the free plan first.
