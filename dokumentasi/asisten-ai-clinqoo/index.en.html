Clinqoo AI Assistant

The AI assistant lives inside every Clincoo project and understands your workspace. It answers questions, writes and edits code, manages files, and even runs commands — you just ask in everyday language.

This article covers what it can do, how to get the best results, and where its limits are.

What the assistant can do

CapabilityWhat you can ask for
Workspace file managementCreating, reading, editing, renaming, searching, and deleting files or folders in your project.
Linux terminalRunning commands in a persistent terminal — sessions survive about 10 minutes between commands, and installed tools stay for the session.
Web searchFetching up-to-date information from the internet to ground its answers.
Reading web pagesTaking the content of a URL and summarizing it in the context of your work.
Site screenshotsCapturing a URL as an image — handy for checking a deployment.
Project deployPublishing your active project to the internet on your request.
Environment variablesViewing and adding project env vars, plus testing the validity of secrets and API keys.
GitHub connectorReading and managing your GitHub repositories through the official integration.
Cloudflare APIRunning the Cloudflare operations your token allows, such as DNS and Pages.

Asking in a way that works

  • Give context: the file name, the symptom you see, and the outcome you expect. Specific questions get precise answers.
  • One problem per conversation. Mixed threads tend to drift off target.
  • If a suggestion misses the mark, reject it and ask again with more detail — the full pattern is covered in Common Issues and Solutions.

Transparency and limits

The AI assistant is an assistant, not an automatic site builder. It cannot click buttons or fill in forms on external sites in real time, and it cannot test your site as a real user would. You review every change and decide what gets saved.

AI can be wrong or incomplete — especially about things outside Clincoo. For questions about Clinqoo itself, it answers from the official knowledge base; for everything else, verify results before relying on them. Keep important credentials in env vars rather than pasting them into a conversation.

Conversation quota follows your plan — Starter includes 50 messages per month. Details are in Plans and Pricing.
