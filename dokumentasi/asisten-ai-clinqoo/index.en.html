The Clincoo AI Assistant: How to Use It and Its Limits

The AI assistant is the most-used feature in Clincoo — and the most misunderstood. This article explains how to use it well and what its limits are, so your expectations stay realistic.

The AI is an assistant, not a site builder

The Clincoo AI helps explain code, write new code snippets, draft page copy, and hunt for bugs. But it does not operate your site for real: it cannot click buttons on your site, fill in forms, or test interactions the way a human can. You decide, review, and test.

How to ask good questions

  • Give context: which file, which page, what you already tried.
  • Describe symptoms concretely instead of "please fix it" — paste error messages when you have them.
  • Ask for small, specific changes; one problem per conversation.
  • Ask the AI to explain its reasoning instead of rewriting whole files.

Reviewing AI suggestions

AI suggestions can always be accepted or rejected. Before accepting: read what changed, make sure no other files were touched, and check the result in live preview. If a suggestion changes too much at once, ask for a smaller version.

Chat quota

AI messages are limited per plan: Starter gets 50 messages per month, Pro up to 500 per month (max 50 per day), and Business follows the Pro limits. These quotas keep the service stable for everyone. You can check your remaining quota on the subscription page.

Bottom line: treat the AI like a fast colleague who needs direction. The clearer your instructions, the better the results.
