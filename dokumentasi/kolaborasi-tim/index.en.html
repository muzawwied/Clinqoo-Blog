Team Collaboration: Inviting Members and Sharing Roles

A good site is rarely one person's work. Clincoo lets several people work in the same project, each with a clear role.

Inviting members

Open a project and go to the Collaboration menu. There you can see the team member list and invite new people by email. Invitations are accepted via a link, and joined members can open the project from their own dashboard.

Member roles

The project owner holds full control: inviting, removing members, deleting the project, and managing domains. Collaborator members can edit files and use work features according to the permissions granted. This separation keeps the site from changing without the owner's knowledge.

Limits per plan

  • Starter: 1 collaborator per project.
  • Pro: up to 5 collaborators per project.
  • Business: up to 20 collaborators per project, plus advanced access control and security.

The crown icon around collaboration features marks the premium boundary: the general pattern is one free, then paid for extras. If your team grows, you can upgrade anytime from the subscription page.
