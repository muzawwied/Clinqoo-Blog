Wrap Clincoo Images in figure and figcaption, Not a Bare Div

figure marks media and its caption as one unit. figcaption gives credit and context without a detached paragraph.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates put an img inside a div.card and write the credit in a small p below. For a screen reader those two nodes are unrelated. Search engines also cannot tell which text explains the image.

Change the wrapper to figure. Put the img inside it and write one figcaption. Use figcaption for credit, source, or a single context sentence. Do not repeat the full alt text.

Alt on the img stays required: short and functional. figcaption may be longer. If the image is decorative, use empty alt and skip figure when there is no caption.

Ask AI to wrap only image blocks that already have a credit. Paste one card-sized figure example, not a full gallery rewrite.

After deploy, check the accessibility outline in DevTools. A correct figure keeps service pages on app.clincoo.buzz clear when read in order.
