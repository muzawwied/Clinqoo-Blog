Mark Clincoo Publish Dates with time and datetime

Plain date text is hard for machines and screen readers to parse. A time element plus datetime gives a stable machine value.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many cards on blog.clincoo.buzz write 2 October 2026 as plain text. Search engines and screen readers do not always know it is a date.

Wrap it with time datetime="2026-10-02". Visible text can stay in Indonesian; the attribute is what machines use.

Do not leave datetime empty or put a mixed 02/10/2026 in the attribute. Use an ISO date so day and month are not swapped.

For a clock time, add the time and zone, for example 2026-10-02T08:00:00+07:00, only if the hour actually matters.

When stuck, paste the time snippet to AI and ask it to check that datetime is valid. Do not ask AI to rewrite the whole page.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz: the date stays human-readable, and view-source shows datetime.
