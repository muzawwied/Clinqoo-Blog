Do Not Use a Profile Photo as a Clincoo Site Favicon

A face photo breaks at 16 pixels and is hard to spot in a tab row. Use a simple mark.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo project tab uses the owner photo. In a row of 20 tabs everything becomes a blur.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, switch to a one-color mark or a two-letter monogram.

Do not crop a square photo and expect it to stay readable. Face detail dies at 16px.

Ask AI to draft a monogram mark from the brand name. Paste a small SVG, not a photo.

A clear mark on app.clincoo.buzz speeds picking the right editor tab.
