Keep Every Clincoo Favicon Size in One Folder

Icons scattered across root, assets, and random uploads. One icons folder makes a brand swap a single step.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo project has favicon.ico at root, a png in assets, and leftover uploads with no names.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, make an icons/ folder and move 32, 180, and 192 there. Update the hrefs.

Do not leave two files named logo.png with different contents. Tabs will pick at random.

Ask AI to tidy icon paths in head. Paste the folder file list, not the whole repo.

Tidy assets on app.clincoo.buzz speed up a brand change without breaking old tabs.
