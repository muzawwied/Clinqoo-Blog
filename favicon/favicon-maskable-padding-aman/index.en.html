Leave Safe Padding on a Clincoo Maskable Icon

A maskable icon without padding gets cropped by the Android launcher. Keep the logo in the inner 80 percent.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo shortcut on Android crops the logo corners until the letter C vanishes.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, make a 192 PNG with the logo centered and 10 percent padding on each side.

Do not mark purpose maskable on a file that hugs the edge. Launchers use a circle or squircle.

Ask AI to add padding on the SVG before you export PNG. Paste the path, not the whole manifest.

An intact icon on app.clincoo.buzz makes the editor shortcut easy to spot.
