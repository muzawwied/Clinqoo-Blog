Ship a 192px Icon in the Manifest for Clincoo Shortcuts

A manifest without a 192 icon makes Android shortcuts use a messy screenshot. One PNG is enough to start.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A visitor pins a Clincoo site to an Android home screen. The icon becomes a random screenshot.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, upload a 192×192 PNG and fill icons in manifest.json.

Do not set purpose maskable without safe padding. Corners get cropped by the launcher.

Ask AI to write a 192 icons entry. Paste the manifest, not the whole PWA.

A branded shortcut to app.clincoo.buzz makes opening the editor daily easier.
