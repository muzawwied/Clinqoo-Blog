Add a 180px Apple Touch Icon for Clincoo Shortcuts on iOS

Without apple-touch-icon, an iPhone home screen uses a page snapshot. A 180px icon keeps the brand tidy.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A visitor adds a Clincoo site to the home screen. iOS crops a hero screenshot, not the logo.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, provide a 180×180 PNG and a rel apple-touch-icon link. Avoid full transparency at the edges.

Do not reuse a 32 favicon for Apple touch. The small size looks broken when pinned.

Ask AI to write one apple-touch-icon tag. Paste the asset folder path.

A shortcut to app.clincoo.buzz that uses the Clincoo logo is easier to spot in the icon row.
