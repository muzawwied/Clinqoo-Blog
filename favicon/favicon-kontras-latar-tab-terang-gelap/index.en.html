Check Clincoo Favicon Contrast on Light and Dark Tabs

A transparent logo vanishes on a dark tab. Add a solid background or two assets so the icon stays readable.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A white Clincoo icon on transparency disappears when the user runs a dark tab theme.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, test the icon in light and dark Chrome. Add a background circle if needed.

Do not rely on prefers-color-scheme for the favicon unless you ship two files.

Ask AI to draft an icon version with a solid background. Paste a simple SVG, not a design pack.

A readable icon on app.clincoo.buzz keeps the brand visible in a row of tabs.
