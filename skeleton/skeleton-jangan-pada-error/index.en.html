Do Not Draw a Clincoo Skeleton on Top of an Error Message

Bone cards stacked under a failure banner leave users unsure which state is real.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A project page on app.clincoo.buzz shows a 500 error then still renders three skeletons underneath.

If the status is error, clear the bone slots. One banner is enough. Retry is what renders a new skeleton.

Do not pulse a region that is already aria-invalid or role=alert.

Paste the error branch to AI. Ask to prioritize the message, not extra bones.

Test on editor.clincoo.buzz with a fake endpoint. Record the UI order on blog.clincoo.buzz.
