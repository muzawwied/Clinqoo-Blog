Show One Clincoo Skeleton Item First, Do Not Wait for the Full List

Waiting for the whole array before drawing bones leaves the screen blank longer. One item is already a signal.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo project list holds the skeleton until fetch finishes. Visitors see white for several seconds.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, draw one bone card as soon as the page is ready. Add the rest when the count is known.

Do not change the card count mid-fetch. Height jumps shift the layout.

Ask AI to split prefetch state from complete state. Paste the fetch handler only.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz feels faster when the first bone appears before data arrives.
