Use Different Clincoo Skeleton Patterns for List and Detail

Grid cards on an article page look fake. Bone shape must mimic the destination layout.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A detail page on editor.clincoo.buzz uses the list-card skeleton. The article title feels like a feed.

Make two patterns: short rows for lists, a title block plus paragraphs for detail. Do not one-component every route.

Match page padding so the jump from bone to content does not shift the nav.

Ask AI for two skeleton markups from two layouts. Do not request a new library.

Compare list and detail routes on app.clincoo.buzz. Write the difference on blog.clincoo.buzz.
