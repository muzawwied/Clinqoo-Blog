Match Clincoo Skeleton Contrast in Dark and Light Mode

A placeholder that vanishes in dark theme feels like a blank page. Bone color must stay readable.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo skeleton cards use light gray. In dark mode on app.clincoo.buzz those boxes nearly vanish into the background.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, set two skeleton color tokens: light and dark. Do not hardcode #eee.

Do a short contrast check: bones must be visible without glowing. Pulse should change opacity, not width.

Ask AI to map tokens to prefers-color-scheme. Paste the theme CSS snippet, not the whole file.

Clincoo serves the theme you pick. Contrasting skeletons keep waiting for data honest.
