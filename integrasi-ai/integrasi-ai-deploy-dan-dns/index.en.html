Deploy Sites and Set Up Domain DNS through AI Commands

The AI can publish your project to the internet and manage custom domain CNAME records when the DNS zone is in your Cloudflare account.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

From inside the chat, the AI can run the deploy: your project is published to the internet via Cloudflare Pages without opening the Cloudflare dashboard.

For a custom domain, the AI can check DNS status, create a CNAME record pointing at your project site, and clean up conflicting old records.

This automation works when your domain zone lives in the Cloudflare account saved in your deploy settings. Domains at other providers get guided manually.

The custom domain page has a set-DNS-with-AI button. Click it and the conversation opens with the right domain context preloaded.

After a record is created, wait for DNS propagation before calling it a failure. The deploy page shows the domain's real status.
