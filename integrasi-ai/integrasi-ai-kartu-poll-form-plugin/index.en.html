POLL, FORM, and PLUGIN Cards: How the Clinqoo AI Asks Cleanly

The AI asks multiple-choice questions, input forms, and integration buttons through interactive cards instead of long text.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

When the AI needs a design decision, it sends a POLL card: a short question with ready-to-click options. You pick one and the work continues.

For data like an API key, the AI sends a FORM card. What you enter goes straight back into the conversation without manual pasting.

If the task needs GitHub, Google Drive, Notion, or Calendar, the AI shows a PLUGIN card with a direct connect button.

When a long answer gets cut off, the system continues it automatically. You never need to type continue or open a new session.

Card questions are reserved for details that truly change the result. Small technical details are settled by the AI itself.
