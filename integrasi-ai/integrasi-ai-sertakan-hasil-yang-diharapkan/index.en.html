Include the Expected Result When Asking Clincoo AI for Help

Write what happens, what should happen, and which file is involved. Clincoo AI stops guessing when the expectation is explicit.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

When a Clincoo preview does not match the design, do not just write fix it. Name the symptom, the expected result, and the file path.

A useful example: the Send button in index.html stays grey after the inputs are filled. Expected: the button enables when both fields are valid.

Attach a short snippet from editor.clincoo.buzz, not the whole file. Five to fifteen lines around the symptom is enough.

Ask the AI to explain the cause first, then suggest a minimal change. Reject advice that rewrites the global stylesheet.

After applying it, reload the preview on app.clincoo.buzz and compare it with the result you wrote at the start.
