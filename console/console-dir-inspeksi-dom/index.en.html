Inspect Clincoo DOM Nodes with console.dir instead of log

Logging an element prints markup. dir prints object properties you can follow while debugging.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

console.log(form) on a Clincoo page often shows only the HTML tag. Properties such as checkValidity stay hidden.

Use console.dir(form) for the property tree. Open dataset, validity, and classList without jumping to the Elements panel.

dir also helps with plain objects proxied by a template. log sometimes renders a misleading toString.

Do not dump all of document.body. Pick the node your script just changed.

Once you understand the object shape, remove dir. Inspection traces do not belong on a site deployed from Clincoo.
