Split Ctrl and Meta for Clincoo Shortcuts on Windows and Mac

A shortcut that only checks ctrlKey fails on Mac. Use metaKey for Command.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, Ctrl+S saves on Windows. On Mac, Command+S does nothing.

Detect the platform or accept ctrlKey or metaKey for save. Do not override Command+T.

Send the keydown listener to AI. Ask for a Windows/Mac branch, not a new keymap.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz on both OS. Document the combo on blog.clincoo.buzz.
