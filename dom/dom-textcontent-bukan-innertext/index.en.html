Fill Clincoo Text with textContent, Not innerText or innerHTML

innerText forces layout. innerHTML parses markup. textContent writes the string as-is.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo scripts that fill a price via innerHTML parse unexpected characters. innerText forces the browser to compute hidden styles first.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, set el.textContent = value. The string appears as-is. No new tags, no extra reflow just to read.

Use innerHTML only when you assemble markup you fully control. Do not mix visitor data into it. For labels, headings, and status, textContent is enough.
