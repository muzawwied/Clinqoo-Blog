Move Clincoo Focus into a Dialog After You Insert the Node

A dialog that appears without focus leaves the keyboard stranded behind the overlay.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Appending a dialog in editor.clincoo.buzz without focus keeps Tab on the page behind the overlay.

After append, call dialog.querySelector(first focusable).focus(). Provide a close button that is in the tab order.

Store the previous node (document.activeElement) and restore focus when the dialog closes.

Ask AI to add the focus move, not only an is-open class. Paste the dialog markup plus the trigger button.

Test Tab and Escape on app.clincoo.buzz. Write a short checklist on blog.clincoo.buzz.
