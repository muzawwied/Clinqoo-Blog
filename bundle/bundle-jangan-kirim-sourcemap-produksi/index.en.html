Do Not Ship Source Maps with the Production Clincoo Bundle

.map files leak source and add megabytes to the first download.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Source maps help in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview, not on the public app.clincoo.buzz domain.

Turn off map generation for the production build or upload maps to private storage that opens only during debug.

Check Network: a 200 for a .map from an anonymous visitor is a leak.

Do not ask AI to enable source maps so production errors look prettier. Ask for structured logging instead.

Document the map policy on blog.clincoo.buzz so the team does not turn them back on during a panic.
