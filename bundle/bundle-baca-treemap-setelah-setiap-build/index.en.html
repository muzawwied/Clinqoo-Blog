Read the Clincoo Bundle Treemap after Every Preview Build

A single total hides which library just ballooned. The treemap shows the box that grew.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Turn on the bundler visualizer in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview once per release, not only during a panic.

Sort boxes from largest. Check whether icons, locales, or dark theme leaked into the main entry.

Compare the treemap before and after one feature. A new box without a ticket is a regression.

Upload a treemap snapshot to AI and ask for three split or replace candidates. Do not ask to switch bundlers.

Keep a compact treemap in the app.clincoo.buzz CI artifacts and link the lesson on blog.clincoo.buzz.
