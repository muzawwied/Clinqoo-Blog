Drop Polyfills Already Supported by Clincoo Target Browsers

A full core-js build often enters the bundle because of one old method.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Open the preview build treemap for editor.clincoo.buzz and look for a core-js or regenerator polyfill folder.

Match browserslist to the features you actually call. Promise and fetch already exist in modern target browsers.

Replace a global polyfill import with one small helper, or remove it if nothing calls it.

Ask AI to mark polyfill imports that are safe to drop. Do not let AI add a new Babel preset.

Record the byte saving in the app.clincoo.buzz release note and link it on blog.clincoo.buzz.
