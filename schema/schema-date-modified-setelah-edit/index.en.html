Update Clincoo Schema dateModified after a Real Edit

Keep datePublished stable. Change dateModified only when the article body actually changes, not for tiny typos with no substance.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Live Clincoo articles get edited. If JSON-LD only has datePublished, search engines cannot tell when the body last changed.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, keep the original datePublished. Add dateModified in ISO 8601 when you change paragraphs, facts, or code samples.

Do not bump the date on every automatic deploy. A jumping date with no real change looks like snippet gaming.

Match the schema date to any visible “Updated” line on the page. Follow schema.org/Article and Google Article guidance.

Clincoo does not fill dates for you. Two honest date fields keep the article history trustworthy.
