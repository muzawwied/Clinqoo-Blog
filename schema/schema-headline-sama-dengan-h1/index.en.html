Match the Clincoo JSON-LD headline to the H1, Not the Tab Title

A headline that differs from the H1 confuses the snippet. Copy the visible H1 text, then drop the brand suffix that exists only in the title.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A blog.clincoo.buzz page sometimes fills headline from document.title, including the Clincoo Blog suffix. The visible H1 is shorter, so the markup does not match the content.

Set headline from the trimmed H1 text. Do not copy the title tag. Change dateModified only when the content was actually edited.

One script type application/ld+json is enough. Validate brackets and commas in the console with JSON.parse before placing it in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Do not mark FAQ or HowTo if that block is not visible on the page. Fake markup is worse than no markup.

Ask AI to align one headline field with the H1 you paste. Do not ask for a full head rewrite. Check the app.clincoo.buzz preview, then view source.
