Use LocalBusiness Schema only when the Address is on the Clincoo Page

LocalBusiness needs a name, address, and area that actually appear on the page. Do not mark a fictional shop.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo client sites often want map visibility. LocalBusiness markup without a visible address hurts trust with search engines.

Write the business name, streetAddress, addressLocality, and telephone in HTML first. Then copy the same values into JSON-LD in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Use a more specific type such as Restaurant or Store only when that type matches the page text.

Do not fill openingHours or geo invented by AI. Check Google Local Business guidance and schema.org/LocalBusiness.

Clincoo does not add maps or NAP for you. Honest local schema supports snippets; it does not replace a contact page.
