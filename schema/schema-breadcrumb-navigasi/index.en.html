Mark Clincoo Breadcrumbs with JSON-LD

BreadcrumbList helps search engines show the category path. Keep itemListElement identical to the links on the page.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo article pages often show a category trail above the title, but without BreadcrumbList markup search results stay a flat URL.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, add one JSON-LD script of type BreadcrumbList. Each ListItem needs position, name, and item that match the visible links.

Start position at the home page. Do not invent extra categories just to make the snippet look deeper.

Validate the object with schema.org and Google rich-result guidance. Reject AI suggestions that add items with no HTML link.

Clincoo does not write breadcrumbs for you. An honest trail keeps navigation and search snippets aligned.
