Do Not Hide a Clincoo Action Behind :hover Only on Phones

Phones do not have a stable hover. A button that appears only on hover cannot be tapped. Show the primary action without depending on a cursor.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Cards in editor.clincoo.buzz often hide the edit button inside :hover. On a touch screen, the first tap only triggers hover, or the action never appears.

Keep the primary button visible below 768px. Hover may add emphasis, not the only path to the action.

Use @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) if a hover effect is truly for a mouse. Do not put an important function inside that query.

Test in DevTools device mode, then on a real phone. Keep the touch target around 44px and clear of the browser bar.

Ask AI to move one button out of the hover rule. Paste only the card CSS, not the whole stylesheet. Check the app.clincoo.buzz preview at 390px.
