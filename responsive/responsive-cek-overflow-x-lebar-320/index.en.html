Check overflow-x at 320px before a Clincoo Release

One line that cannot wrap makes the page slide sideways. Check at 320px before calling the layout done.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the app.clincoo.buzz preview, horizontal slide often comes from a long URL, a button, or a table without a wrapper.

Set the device toolbar to 320px. Scroll vertically and watch whether the document is wider than the viewport.

In the console, compare scrollWidth and clientWidth on documentElement. A small gap is enough for a stray scrollbar.

Fix the source: a min-width on a flex child, a word with no spaces, or padding added to width 100%.

Send the 320px capture to AI with the overflowing element. Ask for one change, then retest.
