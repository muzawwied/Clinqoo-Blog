Use min-height dvh for Clincoo Heroes, Not 100vh That Clips on Phones

100vh on iOS is often taller than the visible screen. dvh follows the dynamic viewport as the address bar moves.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo hero set to height 100vh often overshoots on iPhone: the main button sits under Safari’s address bar. Visitors must scroll just to see the primary call to action.

Switch to min-height: 100dvh or 100svh. Dynamic units follow the viewport as browser chrome shows or hides. min-height is safer than a fixed height so tall hero copy is not clipped.

Still test in DevTools and on a real phone. Rotate the screen, open Clincoo preview, and keep title, subtitle, and button on the first screen without covering the navbar.

If the hero uses a background image, pair object-fit: cover with inner padding. Do not lock overflow hidden on the body just to hide leftover bad vh.

Modern viewport units work in current browsers. Keep a min-height: 100vh fallback above the dvh rule if you still support older engines.
