Ask the AI to Compare Two Approaches, Not Just Pick One

When you hesitate between flex and grid, ask for the trade-offs. The design decision stays yours.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Beginners often ask the AI to "just pick the best one" and then paste without knowing why. In the Clincoo editor, change the question: "compare flexbox and grid for three product cards on desktop and one column on phone — pros and cons of each for my code."

A comparison shows the cost: when grid is tidier, when flex is simpler, what breaks if card content lengths differ. You then choose for the real page, not for a trend.

Do the same for two HTML structures, two palettes, or two menu patterns. Ask for a short example of each side, try the sensible one in preview, drop what feels heavy.

The AI is good at laying out options. You are the one who lives with the site tomorrow. Compare first, choose second — that is how an assistant helps without taking the wheel.
