Ask the AI to Check Color Contrast and Image Alt Text

Before you share a site, ask the Clincoo AI assistant to review text readability and image alt attributes.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A site that looks tidy on a laptop can be hard to read on a phone at noon. In Clincoo AI chat, ask for a basic access review: text contrast against the background, heading size, and whether every important image has alternative text.

Paste the image HTML snippet or name the color classes in CSS. The more concrete the excerpt, the easier it is for the AI to flag text that almost blends into the background or alt text that still says 'image1'.

Fixes are usually small: darken the text, lighten the background, or write alt text that describes the image — not the file name. Preview again after each change.

This is not a formal audit. It is a five-minute habit so first visitors — including people who skim or use a simple screen reader — do not stumble on the home page.
