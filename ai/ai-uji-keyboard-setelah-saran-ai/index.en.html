Test the Keyboard after Accepting Interaction Advice from Clincoo AI

Click and hover suggestions often forget Tab and Escape. Test the keyboard before you deploy a widget.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

AI in editor.clincoo.buzz adds overlays that close only on click. Keyboard users get stuck.

After you paste the widget, Tab to the close button, press Escape, and check that focus returns to the trigger.

If that fails, ask AI to add a keydown listener and focus restore, not a new visual class.

Also do a short screen-reader pass if labels exist. Do not rely on color alone.

Write a Tab-Escape-focus checklist on blog.clincoo.buzz before you ship on app.clincoo.buzz.
