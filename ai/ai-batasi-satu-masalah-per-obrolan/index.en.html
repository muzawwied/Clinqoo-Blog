One Problem per AI Chat: Keep Answers Sharp

An AI chat that mixes five problems at once goes fuzzy. Keep one symptom, one file, one question.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The Clincoo AI assistant can answer at length, but it still follows the conversation trail. If one thread piles a CSS bug, hero copy, and a new page idea together, the reply tends to be thin everywhere and deep nowhere.

Start a new chat when the topic moves. In the first message name the file, the symptom, and what you already tried. Example: "in style.css the .nav class breaks at 375px, the menu stacks; I already tried flex-wrap." That is enough for the AI to trace, not guess.

If the first answer misses, stay in the same thread with one clarification. Do not sneak a new request into the middle. When the issue is closed, save the useful snippet into your code, then open a new chat for the next task.

The habit feels stiff at first, then it saves quota and time. The AI stays an assistant; you keep the conversation from turning into a mixed basket.
