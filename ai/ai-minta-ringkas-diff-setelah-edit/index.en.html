Ask Clincoo AI for a Diff Summary after an Edit

After the AI touches a file, ask for the change list in plain language. You should be able to explain what changed.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A diff in the Clincoo editor can be long. Before you accept, ask the AI to summarize: which file, how many lines, and what it does in preview. Three bullets are easier to watch than a hundred red-green lines.

If the summary names a file you did not ask for, stop. That means the prompt was too loose or the AI wandered.

Keep the summary in a short release note. When the live site looks odd after deploy, you have a trail of what just changed.

You do not have to read every character. You do have to understand the meaning of the change before you hit accept.
