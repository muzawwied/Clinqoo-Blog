Ask the AI for an Example First, Then Let It Change a File

Request a small example and a short why before the AI touches your Clincoo project files.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The Clincoo AI assistant can propose a fast change. Hold on. Ask for a separate example: 'show a five-line mini version and explain why this approach is safer than what I have now.'

A mini example lets you see the idea without tearing apart a page that is almost tidy. If the example makes sense in your head, then ask the AI to adjust the file you name explicitly.

State limits: do not change brand colors, do not delete classes used on other pages, do not rewrite the whole file. Limits keep the advice local and easy to undo.

After the file changes, open desktop and phone preview. The AI speeds up the draft; your eyes decide whether the site still feels like yours.
