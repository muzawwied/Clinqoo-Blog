Ask the AI to Review Your Code Before You Deploy

Use the Clincoo AI assistant as a review partner: catch typos, broken links, and broken CSS before the site goes live.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Deploying is fun, but shipping a site with typos or a dead button feels awkward. Before you publish, open the AI chat in the Clincoo editor and ask it to review the files you just changed.

A useful request looks like this: "please review index.html and style.css, look for unclosed tags, unused classes, and bits that may break on a small screen." The more specific the files and symptoms, the sharper the advice.

The AI is not a substitute for preview. It helps flag things you miss after staring at your own code too long. After you get a list of suggestions, test them one by one in preview — keep what makes sense, ignore what does not match your design.

Make a five-minute AI review a habit before every deploy. The site stays yours; the AI is just a friend who reads it again before guests arrive.
