Break Large Clincoo AI Tasks into Small Steps

Asking the AI for a full site at once often gets messy. Split the work into steps you can test in preview.

Indonesia · English

A prompt like 'build a full landing page with animation and a form' forces the AI to guess too much. In Clincoo, split it: HTML structure, then color, then one interaction.

After one step is applied, open preview. Only then send the next step.

If the AI starts touching other files, stop and restate the boundary.

The large task still finishes through a queue you control.
