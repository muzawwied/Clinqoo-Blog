Use AI to Draft Page Copy, Then Edit It in Your Voice

AI can help draft headings and paragraphs. What makes the copy yours is the edit that follows.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many beginners get stuck on words, not code. In Clincoo AI chat, describe who your visitors are and what you want them to do, then ask for three title options plus one opening paragraph. Do not ask for "cool copy" with no context — the result will feel generic.

When a draft appears, read it out loud. If a sentence does not sound like something you would say, change it. Shorten it. Cut words that only add noise. The AI supplies material; you choose the voice.

Add constraints too: a maximum length, friendly language, no over-promising. Ask for button variants — "Learn more" versus "See the work" — then preview which one feels more honest.

Save the edited version in the HTML file, not in the chat. The conversation is the kitchen; the live page is the dish. That way the site copy stays alive and still sounds like you.
