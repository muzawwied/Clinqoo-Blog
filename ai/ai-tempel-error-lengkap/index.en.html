Paste the Full Error Message into the AI, Don't Just Say 'It's Broken'

Clinqoo AI is far more accurate when you paste the full error, the file name, and the steps you already tried.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

If the AI chat only gets 'the code is broken', the answer has to guess. In the Clinqoo editor, copy the full error from the terminal or console — including the file name and line number — and paste it into the conversation.

Add three short bits of context: which file you just changed, what you expected to happen, and what the preview actually shows. Those three sentences usually beat ten lines of speculation.

If the error is long, do not crop the stack in the middle. The bottom often points to the cause; the top points to the symptom. Let the AI read both, then ask it to explain the sequence in beginner language.

After you get a suggestion, test one change in the editor and save. If it still errors, send the new error — not the old one. A thread that follows the real trail is sharper than a chat that repeats guesses.
