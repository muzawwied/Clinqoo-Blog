Test AI Suggestions in Preview before Saving Them for Good

Do not treat a chat answer as finished work. Paste it, watch Clincoo preview, then save.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

AI suggestions sound confident. Clincoo editor preview decides. Paste a small change, open desktop and phone preview, then choose whether to save.

Watch for overflow, covered buttons, and text that suddenly sits too tight. If it breaks, Undo is cheaper than another deploy.

Keep one suggestion, one test. Mixing three AI proposals at once hides which one broke the layout.

AI speeds the draft. Preview protects visitors. You need both, in that order.
