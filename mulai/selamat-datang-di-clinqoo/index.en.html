Welcome to Clincoo: A Starter Guide from Zero

Clincoo is a workspace for building and publishing websites: a code editor, an AI assistant, ready-made templates, and deployment in one place. This guide is for anyone opening app.clincoo.buzz for the first time and wondering where to begin.

1. Create an account

Open app.clincoo.buzz and choose Continue with Google or Continue with GitHub. No long forms — your account is ready the moment you log in. Email sign-up with your own password is also available.

2. Explore the workspace

After signing in you land on the dashboard. Everything is reachable from here: your projects, AI chat, dashboard, code editor, and account settings. On wide screens the app uses a split view — chat on the left, workspace on the right.

3. Create your first project

Click New project and pick one: start from a template, upload a ZIP file, or import a GitHub repository (Pro plan and above). For a first try, a template is the fastest route — you get a working site structure you can edit right away.

4. Edit and ask the AI for help

Open the project and enter the editor. All your site files live there: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and images. If you are unsure what to change, ask the AI assistant in the chat — for example, "make the button black" or "create an about page". The AI writes code that you review before anything is applied.

5. Deploy to the internet

When your site is ready, use Build & Deployment to publish it. Clincoo gives every project an online address, and you can attach your own domain for a more professional look. Full details are in the article Deploy Your Site and Use Your Own Domain.

Good to know from day one

  • The free Starter plan is enough to learn: up to 3 active projects, 50 AI chat messages per month, and 5 web deploys per month.
  • The AI in Clincoo is an assistant, not an automatic site builder — you stay in control.
  • Every change can be checked in Live preview before publishing.

The rest you will learn as you go. Happy building — and if you get stuck, open the Help Center category on this blog.
