Getting Started

Welcome to Clincoo

Clincoo is a web workspace for building, editing, and publishing websites in one place: a professional code editor, an AI assistant, ready-made templates, a built-in terminal, and a deploy button. Nothing to install on your computer — just a browser.

This article is a short roadmap from zero to your first site live on the internet.

Core features at a glance

FeatureWhat it is for
Code editorEditing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files with a professional editor and live preview.
AI assistantExplaining concepts, writing and editing code, managing files, even helping with deploys.
TemplatesStarting a new site in seconds instead of from scratch.
TerminalRunning Linux commands right from the browser for technical work.
DeployPublishing your site to the internet with automatic HTTPS, in one click.
CollaborationInviting teammates to work on the same project.

Five steps to your first site

  1. Create an account at app.clincoo.buzz with Google, GitHub, or email.
  2. Click new project, then pick the template closest to what you need — a portfolio, a business landing page, or a simple store.
  3. Open files in the editor and change them. Hit save, and the live preview next to you updates instantly.
  4. Talk to the AI assistant whenever you are stuck: ask it to explain confusing code or write the component you need. See the Clinqoo AI Assistant guide.
  5. When everything looks good, press deploy. Within minutes your site is online at its own address — and you can attach your custom domain.

Quotas worth knowing as a new user: the free plan already includes 3 active projects, 50 AI messages per month, and 5 deploys per month. The full comparison lives in the Plans and Pricing article.

After your first site is live

From here you can grow in any direction: learn web concepts by practicing, invite teammates through the team collaboration feature, or simply polish your design until it feels right. If you hit a snag, start from the Help Center — nearly every common issue is already answered there.
