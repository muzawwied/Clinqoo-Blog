Compute Clincoo Events that Cross Midnight per Zone

An event from 22:00–01:00 is not one local date. Split it or store start and end instants.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Invite templates on editor.clincoo.buzz often store one date field. If the event passes 00:00, the list on app.clincoo.buzz clips the end hour.

Store startUtc and endUtc separately. When rendering, format each with the guest zone, not the author zone.

If local end is less than start, that signals a day crossing — add one day to the end date; do not stay silent.

Paste both instants plus the zone to AI. Do not ask AI to assume a fixed 24-hour span without checking DST.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz shows article dates; in the app, an event range needs two instants.
