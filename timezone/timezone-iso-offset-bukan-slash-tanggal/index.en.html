Store Clincoo Time as ISO with an Offset, Not 30/09/2026

A local slash date is ambiguous. ISO 8601 plus an offset parses safely in every zone.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A schedule field on editor.clincoo.buzz that stores 30/09/2026 09:00 with no zone will shift when opened in UTC.

Store an ISO string such as 2026-09-30T09:00:00+07:00 or a UTC instant 2026-09-30T02:00:00Z. Do not join date and time as free text.

Display it with Intl.DateTimeFormat and an IANA timeZone. Do not slice the ISO string by hand.

Paste the stored value plus the device zone to AI. Do not ask AI for a slash parser before you move to ISO.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz should share one canonical time format.
