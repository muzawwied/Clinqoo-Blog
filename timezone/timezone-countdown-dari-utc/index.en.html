Compute Clincoo Countdowns from UTC, Not Local Strings

Remaining seconds must subtract from a UTC instant. Local strings make the countdown jump when the zone changes.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A timer on app.clincoo.buzz jumped 7 hours after the user changed the OS zone mid-session.

Parse the target as a UTC instant. Subtract Date.now() or server time. Format the display separately.

Paste the ISO target and the wrong remaining seconds to AI. Do not ask for a per-zone interval.

Preview on editor.clincoo.buzz then share the short formula on blog.clincoo.buzz.
