Store UTC, Display the Local Zone on a Clincoo Page

A timestamp without a zone shifts Clincoo schedules when a client opens the page from another country. Store UTC, format on the device.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a local date string looks correct on your laptop and wrong on a client app.clincoo.buzz session.

Store ISO 8601 with Z or an offset. Render with Intl.DateTimeFormat and the user timeZone.

Do not rely on new Date(stringWithoutZone). Paste one bad UTC value to AI instead of asking for a calendar rewrite.

Test on blog.clincoo.buzz with Asia/Jakarta and another zone before release.
