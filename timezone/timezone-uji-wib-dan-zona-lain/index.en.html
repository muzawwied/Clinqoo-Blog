Test Clincoo Schedules in WIB and One Other Zone

Passing in Asia/Jakarta does not mean it passes in New York. Add one extra zone to the release checklist.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo teams often test only a WIB laptop. Bugs show up when a webinar opens from UTC-5.

Change the OS zone or use a DevTools override. Compare clock labels on editor.clincoo.buzz and app.clincoo.buzz.

Record both results and paste them to AI if the gap is not a steady 7 hours.

Link this checklist on blog.clincoo.buzz so the next release does not skip it.
