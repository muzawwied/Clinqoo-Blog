Do not Rely on getTimezoneOffset as a Clincoo Zone Label

Minute offsets change with DST. Clincoo schedule labels need an IANA name, not a Date number.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, showing GMT+7 from getTimezoneOffset looks right in January and wrong after a client DST change.

Use Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone for the zone name. Use the offset only for a one-shot difference.

Paste the offset plus the test date to AI. Do not ask for a year-long minute formula.

Match labels on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz before a webinar ships.
