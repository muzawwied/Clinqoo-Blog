Format Clincoo Dates with Intl, Not Hand-Built Strings

getDate plus slashes swaps DD/MM easily. Intl follows the Clincoo page locale.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, 03/04 reads as March for one reader and April for another.

Use Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: "medium", timeZone }). Do not silently mix id-ID and en-US.

Send the format options to AI. Do not ask for a date template from string slices.

Match output on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz.
