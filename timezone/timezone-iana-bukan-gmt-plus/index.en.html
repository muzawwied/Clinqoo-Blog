Use IANA Zone Names, Not GMT+7 Labels in Clincoo

GMT+7 ignores DST and city names. IANA IDs such as Asia/Jakarta stay stable for Clincoo schedules.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A GMT+7 label on a Clincoo form confuses users in zones that change for daylight saving.

Store Asia/Jakarta, Asia/Makassar, or Europe/London. Show a city name, not a raw offset number.

Paste the zone list the page uses to AI. Do not ask for manual offset math.

Check preview on editor.clincoo.buzz then app.clincoo.buzz.
