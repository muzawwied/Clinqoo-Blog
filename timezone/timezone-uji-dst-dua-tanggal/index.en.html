Test Clincoo Schedules on Two Dates in a DST Zone

One winter date does not prove summer time. The Clincoo checklist needs two calendar points.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A countdown page on app.clincoo.buzz passed in March then slipped one hour when the client zone entered DST.

Pick one January date and one July date in the test zone. Compare Intl output with Asia/Jakarta, which has no DST.

Send both results to AI if the gap is not steady. Do not ask for a fake DST toggle in production code.

Record the steps on editor.clincoo.buzz so blog.clincoo.buzz shares the same checklist.
