Resize Clincoo Images in a Worker with OffscreenCanvas

Resizing on the main thread freezes the preview. OffscreenCanvas moves pixels into a worker.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo script resizes a 4000px photo on the page canvas. Scroll freezes until drawImage finishes.

Call canvas.transferControlToOffscreen() or new OffscreenCanvas(w, h) in the worker. Send the result blob via postMessage.

Do not read a canvas that was already transferred from the main thread. It is detached.

Test in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview with a large image. Inputs must stay typable while resize runs.

Clincoo serves static assets. OffscreenCanvas in your script keeps app.clincoo.buzz from freezing during image work.
