Catch unhandledrejection inside a Clincoo Worker

A rejected Promise inside a worker does not show on the main tab. Attach a listener in the worker file.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Heavy work in editor.clincoo.buzz often uses Promises inside a worker. A reject without catch leaves the main console clean.

In the worker file, attach self.addEventListener("unhandledrejection", ...) then postMessage the reason to the UI. Do not rely on window.

Show a toast on app.clincoo.buzz: background task failed, not an endless spinner. Include the task name in the message.

Paste the stack from event.reason to AI. Do not ask AI to rewrite the whole worker before you read the reason.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz uses the same pattern: worker errors must surface in the UI, not only in the worker DevTools.
