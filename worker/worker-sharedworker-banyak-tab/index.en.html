Use a Clincoo SharedWorker so Many Tabs Share One Job

A dedicated worker per tab doubles the work. A SharedWorker is one process for every tab of the site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo visitor opens three preview tabs. Each tab creates a Dedicated Worker and CPU triples.

Use new SharedWorker(url). Every tab connects through a port. Compute once, send the result to the asking port.

Handle onconnect in the worker. Keep a list of ports. Do not assume a single tab.

Test with two app.clincoo.buzz tabs. Task Manager must show one shared worker, not two.

Clincoo serves a static site. A SharedWorker in your script keeps editor.clincoo.buzz lean with many tabs.
