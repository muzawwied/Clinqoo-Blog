Test Dark and Light Views with prefers-color-scheme on Clincoo

A page that only looks right in your system theme will fail when a visitor uses the opposite mode.

Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates drive background and text with CSS variables. If you only test in your laptop dark mode, light-mode contrast can fall under WCAG. MDN prefers-color-scheme is the signal you must flip before release.

In Chrome DevTools open Rendering > Emulate CSS prefers-color-scheme and pick light, then dark. Check the hero, forms, buttons, and placeholders. web.dev asks for 4.5:1 on body text and 3:1 on large text and UI components.

Do not rely on an invert filter. Brand colors that flip often break. Write two blocks for :root and data-theme or a dark prefers-color-scheme media query with explicit tokens.

Also test when a visitor locks a theme with an on-page toggle. The manual choice must win over the media query. Screenshot both modes before deploy.

Ask the AI only to add missing color tokens, not to replace the whole stylesheet. After the edit, emulate light and dark again until no text nearly vanishes into the background.

Sources: MDN prefers-color-scheme, web.dev prefers-color-scheme, WCAG contrast minimum.
