Test tel: and mailto: Links in Clincoo Preview before Release

Phone and email links often look correct while the href is still a sample. Tap them on a phone before you deploy.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Visible “0812-xxx” text does not mean the link works. Many templates render the number as plain text or bind href="#". Phone visitors expect a tap to open the dialer.

The pattern is simple: href="tel:+62812..." with no spaces, and href="mailto:name@domain". Include the country code. Local numbers without a plus often fail on devices with another locale.

Test three places: header, footer, and contact card. One page can hold three copies that drift after a quick edit.

On desktop preview, a tel: link may show an empty dialog. That is not proof of failure. Repeat on a real phone or DevTools device mode and confirm the number matches yours.

Log the result on the Clincoo release checklist next to internal links. A wrong contact burns trust faster than a slightly off color.
