Set Referrer-Policy in the Clincoo Site Meta

Outbound links can send the full URL, including a query with an invite code. A strict policy cuts that leak.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo pages often carry campaign queries or invite tokens. Without a referrer policy, the browser sends the full URL to the site a visitor clicks.

Add a meta referrer tag in head with content strict-origin-when-cross-origin. For sensitive links, also set rel noreferrer on the anchor.

Test in Network: click an external link from the blog.clincoo.buzz preview and inspect the Referer header. Internal paths should not travel.

Do not ask the AI to rewrite the whole head. Ask for one meta line, then check the canonical and og tags you already have.

This policy does not replace HTTPS. It only reduces what leaves with the visitor.
