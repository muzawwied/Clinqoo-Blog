Add rel=noopener to Clincoo Links that Use target=_blank

A new tab without noopener gives the destination access to window.opener. One attribute closes the tabnabbing gap.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo links to docs, marketplaces, or chat often use target="_blank". Without rel="noopener", the destination can write window.opener.location and send your original tab to a fake site.

Add rel="noopener noreferrer" on every external link that opens a new tab. noopener drops the window reference. noreferrer also hides Referer if you do not want the destination to see your page path.

Modern browsers add implicit noopener on target=_blank, but do not rely on that in old markup or WebViews. Write the attribute explicitly in the HTML you deploy.

Ask AI to find target="_blank" in the project and add rel only on those links. Do not let AI rewrite internal blog category links.

Test in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview: click an outbound link and confirm the original tab stays on the Clincoo page. This audit is fast and blocks tabnabbing before release.
