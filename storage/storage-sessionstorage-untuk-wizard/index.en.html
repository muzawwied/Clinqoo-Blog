Store Wizard Steps in sessionStorage, Not Clincoo localStorage

A half-finished step should not survive a closed tab. sessionStorage follows the tab lifetime.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Wizards on editor.clincoo.buzz often write the step to localStorage. The next day a new tab still opens step 3 from an old session.

Use sessionStorage for temporary drafts: step index and short text. Close the tab and the data is gone, which is what you want.

Data that must last, such as a saved draft, stays in named storage with a versioned key, for example clincoo.draft.v1.

Paste the storage keys to AI and ask to split temporary from permanent. Do not ask AI to store a token in either store.

The same limit is noted on blog.clincoo.buzz so the app.clincoo.buzz preview does not restore a stale wizard.
