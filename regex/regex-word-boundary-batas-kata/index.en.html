Use Word Boundaries so a Clincoo Regex Does Not Swallow Neighbor Words

Word boundaries keep a replace from damaging words that only look similar.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo replace changes "form" inside "format" and "information". The copy falls apart.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, wrap the token with \\b on both sides. A word boundary rejects letters that stick to it.
