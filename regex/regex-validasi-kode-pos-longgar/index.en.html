Validate Postal Codes Loosely, Not with a Perfect Pattern

Postal codes differ in length by country. Reject the impossible; do not force one format.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo form that demands five digits rejects valid codes that use spaces or letters. Visitors then blank the field or invent numbers.

Use a loose pattern: length 3–10, letters or digits, spaces and hyphens allowed. Country-strict checks only after a country field is chosen.

Do not parse a full address with one regex. Split postal code from city. Test valid and invalid strings in the console before pasting the pattern into oninput.

Error text should describe the accepted format, not just invalid. Include an example that matches the page locale.

Clincoo ships form scripts as written. A loose pattern plus a human check is safer than a regex that blocks checkout.
