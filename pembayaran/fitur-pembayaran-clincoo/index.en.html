Clincoo Payments: Accept QRIS, Track Balance, Withdraw Funds

Clincoo is more than a place to build and deploy sites. With Clincoo Payments (ClincooPay), the site you deploy can accept money from buyers through QRIS: the pay QR appears automatically, status is tracked in real time, and funds land in your project balance on their own. From that balance you can withdraw to an e-wallet with email OTP verification. Everything is controlled from one page: Project Settings → Payments on app.clincoo.buzz.

How ClincooPay works

The flow is simple and needs no gateway setup:

  1. Enable the Payments feature in project settings — payment credentials are issued automatically.
  2. Your deployed site calls the transaction-creation API with the public key.
  3. The buyer opens the checkout link, scans the QR with any payment app, and confirms.
  4. The system detects the payment and adds the project balance automatically.
  5. You withdraw the balance to an e-wallet any time, with email OTP as the guard.

The payments API at a glance

Every transaction is controlled through the /api/pay endpoint. Full parameters and cURL samples live in the QRIS Payment Gateway API documentation.

The buyer checkout page

Every transaction gets an official checkout link with a clean layout: total bill, QR code, a QR download button, and a copyable Order ID. QRIS instructions are shown on the page, and the status refreshes automatically without reloading: a success confirmation appears when paid, an expiry notice when time runs out. A QR has a time limit; unpaid transactions are marked expired automatically.

StatusMeaningWhat to do
pendingAwaiting payment (or a check in progress).Watch the checkout page or poll the status API.
paidPayment received; project balance increases.Deliver the goods or service, and (optionally) enable the webhook for automation.
expiredQR expired or the transaction was cancelled.Create a new transaction with the same amount.

Balance and real-time reports

All incoming money is recorded in the project balance. The Payments panel shows total incoming, total withdrawn, and total transactions, plus charts: funds in per day (last 7 days), funds out per day (last 7 days), transaction trend (14 days), transaction status breakdown, amount per status, and withdrawals per e-wallet. Recent transactions and withdrawals are always filterable from the same page.

Advantages and benefits

  • No gateway chores: no merchant onboarding, no paperwork, no provider keys of your own to manage.
  • No payment page to build: the official Clincoo checkout covers the QR, instructions, live status, and success/expiry states.
  • Money lands automatically: no manual bookkeeping — balance, history, and charts fill themselves.
  • Automation-ready: the payment.paid webhook plugs straight into your deployed site logic (account activation, license delivery, and more).
  • Full QRIS reach: one QR accepts every popular e-wallet and bank in Indonesia.
  • Safe withdrawals: email OTP verification and an automatic refund when a withdrawal is rejected.
  • Transparent history: every transaction and withdrawal has a tracker you can look up any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Payments feature available on every plan?

No. It activates from the Pro plan. Users on other plans see an upgrade notice before they can enable it.

Do buyers need a Clincoo account?

No. Buyers simply open the checkout link and scan the QR with any app that supports QRIS.

How long until funds reach the project balance?

Funds are recorded the moment the payment status becomes paid, usually within seconds after the buyer completes the payment.

Can the payment key be placed in site code?

Yes. The payment key is meant for the public side, to create and check transactions — just keep your Clincoo account secure as usual.

What happens if a withdrawal is rejected?

The balance of the amount plus the admin fee is returned to the project balance automatically, and the owner receives an email with the reason.
