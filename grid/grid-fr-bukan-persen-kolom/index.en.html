Use fr, Not Percent, for Clincoo Grid Columns That Share Leftover Space

Percentages in grid-template-columns ignore gap. The fr unit shares leftover space after gap in a Clincoo layout.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates use two or three card columns. Writing 50% 50% or 33% 33% 33% looks tidy, but percentages are taken from the container width before gap. The columns then overflow or force horizontal scroll when you raise the gap.

The fr unit divides space that remains after fixed sizes and gap are taken. 1fr 1fr gives two equal columns. 2fr 1fr makes a narrower sidebar without a calculator. MDN describes fr as a flexible share of free space, not a percentage of total width.

Mix fr with minmax when cards need a minimum width. Example: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr)). Percentages still help inside a cell, not on grid tracks that must respect gap.

Test in preview by widening the window and increasing gap. If cards still fit without overflow, the tracks already use fr. Replace percentages in grid-template-columns before adding another media query.
