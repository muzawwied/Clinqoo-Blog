Split justify-items and justify-content on Clincoo Grids

justify-content moves the tracks, not the cell contents. Cards that stay stuck left usually need justify-items.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the editor.clincoo.buzz preview, a three-column grid is even, but icons inside cards stay left. Changing justify-content does not move the icons.

justify-content spaces the tracks. justify-items aligns content inside each cell. For icons, use justify-items: center on the grid, or justify-self on the item.

Turn on the DevTools grid overlay. If tracks are already full, content has no free space to shift.

Paste the grid CSS to AI. Ask to replace the wrong property, not to rewrite template-areas.

Test widths 360 and 1200 on app.clincoo.buzz. Clincoo stores the CSS you write.
