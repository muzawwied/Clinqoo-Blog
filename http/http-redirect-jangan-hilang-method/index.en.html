Do Not Let a 302 Redirect Turn a Clincoo POST into GET

A 302 on POST is often followed by the browser as GET with no body. The saved payload disappears.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

If the app.clincoo.buzz API replies 302 to a login URL or an extra slash, fetch follows the redirect and the method may become GET.

Use redirect: 'manual' while debugging. Read Location. Fix the trailing slash or HTTPS on the origin, not in the client.

For a permanent move, 308 keeps the method. 303 is an intentional GET after a successful POST.

Paste the redirect chain from Network to AI. Do not ask AI to add a random proxy before you inspect the 302.

editor.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz must use canonical URLs so POST does not detour.
