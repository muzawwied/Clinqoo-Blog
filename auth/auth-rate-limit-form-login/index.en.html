Rate-Limit Clincoo Login Attempts per Address

An unbounded login form invites password guessing. Rate limits and progressive delays stop brute force.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

A sign-in page on app.clincoo.buzz that accepts unlimited POSTs lets a script try thousands of passwords per minute. Client-only throttles are easy to skip.

Record failed logins per IP and per email on the server. After five failures in ten minutes, return 429 plus Retry-After. Keep the UI copy generic: too many attempts, try again later.

Do not announce remaining attempts if that helps an attacker pace the guesses. Store the counter in server storage, not in a cookie the user can delete.

Rate-limit the password-reset path as well. A CAPTCHA may appear after the threshold, but it must not be the only control.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, fail five submits and confirm Kirim stays disabled until Retry-After elapses. Ask AI to tidy the 429 copy only, not the counting logic.
