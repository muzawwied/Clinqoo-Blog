Replace Clincoo Template Contact Placeholders before Release

Sample WhatsApp numbers, hello@studio emails, and dummy map links must not ship on a public subdomain.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates almost always ship a contact footer. Visitors tap that button before they finish the hero. If it is still sample data, you send people to someone else’s number.

Search the whole project for wa.me, mailto:, maps, instagram.com, and “lorem” near addresses. Change the href and the visible text together.

Keep the footer identical on every page. Updating only the home page leaves pricing or blog pages with fake contact details.

Click through phone preview. mailto: should open your mailbox, map links should land on the place you mean. Do not trust your eyes; links hide inside icons.

After the swap, keep an official contact list in one project note so the next release does not copy placeholders again.
