Keep One Footer Across Every Clincoo Template Page

A different footer in each file drops legal and contact links. Copy one correct footer into every page.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates often ship a full footer on the home page, while about or pricing still uses a short footer from an older version.

Pick one footer that already has contact, policy, and copyright links. Copy that block into the other HTML files without renaming its CSS classes.

After you copy, check relative paths. A ../policy.html link from a subfolder is not the same as policy.html at the root. Test every link in preview.

Do not let AI invent a new footer per page. Ask it to paste the same block so classes and link order stay identical.

When the year or WhatsApp number changes, edit one source and spread it again. Drifted footers are maintenance debt that grows quietly.

Clincoo serves files as they are. A consistent footer keeps a path to contact and legal pages on every screen.
