Use role=status for Clincoo Toasts, Not role=alert

role=alert interrupts the screen reader for a saved-successfully message. A normal toast only needs role=status.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After a save on editor.clincoo.buzz, a "saved" toast with role=alert cuts off the form reading. The user loses their place.

Success and info messages use role="status" and aria-live="polite". Keep role="alert" for failures that must be heard immediately.

Do not call alert() or confirm() for a save result. That blocks the thread and cannot be styled.

One live region at the end of the page is enough. Do not create a new toast every second; replace the same text.

When asking AI for help, name the message type. AI often pastes role=alert onto every toast on app.clincoo.buzz.
