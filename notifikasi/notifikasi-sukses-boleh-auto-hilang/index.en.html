Clincoo Success Toasts May Dismiss Themselves, Errors Must Not

A short success toast may fade. Error messages must stay until the user closes them.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates use one timer for every toast. A failed form submit then vanishes in three seconds, just like “Saved”.

Split the two kinds of feedback in editor.clincoo.buzz. Success uses aria-live polite and may disappear after it can be read. Errors need an explicit close control.

Do not rely on a fixed duration. Screen-reader users and people who enlarge text need more time. If you keep a timer, pause it while the pointer or focus is on the message.

Ask AI only to add a success-versus-error branch. Paste the current submit handler. Do not let the model rewrite the whole form.

Test in preview: submit a valid form and confirm the toast does not cover the CTA. Submit an empty form and confirm the error stays until closed.
