Do Not Turn Clincoo Notifications into Full-Screen Overlays

A banner that covers the whole viewport forces extra clicks. Use a small strip at the edge of the page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates sometimes reuse a confirmation modal for every toast. The dark backdrop covers the form so users cannot fix fields.

A status notification is not a dialog. Do not set role dialog, do not lock scroll, and do not trap focus unless the user must choose.

Place the message in a corner or under the form. Leave the rest of the page clickable. Reserve a full overlay for choices that destroy data.

Ask AI to replace the overlay with a persistent status region. Paste the position:fixed CSS that currently covers 100vh.

Test with the keyboard: after the toast appears, Tab should move to the next field, not get trapped on the backdrop.
