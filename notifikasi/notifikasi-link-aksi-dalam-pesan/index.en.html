Include an Action Link in Clincoo Notifications When a Next Step Exists

A ‘sent’ message with no link forces users to hunt for the next page. Add one clear action.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After a Clincoo form succeeds, the toast only says “Success”. Visitors do not know whether to wait for email or open another page.

If there is a next step, add one link in the message: “View the thank-you page” or “Back to home”. Do not put five links in a toast.

Make the link reachable by keyboard and do not dismiss the toast before Tab can reach it. If a success toast auto-hides, keep a separate confirmation page as fallback.

Ask AI to add the link inside the status region, not to change site navigation. Paste the current toast markup.

Test on a phone: the link target should be easy to tap and must not overlap the close button.
