Queue Clincoo Notifications Instead of Stacking Four Toasts

Four stacked toasts cover the CTA and are announced in a jumble. Show one message at a time.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo scripts that call showToast on every validation step produce a pile of messages. Screen readers announce all of them at once.

Keep one status region. Replace its text; do not clone a new element. If two events arrive close together, queue them and show the more important one first: errors beat success.

Do not spam the live region while typing. Field validation belongs next to the input. Toasts are for action results, not every keyup.

Ask AI to merge three toast calls into one queue function. Paste the current JS file.

Test by tapping submit twice quickly. Only one message should be visible. The old message must be replaced, not stacked below.
