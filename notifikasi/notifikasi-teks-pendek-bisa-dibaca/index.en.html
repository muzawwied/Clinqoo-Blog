Write Clincoo Notifications as One Readable Sentence

A toast that is only an icon or the word OK does not explain what happened. Write one concrete sentence.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo pages show a green check with no text. Color-blind users and screen readers cannot tell whether the message was saved or failed.

Replace it with a sentence such as “Contact message sent” or “Email is invalid, check the address field”. One sentence is enough. Avoid a paragraph inside a toast.

Put the same words inside the status region, not only in a title attribute. Screen readers speak live-region content, not tooltips.

Ask AI to tidy notification strings only. Paste the three messages that currently say OK, Success, and Error.

Check text contrast against the toast background. Light green on white often fails WCAG for small text.
