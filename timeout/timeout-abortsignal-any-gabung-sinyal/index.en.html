Combine Clincoo Timeout and User Cancel with AbortSignal.any

Two separate controllers are easy to forget to abort. One combined signal cancels fetch from time or from a cancel button.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo save fetch has its own timer and its own cancel button. One path fires, the other listener stays alive.

Use AbortSignal.any when the browser supports it, or wrap two signals in editor.clincoo.buzz. Abort once for every reason.

Show different copy: timed out versus user canceled. Do not treat both as a network error.

Ask AI to write a fetch wrapper with one signal. Paste the AbortController snippet, not the whole app.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz stops waiting when the user cancels or the time limit hits.
