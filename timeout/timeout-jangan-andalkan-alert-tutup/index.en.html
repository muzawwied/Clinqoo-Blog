Do Not Rely on alert() as a Clincoo Timeout

alert freezes the thread and delays other timers. Use a non-blocking UI for a time-limit warning.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Some templates on editor.clincoo.buzz call alert when a fetch times out. Other timers pause until the dialog closes.

Replace alert with a banner or toast. Keep the event loop running so cancel and retry still work.

Ask AI for one timeout-warning component. Do not ask for an alert polyfill.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz with two parallel requests. Record the behavior on blog.clincoo.buzz.
