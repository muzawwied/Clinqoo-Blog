Lazy-load Embed Iframes with Intersection Observer in Clincoo

Several YouTube or map iframes in the hero slow First Contentful Paint. Load an iframe only when it nears the viewport.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, three embed iframes below the fold still download when the page opens.

Store the real src on data-src. Attach one IntersectionObserver. When the ratio is visible, move data-src to src and unobserve.

Give a fixed-height placeholder so the layout does not jump. Paste the short observer to AI, not the whole homepage.

Measure Network on app.clincoo.buzz before and after. Record the result on blog.clincoo.buzz.
