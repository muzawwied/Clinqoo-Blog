Show a Fallback when a Clincoo Embed Widget Fails to Load

A silently failing embed leaves a blank hole. Catch iframe onerror and swap in a link or message.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a 404 calendar iframe still shows a white frame. Visitors think the feature is broken.

Attach onerror on the iframe or inspect contentWindow after load. Replace it with an Open in a new tab link plus a short note.

Do not leave the alternative empty. Paste the iframe markup to AI and ask only for the fallback handler.

Test a bad embed URL on app.clincoo.buzz. Save the fallback pattern on blog.clincoo.buzz.
