Allow Embed Hosts in CSP before Adding a Clincoo Widget

Third-party iframes go blank when Content-Security-Policy blocks frame-src. Add the hosts you use; do not turn CSP off.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a map or calendar that worked on localhost becomes an empty box after CSP is enabled.

Open DevTools Network and Console. Find the blocked frame-src or child-src message. Add only the hosts you actually use to frame-src.

Do not use frame-src * in production. Paste the CSP header and embed URL to AI instead of asking AI to disable security.

Retest on app.clincoo.buzz and list allowed hosts on blog.clincoo.buzz.
