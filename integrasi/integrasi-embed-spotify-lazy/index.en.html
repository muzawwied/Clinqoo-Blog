Embed Spotify in Clincoo with Lazy-load and a Locked Height

Spotify player iframes often stretch cards and delay First Paint. Lock the height, lazy-load, and provide an open-in-Spotify link.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Spotify embed is handy for an episode or playlist on a Clincoo page, but the iframe has a fixed height that rarely matches your grid cards. Pasting the raw embed often stretches neighboring cards or pushes the footer far down.

First lock the player box with aspect-ratio or a minimum height you choose, then let the iframe fill that box (width 100%, height 100%, border 0). Do not let Spotify default height attribute drive the whole section.

Load the iframe lazily. Use loading="lazy" on the iframe, or set src only after an Intersection Observer sees the card enter the viewport. That follows the iframe lazy-load pattern on web.dev and cuts network work when a visitor first opens the home page.

Provide a fallback: an Open in Spotify text link under the player. Some office networks block embeds, and readers on a data-saver device prefer the official app over downloading a widget.

Test in Clincoo preview at a narrow card width and on desktop. If the player clips controls or causes overflow-x, lower the height instead of adding overflow:hidden on body.

Keep one embed per page when you can. Several live Spotify iframes stall the main thread. For a list of episodes, show link cards instead of five live players.
