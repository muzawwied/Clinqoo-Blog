Add UTM Parameters to Outbound Clincoo Widget Links

WhatsApp or calendar clicks are hard to attribute when outbound URLs are bare. Insert a consistent utm_source.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, the chat button opens wa.me with no source. Analytics then mixes organic traffic and the widget.

When you build the href, add utm_source=clincoo&utm_medium=widget&utm_campaign=page-name. Do not overwrite existing query keys.

Do not put personal data in UTM. Paste one sample URL to AI to check encoding.

Test a click on app.clincoo.buzz and document the campaign naming on blog.clincoo.buzz.
