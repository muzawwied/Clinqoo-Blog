Keep the Clincoo Kill Switch Separate from the Rollout Flag

A percentage flag is not enough during an incident. Provide an emergency off switch that ignores buckets.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A 5 percent rollout on editor.clincoo.buzz still hurts some users if the bug is severe. You need an instant off switch.

The kill switch must fail safe, require no redeploy, and document who may flip it.

Do not merge the kill switch with a UI experiment. One switch, one decision: on or off.

Ask AI to review the check order: kill switch first, then percentage. Do not ask it to merge both maps.

Drill flipping the switch on app.clincoo.buzz during an incident exercise, then record the steps on blog.clincoo.buzz.
