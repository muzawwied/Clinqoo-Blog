Log Clincoo Feature-Flag Evaluations without Leaking Secrets

You need an on/off trail while debugging, not a dump of the whole flag map. Log the name and result only.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

console.log(flags) on editor.clincoo.buzz ends up in screenshots sent to AI.

Log one line: flag name, value used, and a truncated project id.

Disable evaluation logs in production on app.clincoo.buzz or gate them behind debug mode.

When you ask AI for help, paste one log line, not the full env file.

Keep a safe log example on blog.clincoo.buzz so the team format stays consistent.
