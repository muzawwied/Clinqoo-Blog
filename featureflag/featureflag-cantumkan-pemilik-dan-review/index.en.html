Assign an Owner and a Review Date to Every Clincoo Feature Flag

A flag without an owner is never deleted. Write the owner name and the next review date.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, orphan flags pile up because nobody feels allowed to delete them.

Add two columns to the flag list: owner and review date. Without both, the flag must not ship to production on app.clincoo.buzz.

Review means decide: keep, raise the rollout, or delete the dead branch. It is not a checkbox on a doc.

Paste the flag list to AI and ask which owners are missing. Do not ask AI to invent names.

Announce the new owner on blog.clincoo.buzz so teammates know whom to ping when the flag breaks.
