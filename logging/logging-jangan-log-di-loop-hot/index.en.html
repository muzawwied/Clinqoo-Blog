Do Not console.log inside Clincoo Hot Loops

Logs inside requestAnimationFrame or scroll listeners stall the Console and hide the real error.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, logs in a render or scroll loop flood the Console until the important error is pushed away.

Move logs to a rare branch: after a user action, or behind a debug flag that is off in production on app.clincoo.buzz.

If you must watch a loop, log every N frames or only when a value changes. Do not log a whole DOM node.

Paste two log lines plus the stack to AI. Do not ask AI to disable the entire Console.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz uses sparse logs; your pages should be just as sparse.
