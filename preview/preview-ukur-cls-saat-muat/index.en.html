Measure Clincoo Preview CLS before Images and Fonts Finish

A layout that jumps when assets arrive ruins a release. Check shift in Performance, not only the final screenshot.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, the hero card jumps after a 1200px photo arrives. Visitors see the button move.

Open Performance or Experience in DevTools. Note CLS. Give img width/height or aspect-ratio and font-display: optional.

Send the Performance filmstrip to AI instead of asking for a full grid rewrite.

Repeat on app.clincoo.buzz. Write the CLS threshold on blog.clincoo.buzz.
