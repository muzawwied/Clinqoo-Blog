Check Print Preview of a Clincoo Page before You Ship

Visitors still print pricing and terms. Open print preview so a dark background and extra links do not ruin the page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The preview in editor.clincoo.buzz shows a screen. Pricing pages, simple invoices, and terms still get printed. A dark background, fixed buttons, and navigation waste ink and clip the text.

Open the print dialog in the preview browser. Hide nav and footer with @media print. Force dark text on paper. A wide hero image often does not belong on paper; display: none in print is enough.

Icon-only links vanish on paper. Keep the link text. If you print full URLs with CSS, limit that to legal pages so lines do not wrap on every word.

Watch page breaks too. A pricing table split across a row is hard to read. page-break-inside: avoid on a card or an important row keeps the block whole.

One minute in print preview catches defects that stay hidden in a dark screen theme. Do this on pages a client might save as PDF before you hit publish on the subdomain.
