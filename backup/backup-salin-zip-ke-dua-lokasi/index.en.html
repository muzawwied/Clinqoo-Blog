Copy a Clincoo Backup ZIP to Two Locations

A single Downloads folder is easy to wipe. Copy the archive to a second disk before a large experiment.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Developers keep one ZIP in Downloads. Cleaning the disk deletes the backup with no warning.

After downloading from app.clincoo.buzz, copy the ZIP into the project folder and onto a second disk or private cloud.

Do not rely on AI chat or the browser cache. Binary files do not live in the conversation.

Test that both copies open. If one location is corrupt, the other still exists.

Clincoo does not replicate the ZIP to your devices. Two locations are the net you control.
