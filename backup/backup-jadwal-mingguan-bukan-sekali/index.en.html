Schedule a Weekly Backup of Your Clincoo Project

A single ZIP from kickoff goes stale fast. Download a dated backup every week before large edits.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo site owners download a ZIP only at first publish. Three weeks later client copy, new photos, and AI fixes are missing from that archive.

Set a rhythm: every Monday, or before a large AI generate, export the project and name it with an ISO date. Keep copies on local disk and a separate cloud folder.

Once a month open the ZIP in a temp folder and confirm index.html plus the hero image still work. A file you cannot open is not a backup.

Do not overwrite weekly-old.zip. Keep the last three versions so a broken page can be restored.

Clincoo stores the online workspace, but the export you hold is the safety net. A small schedule beats panic when an account or browser fails.
