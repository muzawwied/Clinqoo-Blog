Export a Clincoo ZIP after a Stable Release, Not Only before Changes

A backup taken only before an experiment is not enough. Also save the version that passed preview.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Teams only download a ZIP before an AI generate. After a good release, there is no copy of what went live.

After preview in editor.clincoo.buzz passes link and form checks, download another ZIP from app.clincoo.buzz.

Mark the filename as a release, for example proyek-2026-09-22-rilis.zip.

Do not mix experiment ZIPs with release ZIPs in the same folder without labels.

Clincoo serves whatever is in the editor at that moment. A release ZIP is a tested restore point.
