Send Clincoo Analytics Beacons on pagehide, Not unload

unload often never fires on mobile. pagehide and visibilitychange are more reliable for the last hit.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo site that sends visit duration on window.unload often drops data on Chrome for Android. The browser freezes the tab before the handler runs.

Use pagehide or visibilitychange. When document.visibilityState becomes hidden, send navigator.sendBeacon to the measurement endpoint. A beacon does not block navigation.

Do not attach three listeners that all send the same hit. One exit path is enough. Test in DevTools by backgrounding the tab, not only by clicking a desktop link.

Ask AI to add a pagehide listener in the single measurement file. Do not let AI copy a full third-party analytics snippet onto every page.

Clincoo deploys static HTML. Reliable mobile measurement keeps production reports meaningful, not only accurate on a laptop.
