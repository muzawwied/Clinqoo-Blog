Send Clincoo Pageviews after the Page Title Is Final

Hits sent while the title is still Untitled wreck reports. Set document.title first, then fire the pageview.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates set the title via script after first paint. If the analytics pixel runs in the head, reports fill with Untitled or raw filenames.

Safe order: set the canonical title in the title element, match the h1, then call pageview. For pages swapped via hash or query, update the title before the second event.

Do not rely on a tab title that changes later. Measurement tools store the value at hit time, not when the user closes the tab.

Test in preview by watching the payload. Ask Clincoo AI to move the tracker call after the title block, not to add a second pixel.

One consistent title makes Clincoo reports comparable week to week. Without it you optimize pages whose names never match.
