Measure Time on a Clincoo Page, Not Only Pageview Hits

Pageviews without duration treat a 2-second bounce like a 3-minute read. Record engagement time on important CTAs.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A pageview only answers “this page opened”. It does not tell you whether a visitor read anything. On Clincoo guide sites, duration and scroll depth are more useful.

Pick one or two events: active time on the home page, or scroll to the pricing block. Do not attach timers to every paragraph. Too many events clutter reports and slow the page.

Respect consent. Do not send measurement before the visitor agrees if you use cookies or a device id. Official measurement docs put consent before the tracker runs.

Test in the Clincoo preview with tracking off first, then enable one script. Make sure document.title is final before the first event so reports are not full of “Untitled”.

Read weekly reports, not daily noise. A quiet day does not mean the CTA is broken. Fix pages with many hits but near-zero active time.
