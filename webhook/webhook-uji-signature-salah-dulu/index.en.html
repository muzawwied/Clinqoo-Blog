Test an Invalid Signature before Accepting a Clincoo Webhook

An endpoint that accepts POST without HMAC will swallow fake payloads. Reject empty and wrong signatures first.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, webhooks are often tested only with a success payload. Attackers send a body with no signature header.

Send three requests: no header, a random signature, then a valid signature. The first two must be 401. Do not continue into business logic.

Compare HMAC over the raw body, not a re-parsed object. A changed JSON key order makes a valid signature fail.

Ask AI to review the reject branch, not to rewrite the handler. Paste the verify snippet and the expected status code.

Record the test on blog.clincoo.buzz. The app.clincoo.buzz preview must not accept a fake callback.
