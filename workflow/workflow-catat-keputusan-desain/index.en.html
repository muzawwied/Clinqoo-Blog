Record Design Decisions in HTML Comments before the Next Clincoo Edit

Color, breakpoint, and copy choices vanish when AI or a teammate edits a file unless you write them down.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo projects move fast: template, AI chat, then deploy. A choice like “the hero uses a dark photo because the logo is white” disappears in a day if it lives only in your head.

Put a short HTML comment next to the block. One or two sentences are enough: the reason, the date, and what must not change. Skip essays inside markup.

When you ask AI to edit, paste that comment into the prompt. Models overwrite written constraints less often. After the edit, check the comment still matches; update it if the decision changed.

If comments pile up, keep cross-page notes in NOTES.html or the project README. What matters is a trail that exports with the project.

This workflow cuts regressions. Rollback is easier when you know why padding is 32px instead of guessing from a diff.
