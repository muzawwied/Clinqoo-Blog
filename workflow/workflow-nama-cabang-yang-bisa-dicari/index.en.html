Name Clincoo Branches So You Can Find Them Later

Branches named fix, test, or update disappear after a week. Use type, area, and a short intent.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo teams often create a branch named fix2 on editor.clincoo.buzz. A month later nobody remembers what it holds.

Use type/area-intent, for example fix/form-empty-label or docs/webhook-receive-time. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces.

Do not use a ticket number alone. The name should stay clear in the branch list without opening the description.

Ask AI for three names, then pick one. Do not let AI create a branch outside the pattern.

Record the pattern on blog.clincoo.buzz so the app.clincoo.buzz preview stops stacking anonymous branches.
