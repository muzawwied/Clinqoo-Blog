Do Not Hide Key Facts in a Clincoo Accordion

Price, hours, and terms should be visible without an extra click. Use accordions for detail, not the core promise.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

AI often tucks price into a closed FAQ so the hero looks clean. Busy visitors never open the panel.

Keep the key number and terms on the page surface. Accordions are for follow-up questions only.

Ask the AI to move one price block into a visible section, not to rewrite the whole layout.

Check preview: without clicking anything, you should find the price or how to get in touch.

Clincoo ships closed HTML as saved. Hidden key facts are UX debt.
