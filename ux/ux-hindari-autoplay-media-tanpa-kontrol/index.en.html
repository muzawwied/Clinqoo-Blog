Do Not Autoplay Video or Audio without Controls on Clincoo Sites

Media that starts on its own is hard to stop on a phone. Give controls and skip automatic sound.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates sometimes ship a hero video with autoplay and sound on. Phone visitors hear audio they never asked for.

Remove unmuted autoplay. If a looping background is needed, use muted playsinline and provide a visible stop control.

video and audio elements need controls unless you build equivalent custom buttons. Without controls, people cannot pause.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion: do not force a moving video when the user asked for less motion. A still hero image is enough.

Test in the mobile preview on editor.clincoo.buzz. Play, pause, and mute. If the hit target is tiny, enlarge it.

Good UX on Clincoo does not grab attention with sound. Comfortable visitors are more likely to read the main CTA.
