Place Form Errors Next to the Field on Clincoo

A banner at the top of the page is easy to miss. Point at the bad field and say how to fix it.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo scripts use alert() or one paragraph above the form. On a phone that message is already off-screen.

Write the error under the failing input. Mark aria-invalid and do not rely on red color alone.

Ask the AI to add one validation function in the form file, not to replace the whole page.

Test in preview: submit empty, and make sure the eye lands on the field, not a distant banner.

Clincoo runs the script you saved. Error placement is UX, not decoration.
