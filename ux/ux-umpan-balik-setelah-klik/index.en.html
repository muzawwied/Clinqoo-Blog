Give Feedback after a Click in a Clincoo Project

A silent button after a click feels broken. Show loading, success, or error.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Static Clincoo forms often only link to another page. If a script sends data, the button must change as soon as it is clicked.

Disable the button and show Sending… or land on a thank-you page. Do not let visitors double-submit.

Ask the AI to add feedback in one handler, not to rewrite the whole form.

Test in preview: click, watch the change, then check the destination on the app.clincoo.buzz subdomain.

Polite UX on Clincoo is a signal that the action was received before the next page appears.
