Do Not Silently Open New Tabs from Clincoo Links

target=_blank with no warning confuses people. Use a new tab only for downloads or third-party flows that must stay open.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates add target="_blank" to every menu link. Visitors who want Home back find a pile of tabs. The pattern feels safe; it actually breaks the Back button.

In the editor, keep target="_blank" only for outbound links that should leave your site open — a PDF download or a third-party checkout. Internal links need a normal href.

If you still open a new tab, add rel="noopener noreferrer" and say so in the link text, such as opens in a new tab. An arrow icon alone is not enough for a screen reader.

Test with the keyboard: Tab to the link, Enter, then switch back. If the user loses scroll position, stay in the same tab. Ask AI to strip blanket target=_blank, not to add window.open scripts.

Clincoo follows your link markup as written. Polite UX lets visitors own their browser history.
