Cut Contact Form Fields on a Clincoo Site

Long forms make people leave. Ask for a name, a contact, and one message — the rest can wait.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates sometimes copy a CRM: company, title, budget, and five selects. That is a sales form, not first contact.

Three fields are enough: name, email or WhatsApp, and a message. Validate in HTML, not in a five-step wizard.

If the AI adds nested selects, limit the job to one form file and delete unused fields.

Fill the form in a phone preview on editor.clincoo.buzz. If you scroll two screens just to send, cut fields.

Clincoo does not shrink forms for you. Field count is a UX decision.
