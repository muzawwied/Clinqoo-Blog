One Primary Action per Screen on a Clincoo Site

Visitors stall when every block asks for a different click. Pick one primary button per viewport.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates often put Sign Up, Learn More, and Buy in the same hero. Three equal calls mean nobody chooses.

Pick one primary action: submit a form, open services, or contact. Other buttons may stay, but smaller and quieter.

If the AI adds a CTA in every section, limit the job to one file and ask it to demote secondary buttons.

Check preview on editor.clincoo.buzz: from the top of the screen, the eye should land on one button.

Clincoo ships markup as saved. Action hierarchy is a UX decision before deploy.
