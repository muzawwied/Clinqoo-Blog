Stack Visual Hierarchy Top to Bottom on Clincoo

Eyes read from headline to action. Do not put small details above the page promise.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates put a badge, three prices, and testimonials before the benefit sentence. Visitors still do not know the product.

Order it: one-line headline, one benefit sentence, one action. Logo and menu may sit above, but they must not compete with the title.

If the AI lifts pricing cards into the hero, ask it to restore hierarchy in one HTML file.

Preview in editor.clincoo.buzz at a narrow width. The first paragraph should answer who the site is for.

Clincoo does not tidy block order. Visual hierarchy is UX work before deploy.
