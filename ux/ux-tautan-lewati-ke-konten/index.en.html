Add a Skip Link to Main Content on Clincoo Sites

Keyboard users must tab through the whole nav on every page. One skip link at the start of body saves dozens of Tabs.

Indonesia · English

Clincoo sites often have a long nav. MDN and WCAG technique G1 recommend a Skip to content link as the first focusable element.

Practical pattern: a link at the top that becomes visible on :focus, pointing to id=main on the main element. Do not use display:none; park it off-screen and show it on focus.

Test Tab right after load. First focus should be the skip link, then the logo or menu. web.dev treats skip links as a foundational access pattern.

Give main an id and tabindex=-1 so the target can take focus after the jump in some browsers.

Ask the AI to add the link and the landmark id, not to rebuild the whole header.

Sources: MDN main, W3C WCAG Technique G1 Skip links, web.dev Focus and skip links.
