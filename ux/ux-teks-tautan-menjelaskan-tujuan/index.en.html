Write Link Text that Names the Destination on Clincoo

Click here gives no context. Name the destination with words people can scan.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

AI copy often uses Click here or Learn more on repeat. A screen reader then hears a list of identical links.

Replace them with the page or action: View pricing, Send a message, Open the Clincoo editor.

Ask the AI only to change link text in the file you name. Refuse a full hero rewrite.

Scan the page in preview: each link should make sense without the surrounding sentence.

Honest link text helps UX, access, and SEO. Clincoo does not write labels for you.
