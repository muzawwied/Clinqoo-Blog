Do Not Let a Clincoo Sticky Header Cover Keyboard Focus

A sticky header that hides the Tab target breaks keyboard navigation. Reserve scroll space with scroll-margin.

Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates use a sticky header. When a user presses Tab, the next link is focused but hidden under the nav bar. The focus target must stay visible.

MDN scroll-margin-top on headings and links adds scroll padding equal to the header height. Example: a, h2 { scroll-margin-top: 5rem; } match the real bar height.

Test with keyboard only: Tab from the logo to the first body link. If the focus ring is clipped, raise the margin; do not remove the outline. web.dev covers focus patterns.

A tall header on phones steals viewport. Consider a shorter bar after scroll, but keep the menu control at least 44px.

Ask the AI only to add scroll-margin on headings and hash targets, not to drop position sticky without a reason.

Sources: MDN scroll-margin, MDN position, web.dev Manage focus.
