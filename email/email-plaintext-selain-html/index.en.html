Include a Plain-Text Part besides Clincoo HTML Email

HTML-only email breaks in clients that block images. Send a plain-text part with the same links.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In editor.clincoo.buzz open the invite or reset email template. Confirm there is a text/plain part, not only text/html.

Copy the call to action and the full URL into the text part. Do not rely on an image button that disappears when images are blocked.

Keep plain-text lines short so they do not wrap badly. One link per line is easier to copy.

Preview in a client that shows the plain part. Links should point at app.clincoo.buzz or blog.clincoo.buzz, not at a temporary preview URL.

Ask AI to draft the text part from the HTML you already have. Compare every link before you send.
