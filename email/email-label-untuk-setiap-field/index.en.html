Put an HTML label on Every Clincoo Email Form Field

A placeholder is not a label. Without for and id, screen readers and text clicks never focus the input.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo contact forms often rely on a placeholder that says "Email address". Once the visitor types, the hint disappears. Screen readers also miss a stable field name.

Write and id="email" on the input. A placeholder may stay as a format example, not as the only caption. type="email" and autocomplete="email" help the browser autofill.

Test by clicking the label text: focus should move to the input. In DevTools, confirm no label lacks for, and no two ids collide on one page in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Ask AI only to add labels and for/id attributes. Do not let it restyle the whole form. Paste just the email field markup so the diff stays small.

Correct labels cut send errors and raise accessibility without a new backend. An honest form on app.clincoo.buzz earns more trust than an empty box with a gray placeholder.
