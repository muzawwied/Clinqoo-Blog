Add a List-Unsubscribe Header to Clincoo Email

Mail clients show unsubscribe when the List-Unsubscribe header exists and its URL is real.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the editor.clincoo.buzz email template add a List-Unsubscribe header pointing at an unsubscribe URL on app.clincoo.buzz, plus a mailto fallback.

The URL should be one-click and should not ask for login if the token is already in the link. Do not send people to a marketing page.

Include the same link in the HTML footer and in the plain-text part. The header alone is not enough for older clients.

Send a test to your own inbox. Confirm the unsubscribe control appears and does not 404.

Ask AI to draft the header example, not to paste the recipient list. Do not send real addresses into chat.

Record the header format on blog.clincoo.buzz so the next campaign does not ship mail with no way out.
