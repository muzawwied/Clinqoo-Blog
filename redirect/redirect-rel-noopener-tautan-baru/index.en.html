Add rel=noopener on Clincoo Links that Open a New Tab

target=_blank without rel=noopener gives the new tab window.opener. Lock the origin tab when sending users off-site.

Artikel ini tersedia dalam: Indonesia · English

Clincoo pages often send people to docs, checkout, or a preview with target=_blank. The new tab can write window.opener.location and bounce the project tab elsewhere.

Set rel="noopener noreferrer" on every external link that opens a new tab. noopener drops opener; noreferrer also hides Referer when you do not want the project domain leaking to a third party.

Do not rely on modern browsers alone. Old templates and AI snippets still paste target=_blank without rel. Search for _blank in editor.clincoo.buzz and fill the attribute one link at a time.

Test source links and CTA buttons. The new tab should open, the project tab should stay on the same URL, and the console should stay free of tabnabbing warnings.

Clincoo renders the HTML you save. The right rel keeps off-site redirects from hijacking the app.clincoo.buzz session.
