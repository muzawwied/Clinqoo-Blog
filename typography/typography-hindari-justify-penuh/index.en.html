Avoid Full text-align: justify on Clincoo Body Copy

Full justification creates rivers of space in narrow columns. Use start/left for Indonesian and English body text.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

text-align: justify evens both edges by stretching word spacing. On a phone, those gaps become white rivers that break reading rhythm.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, keep body paragraphs at text-align: start. MDN notes justify is safer in print with hyphenation, not in a responsive column.

Indonesian rarely has reliable automatic hyphenation. Without good hyphens, justify only widens spaces.

Ask AI to find justify rules in the page CSS. Paste the typography file and request a switch to start except for a narrow quote block designed for print.

web.dev stresses readability on real devices. Clincoo pages on app.clincoo.buzz should feel fine in one hand, not imitate magazine edges.
