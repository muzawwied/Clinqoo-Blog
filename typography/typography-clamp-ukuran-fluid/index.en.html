Use clamp() for Fluid Text Sizes on Clincoo Pages

Headings that jump from 1.25rem to 3rem at one breakpoint feel harsh. clamp() eases the scale between phone and desktop.

Indonesia · English

Separate media queries for every heading make CSS long and easy to desync. MDN clamp(MIN, VAL, MAX) gives a floor, a preferred value (often vw), and a ceiling in one declaration.

Practical example: font-size: clamp(1.5rem, 4vw + 1rem, 2.5rem) on h1. web.dev reminds you that VAL should mix a viewport unit with rem so text zoom still works. Do not lock MAX in raw px if you care about access.

Test at 320, 768, and 1280 plus 200% text zoom. If a heading collides with the logo, lower MAX; do not disable zoom. Keep body near 1rem; fluid helps most on h1-h3 and leads.

Limit tokens. One heading scale and one body size is easier to audit than five competing clamps. Avoid font-size on wrappers that break rem inheritance.

Ask the AI only to replace heading font-size rules, not the whole type system. Then check overflow on long Indonesian titles in category cards.

Sources: MDN clamp(), MDN font-size, web.dev min(), max(), and clamp().
