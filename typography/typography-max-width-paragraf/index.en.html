Cap Paragraph Max-Width on Clincoo Pages

Lines that stretch edge to edge tire the eye. Keep body measure around 60-75 characters.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Wide templates let Clincoo paragraphs run gutter to gutter on desktop. The eye loses the start of the next line. That is a typography defect, not a taste for extra space.

In project CSS, set max-width on the article container or on p inside .body. About 36-40rem, or 60-75 characters per line, reads well. Do not shrink the whole layout to 400px; nav and hero may stay wide.

Check desktop preview and 200% zoom. A capped paragraph must not shove images or buttons off the viewport. Use margin auto if you want the text column centered.

Ask AI only to add max-width and line-height on selectors you already have. Reject a full typography rewrite. Make lists and blockquotes share the same measure.

Clincoo renders the CSS you save. Scannable paragraphs make the site feel finished, not merely filled with text.
