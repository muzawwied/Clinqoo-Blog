Raise Line-Height on Long-Form Clincoo Text

Dense text on blog cards is hard to read on a phone. Split heading and paragraph line-height.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates use line-height 1.2 for every text node. Paragraphs on blog.clincoo.buzz then feel cramped on a narrow screen.

On editor.clincoo.buzz, set headings to 1.2–1.3 and body copy to 1.5–1.7. Do not inherit one value from body onto h1.

Test in mobile preview: if lines stick together, raise body line-height only, not the global font-size.

Ask AI for typography tokens, not a full CSS rewrite of app.clincoo.buzz.

Clincoo keeps the brand font; only reading line gap changes.
