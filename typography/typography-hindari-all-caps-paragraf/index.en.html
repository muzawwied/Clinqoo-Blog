Avoid All Caps on Long Clincoo Paragraphs

Full capitals slow reading and feel like shouting. Caps belong on short labels only.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Some heroes on editor.clincoo.buzz use text-transform: uppercase on the whole lead. On a phone the lines are hard to scan.

Limit caps to badges, buttons, and 1–3 word labels. Body stays sentence case. Do not overdo letter-spacing on caps.

Paste the hero class to AI. Ask to drop the transform, not to change global font-size.

Read the preview aloud on app.clincoo.buzz. Write the caps rule on blog.clincoo.buzz.
