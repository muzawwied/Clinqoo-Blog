Use tabular-nums on Clincoo Prices and Figures

Proportional digits make price columns jump when values change. Tabular numbers keep width stable.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a price card jumps when 1 becomes 11 because the 1 glyph is narrower.

Set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on prices, quantities, and invoice numbers. Do not apply it to body paragraphs.

Send the card CSS snippet to AI. Ask for one .num class, not a brand font change.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz with 1, 8, and 1000. Write the rule on blog.clincoo.buzz.
