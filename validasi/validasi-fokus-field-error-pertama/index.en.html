Move Focus to the First Invalid Field on a Clincoo Form

After a failed submit, do not leave the screen sitting on the button. Focus the first invalid field and keep the message nearby.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Long forms on editor.clincoo.buzz often fail silently. Users hit submit and never learn which field broke.

After validation, call focus() on the first invalid control. Scroll it into view if it sits outside the viewport.

Wire aria-describedby to the error text under the field. Do not rely on alert() or a toast that vanishes.

Paste the form markup to AI and ask for this order: check, mark, focus, read the message. Reject a validation library before this flow works.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz follows the same order so fixes on blog.clincoo.buzz stay easy to copy.
