Set the Right inputmode so Clincoo Phone Keyboards Match the Field

inputmode numeric or email shows the right keypad without changing the value.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Contact forms on Clincoo sites often keep type=text for numbers. Phone visitors then hunt for digits on a letter keyboard. inputmode numeric or decimal shows a number pad without rigid native validation.

MDN separates type (data meaning) from inputmode (keypad). For email use type=email plus inputmode=email. For phone, type=tel and inputmode=tel so plus and star keys appear.

web.dev stresses mobile forms that avoid extra panning. Test in the narrow preview of editor.clincoo.buzz, then open the subdomain on a real phone.

Ask AI to add inputmode on one form only. Do not rename fields. Check autofill and 16px font size so Safari does not zoom.

The right keyboard speeds up forms on app.clincoo.buzz and cuts typos in order numbers.
