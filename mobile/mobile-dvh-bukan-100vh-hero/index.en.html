Use dvh, Not 100vh, for a Clincoo Hero on Phones

100vh in iOS Safari is taller than the visible area. The hero gets clipped by browser chrome. Prefer dvh or a shorter min-height.

Indonesia · English

A Clincoo hero with height: 100vh often hides the CTA behind Safari chrome. Classic viewport height includes toolbars that show and hide.

Use 100dvh or min-height: 80svh so the hero follows the dynamic area. Keep 100vh as a fallback for older browsers, then test on a real phone.

Do not pair 100vh with notch padding and skip safe-area. Top content can slide under the status bar.

In Clincoo preview, shrink the window height and open the URL in iOS Safari. If Contact is clipped, shorten the hero instead of forcing a full screen.

MDN and web.dev treat svh/lvh/dvh as the honest phone units. Clincoo deploys the CSS you save.
