Do Not Block Phone Scrolling with touch-action on Clincoo Sites

touch-action: none on a large wrapper freezes scroll. Limit it to the swipe control that actually needs it.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Sliders, maps, or carousels in Clincoo templates often get touch-action: none so a horizontal swipe does not scroll the page. If that property sits on a full section, phone visitors cannot scroll.

Apply touch-action only on the narrow control element. Leave body and article on the browser default scroll behavior.

Test in iOS Safari and Android Chrome from the editor.clincoo.buzz preview. A one-finger up-down gesture must still move the page.

If you use a swipe library, read its official touch-action option. Do not paste CSS that disables manipulation on * or .wrap.

After the subdomain deploy, thumb-scroll from hero to footer. If the page feels stuck, remove touch-action from the outer wrapper.
