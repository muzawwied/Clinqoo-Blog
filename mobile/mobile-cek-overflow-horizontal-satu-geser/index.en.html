Check Horizontal Overflow with One Swipe on a Phone

A Clincoo page that can be swiped sideways hides important buttons.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Open the app.clincoo.buzz preview on a phone. Swipe the page to the right. If the body moves, some element is wider than the screen.

In DevTools, find elements with a fixed width or images without max-width.

Fix them on editor.clincoo.buzz: max-width 100 percent, min-width zero on flex items, and avoid padding added on top of width 100 percent.

Test again in portrait and landscape. A single leftover swipe already breaks the CTA.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz only feels tidy on a phone when the page cannot be swiped sideways.
