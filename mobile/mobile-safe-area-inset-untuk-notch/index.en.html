Use safe-area-inset so Clincoo Buttons Stay Clear of the Notch

The phone home bar often covers the save button. Padding with env(safe-area-inset-bottom) keeps the hit area usable.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the app.clincoo.buzz preview on a notched phone, a bottom save button is often covered by the system bar.

Add viewport-fit=cover to the viewport meta, then padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom) on the action bar.

Do not only add a fixed 20px padding. The env value follows the device, including left and right edges in landscape.

Test in DevTools device mode and on a real phone. Keep the touch target at least 44px after padding.

Note this CSS snippet on blog.clincoo.buzz so new templates do not repeat a clipped button.
