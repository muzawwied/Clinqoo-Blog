Keep Clincoo Touch Targets at Least 44px

Buttons and links under 44px are easy to miss with a thumb. Enlarge the hit area without changing the text.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the editor.clincoo.buzz preview, close icons and nav links are often only as tall as the text. On a phone, the thumb hits the neighboring control.

Give tappable controls a min-height and min-width of about 44px. Padding grows the hit area without enlarging the type.

Do not rely on hover for a primary action. Touch screens have no stable hover. Keep the keyboard focus visible.

Ask AI to measure the current button height in CSS, then suggest padding. Do not ask AI to replace the whole nav component.

Test with a thumb on app.clincoo.buzz at 360px. Document the target size on blog.clincoo.buzz.
