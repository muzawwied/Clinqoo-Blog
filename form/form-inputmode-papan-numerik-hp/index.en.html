Set inputmode so Clincoo Forms Open a Numeric Keypad on Phones

Postal codes, OTPs, and amounts in Clincoo are faster to type when inputmode opens a number pad on touch devices.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

type=number shows steppers and rejects leading zeros. Postal codes, OTPs, and account numbers are not values to calculate. type=text plus inputmode=numeric or decimal is a better fit.

Set inputmode="numeric" on digit-only fields and inputmode="decimal" when currency separators appear. Keep name, autocomplete, and maxlength. inputmode only changes the virtual keyboard, not validation.

MDN lists inputmode values. web.dev notes that the wrong keyboard slows form completion on touch devices. In the Clincoo editor, focus the field in phone preview or DevTools device mode.

Do not combine type=number with inputmode unless you truly need a native numeric value. Steppers and accidental scrolls change values on phones. Keep error text next to the field, not only on the keyboard.

One attribute cuts friction without script. Clincoo forms that people fill on phones gain the most from a keypad that matches the data.
