Use novalidate, Then Check Fields Yourself in Clincoo

Native browser validation often clashes with Clincoo form design. Turn the default off and inspect fields one by one.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The novalidate attribute on a form in editor.clincoo.buzz stops native popups from covering your custom error text.

After submit, loop each required input. If empty, set textContent on the error element next to the field, then focus the first failure.

Do not rely on alert(). In the app.clincoo.buzz preview an alert breaks the flow and is hard to read on a phone.

Paste the check function into AI and ask it which field still has no message. One form, one request.

Test in Clincoo preview: submit empty, submit a broken email, then submit valid data. Those three paths catch most form bugs.
