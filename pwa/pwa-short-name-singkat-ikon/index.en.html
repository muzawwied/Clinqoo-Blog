Set a Short short_name in the Clincoo PWA Manifest

Long names get clipped under home-screen icons. Use a short_name of about 12 characters or fewer in the Clincoo manifest.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The manifest name field may be descriptive. The short_name that appears under the home-screen icon may not. If you only set name to "Studio Desain Andalan Nusantara", the icon label becomes a random fragment.

Write short_name as a short brand: one word or two syllables, ideally under 12 characters. Keep the full name for the install prompt and app manager. Both should refer to the same site, not a different slogan.

Place manifest.json at the root of the Clincoo project and link it from head with rel="manifest". After you change short_name, people who already installed the app may keep the old label until they remove the icon and install again.

Test on Android and desktop Chromium. iOS uses apple-mobile-web-app-title; match it to short_name so the label does not split into two identities.

Do not change short_name on every small rebrand. An icon rename makes people wonder whether this is the same app. Record the name decision in the project README.
