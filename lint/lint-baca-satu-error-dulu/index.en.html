Read One Lint Error First, Not the Whole List

Fix the top lint error first. Many warnings disappear after one fix in Clincoo.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz the lint list is often full because of one broken comma or quote. Do not ask AI to fix 40 lines at once.

Open the first red line. Fix it, save, then recheck. no-undef and missing-semicolon rules often cascade.

Paste the raw error plus about 10 nearby lines to AI. Do not paste the whole app.clincoo.buzz file.

Disable rules that do not matter on a small project, but note them in the README so the team knows.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz uses the same flow: one error, one fix, then commit.
