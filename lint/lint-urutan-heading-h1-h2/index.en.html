Check H1 to H2 Heading Order before Deploying Clincoo

Jumping from H1 to H3 breaks the outline. Fix heading hierarchy before you publish.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo pages often jump from a single H1 to H3 because card templates use H3. Screen readers and search engines treat that jump as a broken outline.

One page, one H1. Sections are H2. Subsections are H3. Do not change levels for visual size; style size with CSS instead.

After an AI generate, list every h1–h6 tag. If an H3 has no H2 above it, demote it or insert an H2 that names the section.

Preview the outline as a table of contents. WCAG expects a meaningful structure, not contrast alone.

Clincoo does not auto-fix headings. A three-minute heading lint saves post-deploy cleanup.
