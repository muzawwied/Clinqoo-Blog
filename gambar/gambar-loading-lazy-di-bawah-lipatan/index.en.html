Add loading=lazy to Images Below the Fold

Below-the-fold images downloaded at once slow LCP. lazy delays the download until they are almost visible.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

A story page on blog.clincoo.buzz often loads ten images at once. Those below the fold do not need to download on the first visit.

Add loading="lazy" and decoding="async" on img elements below the fold. Do not lazy-load the hero image.

Still set width and height so the image slot does not jump when the lazy image finishes.

Ask the AI to mark only below-the-fold images. Reject lazy on the logo and the first image.

Clincoo does not defer images for you. The right loading attribute saves data on app.clincoo.buzz.
