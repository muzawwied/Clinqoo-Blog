Use figcaption for a Long Caption, Not alt

An alt the length of a paragraph is read again by a screen reader. Move the caption to figcaption and keep alt short.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz tutorial images are often given an alt of three sentences. A screen reader repeats all of that text whenever focus enters.

Wrap the img in figure. Put the visible caption in figcaption. alt should name only what is not visible, in one sentence.

If the image is decorative beside the same caption, alt may be empty. Do not copy figcaption into alt.

Ask AI to split alt and figcaption. Paste the old markup and ask for a version that does not repeat the sentence.

Check the result in the app.clincoo.buzz preview. Pages on blog.clincoo.buzz use the same pattern so article excerpts do not force a long alt.
