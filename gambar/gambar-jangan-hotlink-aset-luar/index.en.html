Do Not Hotlink External Images on Clincoo Pages

A src on another domain can break, slow the page, or get blocked. Upload a copy to Clincoo assets and use a local path.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Hotlinking feels fast in editor.clincoo.buzz: paste someone else's image URL and the preview shows it. Tomorrow the source site blocks the referer and the slot becomes a broken icon.

Download the file, compress it, and store it in the project image folder. Point src at a relative path that ships with the deploy.

Do not hotlink avatars, brand logos, or documentation screenshots. Besides breaking, that often violates usage terms.

Check Network in DevTools. If an image is requested from a domain other than app.clincoo.buzz, move it before publish.

When asking AI for help, send the local path and the 403, not the hotlink URL. AI cannot keep someone else's asset online.

List local images on blog.clincoo.buzz so the team stops pasting external URLs.
