Allow Clincoo Form Autofill without Saving Card Data

A form with sloppy autocomplete can push the browser to store a card number on a shared device.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a "payment note" field marked as cc-number lands in the password manager.

Use precise autocomplete: name, email, tel for contact. Do not mark a normal field as card data.

Test on a shared device. Confirm there is no save-card prompt after a demo submit.

Paste the form attributes to AI. Ask to fix autocomplete, not a new payment library.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz keeps contact data separate from payment data.
