Wipe Clincoo Drafts from a Shared Device before Logout

Cafe and office laptops keep client page previews in cache and IndexedDB after the tab closes.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After editing on editor.clincoo.buzz on a shared device, logout alone does not always clear local drafts.

Add a wipe-local-data button: localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB for that origin.

Remind in the UI if the page opened in a normal window, not a private window.

Ask AI to review the wipe function, not how to copy the whole origin to a USB drive.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz treats a shared device as a default privacy risk.
