Do Not Store Client PII in Clincoo localStorage

A name, email, and phone in localStorage copy along when someone shares a browser profile or device backup.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a draft that saves a client email in localStorage feels handy until the device is borrowed.

Keep identity on a server session or storage you can wipe per account. Use localStorage only for non-personal preferences.

Audit the Application tab in DevTools before release. Delete keys that hold a name or phone.

Paste the storage key list to AI. Ask to split preferences from PII, not a storage rewrite.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz treats client data as a privacy load, not UI cache.
