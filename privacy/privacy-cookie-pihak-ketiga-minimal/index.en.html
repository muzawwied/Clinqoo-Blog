Limit Third-Party Cookies on Clincoo Pages

Chat widgets and analytics plant cross-site cookies you do not need to ship a page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A page on editor.clincoo.buzz that embeds a chat script immediately sends an identifier to an outside domain.

Load the widget only after the visitor consents, or replace it with a link. Check Application Cookies per origin.

Do not install three analytics tools for a one-screen page. One metric is enough for app.clincoo.buzz.

Paste the third-party script list to AI. Ask which ones are required versus deferrable.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz keeps pages thin so visitor data does not leak quietly.
