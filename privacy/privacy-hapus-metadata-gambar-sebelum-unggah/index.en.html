Strip Image Metadata before Uploading to Clincoo

Phone photos often carry GPS, device brand, and timestamps that do not belong on a public page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

An image uploaded to editor.clincoo.buzz without stripping EXIF can expose home coordinates in the original file.

Re-export or use a tool that drops GPS before the file enters the asset folder.

Do not rely on "just compress": some tools leave metadata. Check Properties or exiftool.

Ask AI to review the upload flow, not how to embed coordinates into an image.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz and app.clincoo.buzz shows a product photo, not a client home map.
