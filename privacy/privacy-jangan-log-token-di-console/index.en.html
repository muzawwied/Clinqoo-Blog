Do Not Log Tokens or Cookies in the Clincoo Console

Logging a token exposes the session when you share a screen or when AI reads an error screenshot.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

While debugging on editor.clincoo.buzz, a token in console.log is captured in screen recordings and issue trackers.

Log only a short prefix or a "has token" boolean. Do not paste a full Authorization header into an AI chat.

Clear the console before a screenshot for blog.clincoo.buzz or a ticket. Revoke any token that leaked.

Ask AI to review logging code, not how to store tokens in localStorage forever.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz treats the session as sensitive data, not debug text.
