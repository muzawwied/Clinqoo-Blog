Ask for Camera and Mic Permission with a Clear Reason in Clincoo

A permission prompt with no context makes visitors deny it, then your record feature looks broken.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Do not call getUserMedia when an editor.clincoo.buzz page first opens. Explain why the camera is needed first.

Show an explicit button. If denied, write how to reopen permission in browser settings.

Do not record without an indicator. Stop media tracks when the modal closes.

Paste the permission flow to AI. Ask for button copy and a fallback, not a getUserMedia polyfill.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz treats the visitor device as private space.
