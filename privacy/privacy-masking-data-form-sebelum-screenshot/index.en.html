Mask Form Data before a Clincoo Bug Screenshot

A bug report that includes a client name, email, or phone travels farther than you think.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Before you send an editor.clincoo.buzz preview screenshot to AI or a team channel, cover email, phone, and address fields.

Use dummy data that is obviously fake. Do not upload production form values to a public chat.

If you attach a HAR file, open it first and strip cookies plus token query strings.

Ask AI to explain repro steps from the masked UI. Do not send a client database.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz and app.clincoo.buzz protects client trust starting with bug attachments.
