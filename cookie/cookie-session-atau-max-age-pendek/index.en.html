Choose a Session Cookie or Short Max-Age for Clincoo Sessions

A session cookie that lives for years keeps logins on shared devices. Use a session cookie or a short Max-Age.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A login token with an Expires date far in the future survives after the tab closes. On a cafe PC or family laptop, the next person inherits the Clincoo account.

For work sessions, omit Expires and Max-Age so the cookie is a session cookie. For "remember me", set Max-Age in hours or days, not years.

Still set Secure and SameSite. Use HttpOnly when the token does not need to be read by scripts in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Check Application → Cookies in DevTools after logout. The session row should disappear or expire with the visitor's choice.

Ask the AI to change only Set-Cookie attributes on the auth script. Do not touch the consent banner or analytics cookies you already split.
