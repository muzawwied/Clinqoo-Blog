Set SameSite Lax on Clincoo Form Cookies

A session cookie without SameSite is sent on cross-site requests. Lax is enough for a normal login form.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On app.clincoo.buzz open Application, then Cookies. Find the session cookie used by the login form or draft save.

Set SameSite=Lax, Secure, and HttpOnly if JavaScript does not need to read the cookie. Lax is still sent on top-level navigation, but not on cross-site POST requests.

Do not use SameSite=None unless you truly embed across sites. None requires Secure and widens the surface.

A form on editor.clincoo.buzz that posts to the same domain does not need None. Test login after you change the attributes.

Do not print the cookie value in the console. Ask AI to review the Set-Cookie attributes only, and hide the token value.
