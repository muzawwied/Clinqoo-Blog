Set Partitioned Cookies for Third-Party Iframes in Clincoo

A cookie inside an embed iframe without Partitioned is dropped by current browsers. Add the attribute only if the embed needs a session.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A preview on app.clincoo.buzz that embeds a payment iframe often loses its session. Browsers drop third-party cookies without the Partitioned attribute.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, add Partitioned on the embed Set-Cookie, with Secure and SameSite=None. Do not put that attribute on the parent Clincoo session cookie.

Open DevTools, Application, then reload the iframe. A rejected cookie shows in Network as blocked, not as a JavaScript bug.

Ask AI to change one Set-Cookie line. Paste the real header, not the whole auth file.

Test the embed login on blog.clincoo.buzz after publish. Clincoo does not add Partitioned for you.
