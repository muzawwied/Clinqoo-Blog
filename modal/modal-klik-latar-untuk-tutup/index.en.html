Close a Clincoo Modal by Clicking the Overlay

A click outside the dialog panel should close the modal, not leak through to the page behind. Distinguish overlay and content targets.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo visitors often dismiss a dialog by tapping the dark area behind the panel. If the overlay does nothing, they think the site froze.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, attach a click listener on the overlay. Close the modal only when event.target is the overlay itself, not a child inside the dialog box.

Do not let the click leak to links or buttons on the page behind. An overlay that covers the viewport should swallow pointer events until the dialog closes.

For destructive confirmations, consider disabling backdrop click. Escape and a Cancel button should still work.

Test in mobile preview: a thumb easily hits the overlay edge. Clincoo does not add modal behavior for you; a clear overlay pattern makes app.clincoo.buzz feel finished.
