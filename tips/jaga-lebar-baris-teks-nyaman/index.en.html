Keep Line Length Comfortable to Read

Paragraphs that stretch across a Clincoo desktop preview tire the eye. Limit the body column width.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Good copy still feels heavy if one line holds too many words. In your Clincoo project CSS, cap the article column max-width around 60 to 75 characters.

Do not let the main container grow with a wide window. Center the column, add side padding, then preview desktop and phone.

If the heading feels far from the body, check margins instead of bumping font size. Comfort beats drama.

A comfortable site gets people to the footer. That small tip feels large once the page is live.
