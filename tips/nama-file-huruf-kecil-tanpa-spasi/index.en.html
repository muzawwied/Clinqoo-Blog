Name Files in Lowercase with No Spaces

Spaces and mixed capitalization often break links after deploy. Tidy names in the Clincoo editor.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On a laptop, my photo.jpg looks normal. On the web, spaces become %20 and capitalization can mean a different file. After a Clincoo deploy, broken images often come from HTML that does not match the folder name exactly.

Use a simple pattern: lowercase, hyphens as separators, a clear extension. Examples: hero-home.jpg, style.css, contact.html. Avoid spaces, parentheses, and emoji in filenames.

If the mess already exists, rename in the file explorer and update every link. Save, preview, then deploy. One pass is cheaper than explaining to a friend why the photo is blank.

Clean names also make explorer search and AI chat easier. You and someone else can point at the same file without translating.
