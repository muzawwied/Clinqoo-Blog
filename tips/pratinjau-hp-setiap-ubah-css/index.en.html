Open Phone Preview Every Time You Change CSS

A layout that looks tidy on the Clincoo editor desktop often breaks on a narrow screen. Check phone mode before you continue.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Changing padding, type, or flex in CSS feels done the moment it looks good in a wide window. Most of your visitors arrive on a phone. In Clincoo, shrink preview or use phone mode after every style change.

What breaks most often: a menu you cannot tap, text that hits the edge, and buttons that are too small. One scroll from the hero to the footer is enough to see it.

If it breaks, revert the last change first. Do not stack three new rules before you know which one is guilty. Save, preview again, then continue.

The habit is shorter than repairing a site you already shared. The CSS you can trust is the CSS you have already seen on a small screen.
