Keep the Browser Tab Title Clear

The HTML title tag in Clinqoo is your site's name in the tab and in search. Do not leave template copy there.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After you start from a template, the tab title is often still generic. A visitor with three tabs open will not find yours. In the Clinqoo editor, edit the title tag on every page: site name plus the page, such as Raka Portfolio — About.

Match the tone of the main heading, but keep it shorter. Avoid all caps and a long slogan. A good title still fits on a phone tab.

Check again after deploy: open the public URL and look at the tab text. If template or Untitled is still there, fix it and publish again.

The tab title is a nameplate you carry everywhere. Four precise words beat one sentence nobody finishes reading.
