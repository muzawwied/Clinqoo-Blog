Keyboard Shortcuts That Will Speed Up Your Editor Work

Save, open files, and close tabs without touching the mouse — five shortcuts that give time back to your day.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Saving through the menu is slow. Ctrl+S (or Cmd+S on Mac) saves a file in a fraction of a second — and saving often is the single best guard against losing work.

Follow up with Ctrl+Z to undo a mistake — and do not forget Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo what you just undid. Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Z, and Enter when asking the Clinqoo AI: that combination alone covers most of your daily actions.

Close tabs quickly with Ctrl+W, and search inside a file with Ctrl+F. If you forget all of this, remember just one thing: the Ctrl (or Cmd) key is the doorway to every shortcut.

Train one shortcut per week until it becomes reflex, not five at once until they blur together. Within a month your hands will work far faster than the mouse ever allowed.
