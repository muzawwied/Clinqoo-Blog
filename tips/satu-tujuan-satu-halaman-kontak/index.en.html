Give the Contact Page One Clear Job

A contact page packed with links makes people hesitate. In Clincoo, pick one primary action.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many first sites put email, three social profiles, a form, and a map on one page until visitors do not know where to start. In the Clincoo editor, pick one primary action: send a message or open WhatsApp, for example.

Other links can stay, but smaller. The page heading should say what happens if someone reaches out — a consult, an order, or a simple hello.

Test the button in phone preview with a finger, not only a mouse. If you use a form, submit test data once before deploy so you know where the contents go.

Simple contact feels more polite. People finish one step instead of comparing seven icons.
