Install Clincoo on Your Phone like a Regular App

Clincoo is a PWA — installable on your home screen without an app store, lightweight, and always up to date.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo is built as a PWA (Progressive Web App) — a website that installs like a native app. That means: no opening a browser and typing an address every time; one icon on your home screen opens Clincoo full-screen like any other app.

Installing is easy: open Clincoo in Chrome or Safari on your phone, then choose "Add to Home Screen" from the browser menu. The Clincoo icon appears on your home screen, and access becomes a single tap.

The benefits go beyond convenience. PWAs save space — far smaller than native apps — they do not eat data on giant updates, and they always open the latest version because they load straight from the server.

If you often continue projects from your phone — checking previews, making small text edits, or showing a site to a client — installing the Clincoo PWA is a small step that feels big every single day.
