Give Meaningful Clincoo SVGs role=img and an aria-label

Inline SVG without a role is read as a bundle of paths. Mark it as an image and give it a short name.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

An icon that explains a button or status is not decoration. Without role="img" and a name, a screen reader may recite every path or stay silent.
