Turn Off Focus on Clincoo Icon SVG Paths

Paths inside an icon button can become Tab targets. Mark focusable=false so focus stays on the button.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

SVG icons in Clincoo buttons sometimes take focus themselves. Keyboard users stop on a path, not on the control.

Add focusable="false" on decorative svg, plus aria-hidden="true" when the icon has no meaning of its own.

Test Tab in editor.clincoo.buzz. Order should jump between buttons, not into the drawing.

Ask AI to flag svg without focusable in button components. Do not ask it to rewrite the whole sprite.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz feels tidy when icons do not steal focus.
