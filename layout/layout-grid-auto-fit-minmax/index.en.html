Use grid auto-fit and minmax for Fragile Clincoo Cards

Fixed-px card columns break in a narrow sidebar. auto-fit + minmax adapt without stacked media queries.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Many Clincoo templates force three card columns with fixed widths. In a sidebar or phone preview, the third card wraps and leaves an odd empty gap.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, switch the wrapper to display:grid and grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr)). Items fill leftover space without overflow.

Pick a minmax floor that fits the card contents (title plus button), not a number copied from a desktop mockup. Test the narrow preview before deploy.

Do not mix floats or percentage flex-basis on the same grid children. One track system is easier for AI review and survives when the section moves.

Clincoo renders the CSS you save. Auto-fit grids keep card rows tidy on app.clincoo.buzz when the container shrinks.
