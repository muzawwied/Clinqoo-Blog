Audit Text Color Contrast in a Clincoo Project

Gray text on a bright background fails WCAG. Measure button and paragraph contrast before release.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo heroes often use translucent white text over a photo. On a dim screen, buttons and prices disappear.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, pick the main text, link, and form-label colors. Compare them with the background in a contrast checker. Aim for 4.5:1 on body text and 3:1 on large text.

Do not trust design taste alone. If the brand uses pastels, add a solid backdrop or text shadow behind the copy.

Ask AI to adjust hex values, not to replace the brand palette. Re-check after you change CSS variables.

Clincoo ships the CSS you save. A contrast audit keeps pages on app.clincoo.buzz readable outside the studio.
