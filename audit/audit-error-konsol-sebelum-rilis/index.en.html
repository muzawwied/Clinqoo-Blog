Audit Console Errors in Clincoo Preview before Release

Script 404s and CORS issues slip past a visual check. Open DevTools once per main page.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo preview looks fine, but the console is full of Failed to load resource for a mistyped analytics.js path.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, open preview, press F12, then Console and Network. Log every red line: 404, CORS, or Uncaught.

Fix the bad src or delete the unused script. Do not close the console and call it done.

Paste the full stack trace into AI. Include the filename. Refuse a bare try that hides the error.

Clincoo ships raw files. A clean preview console makes app.clincoo.buzz more predictable.
