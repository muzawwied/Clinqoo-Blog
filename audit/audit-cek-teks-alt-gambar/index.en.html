Audit Image Alt Text in a Clincoo Project before Release

Missing alt or filename-style alt harms accessibility. Audit one page of img attributes first.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates often leave alt="image" or alt="hero-1.png". Screen readers then announce a filename, not the meaning of the picture.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, open the HTML file you share most. Find every img tag. Write alt that describes the image purpose in one short sentence. Use empty alt for pure decoration.

Do not stuff alt with SEO keywords. Good alt answers what is shown and why the image is on that page.

Paste the img list to AI and ask for per-file alt suggestions. Reject a full markup rewrite. Test in preview, not only a linter.

Clincoo renders attributes as saved. An alt audit makes pages on app.clincoo.buzz clearer for every visitor.
