Audit Form Labels in a Clincoo Project before Release

Inputs without associated labels confuse keyboard and screen-reader users. Audit the contact form first.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo forms rely on placeholders only. When focus moves, the hint vanishes and visitors cannot tell which fields are required.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, open the contact or signup page. Every input needs a unique id and a label whose for matches it. A placeholder is not a label.

Add error text that names the field. Do not rely on red color alone to mark a mistake.

Paste the form markup to AI and ask for a label-input pairing list. Reject a full-page rewrite. Tab from the first field to the submit button.

Clincoo ships the form HTML as saved. A label audit lets people finish flows on app.clincoo.buzz without a mouse.
