Audit Labels and Required Fields on a Clincoo Form

A placeholder is not a label. Check for/id pairs and required hints before the form ships.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo contact form only has a Name placeholder. After typing, the hint vanishes and a screen reader has no field name.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, give every input a label whose for matches the id. Mark required fields in text, not only with the required attribute.

Submit empty in preview. The error must name the field that failed.

Paste the form markup into AI and ask for inputs without labels. Refuse swapping the form for a third-party widget.

Clincoo does not invent labels. An audited form makes app.clincoo.buzz usable without guessing.
