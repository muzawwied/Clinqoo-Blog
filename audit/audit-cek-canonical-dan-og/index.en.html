Audit Canonical and Open Graph Tags in a Clincoo Project

A preview URL leaking into canonical or og:url makes social shares point at the wrong domain.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo template copies often keep a sample-domain canonical. When the page is shared, social previews show someone else's brand.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, open the head of every public page. Make link rel=canonical, og:url, and og:image use the production domain, not a preview URL.

Keep og:title aligned with the page title. The og description must not copy a generic template slogan.

Ask AI to compare two file heads, not to rewrite the body. Confirm the og image URL downloads without a login.

Clincoo renders meta as saved. A canonical and OG audit keeps shares from app.clincoo.buzz pointed at your site.
