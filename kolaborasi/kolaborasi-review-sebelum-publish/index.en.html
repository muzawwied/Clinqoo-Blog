Ask One Person to Review before You Publish a Clincoo Site

Publishing alone misses a broken link. One second pair of eyes is enough for a small release.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo project owner hits publish at night. In the morning a menu points at an old folder.

Before release from editor.clincoo.buzz, ask one teammate to open preview and click three main paths: home, form, footer links.

Log findings as file comments, not only 'LGTM' in chat. Fix first, then publish to app.clincoo.buzz.

Ask AI for a five-item review checklist. Paste the page map, not the whole HTML.

Clincoo publishes the files you save. A short review keeps the release from surprising visitors.
