Revoke Clincoo Collaborator Access When a Member Leaves

Old accounts can still save to production. Revoke first, then rotate shared tokens.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A member leaves the Clincoo team but the invite stays active. They can still open the editor.

Revoke access in editor.clincoo.buzz the same day. Rotate any token that was shared.

Do not rely on a personal password change. Project rights are separate from email.

Ask AI for an offboarding checklist. Paste roles, not secret values.

Clincoo honors whoever still holds a key. Offboarding protects app.clincoo.buzz.
