Summarize Changes Before Handing Off a Clincoo Draft

A handoff without a summary forces teammates to read the whole diff. State what changed and why.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo drafts move owners with no notes. The next person repeats finished work.

Write three sentences: what changed, what is still open, and how to test in editor.clincoo.buzz.

List the files you touched. Do not attach the whole project.

Ask AI to turn a diff into a handoff paragraph. Paste the file list, not a minified bundle.
