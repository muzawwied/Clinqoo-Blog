Replace a Long Standup with Three Text Sentences on a Clincoo Team

A daily meeting eats edit time. Write yesterday, today, blocker — then return to the editor.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo team spends 40 minutes on standup. Files in editor.clincoo.buzz stay untouched until noon.

Set a standing thread: what finished yesterday, which file today, what is blocked. Cap each person at three sentences.

Call a meeting only when a blocker needs a decision. Do not repeat status that is already written.

Ask AI to turn the thread into a blocker list. Paste the messages, not a meeting recording.

Clincoo moves through saved files. A text standup keeps time for app.clincoo.buzz.
