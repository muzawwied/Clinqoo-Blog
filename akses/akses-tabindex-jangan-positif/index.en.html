Avoid Positive tabindex on Clincoo Pages

tabindex=3,7,99 breaks natural Tab order. Use only 0 or -1.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Positive values force an order that ignores the DOM. An ad or chat widget can jump ahead of the form.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, search tabindex=[1-9] and remove it. Native button and a are already focusable.

Use tabindex=-1 only to move scripted focus to a dialog title, then restore.

If AI adds a positive tabindex so the CTA comes first, reject it. Reading order beats fake conversion.

Tab from logo to footer before you deploy from app.clincoo.buzz.
