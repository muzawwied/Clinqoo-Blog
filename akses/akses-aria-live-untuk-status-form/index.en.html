Announce Form Status with aria-live in Clincoo

A success line that only mutates the DOM is silent to screen readers. Add a polite live region.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After submit on editor.clincoo.buzz, the text "Sent" often only replaces innerText. Screen-reader users hear nothing.

Add role=status or aria-live=polite on the message container. Do not mix polite and assertive on one node.

Test with NVDA or VoiceOver in preview: focus stays on submit, the announcement fires once.

Reject an AI suggestion that uses alert() as status. Alerts break keyboard flow.

Record the live-region pattern in the app.clincoo.buzz form component and link an example on blog.clincoo.buzz.
