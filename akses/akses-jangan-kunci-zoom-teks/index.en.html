Do Not Lock Text Zoom in the Viewport

maximum-scale=1 and user-scalable=no force tired eyes to read tiny type.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many templates paste a viewport meta with maximum-scale=1 so the "layout does not break". Visitors who need larger type then cannot pinch or use system text zoom.

In a Clincoo project, viewport only needs width=device-width and initial-scale=1. Let people enlarge. Fix a breaking layout instead of locking zoom.

Test by enlarging text in preview or on a phone. Buttons, forms, and headings should stay usable, not stack with no scroll.

If the AI puts user-scalable=no back while "tidying the head", delete that attribute before deploy. That is not tidy — it locks access.

Clincoo does not patch viewport for you. The meta you save decides whether people who need zoom can still read the site.
