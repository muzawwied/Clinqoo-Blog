Keyboard Focus Color from Tokens, Not a Random Outline

Hover is not enough. Give keyboard focus a brand token so Tab navigation stays visible on a Clincoo site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates style :hover and strip the native outline. Keyboard users then lose their place in menus, forms, and CTA buttons.

Add a --warna-fokus token on :root. Apply a 2–3px outline or box-shadow on :focus-visible, not on :focus that fires on mouse click.

Test focus contrast on both the hero and the footer. A token that works on a white card can vanish on a dark header. Check ratios with WCAG guidance on MDN and web.dev.

Ask the AI to touch focus rules only. Do not let it rewrite the brand palette while cleaning accessibility.

Preview in editor.clincoo.buzz with Tab only, no mouse. If the focus ring is missing on any link, the token is not applied everywhere.
