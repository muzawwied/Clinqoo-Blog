Build Clincoo Gradients from Brand Tokens, Not Random Hex

A hero gradient with stray hex breaks when the brand changes. Build linear-gradient from existing tokens.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

AI often writes background: linear-gradient(#4F46E5, #06B6D4) on the hero. Those two hex values are not in the palette, so a brand swap leaves a foreign stripe.

Build the gradient from var(--warna-merek) and var(--warna-merek-lembut). One :root edit then updates the hero, card overlays, and badges together.

Keep text contrast on top of the gradient. Test white titles and dark titles in preview. MDN documents gradient functions; web.dev covers readability over color and images.

Reject AI suggestions that add a third color stop without a reason. A tight palette is easier to keep in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Clincoo ships the CSS you save. Token-based gradients keep the brand intact after deploy.
