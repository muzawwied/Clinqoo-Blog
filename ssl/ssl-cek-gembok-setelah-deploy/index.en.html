Check the Lock Icon after Every Clincoo Deploy

A freshly installed certificate can fail on a preview subdomain. The lock is a release test, not decoration.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After publishing from editor.clincoo.buzz, open app.clincoo.buzz in a private window. Look at the lock, not only status 200.

Click the lock, read the host name and expiry date. Preview subdomains often use a different certificate.

If the browser marks Not secure, copy the full message to AI. Do not ask AI to silence the warning.

Repeat on blog.clincoo.buzz. A deploy without a lock breaks Secure cookies and the Clipboard API.
