Check the Full SSL Certificate Chain on Clincoo

A green lock is not enough if an intermediate is missing. Older devices fail to open editor.clincoo.buzz.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After a deploy to app.clincoo.buzz, open the page on another device. If only desktop Chrome works, the intermediate chain may be missing.

Use DevTools Security or an external test. Leaf, intermediate, and root must arrive in order. Do not upload only the leaf .crt.

Pages hosting often needs fullchain.pem, not cert.pem alone. A shuffled order causes NET::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID on some browsers.

Send the chain output to AI with the host. Do not ask AI to silence certificate warnings in the client.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz should open cleanly on older phones and new desktops.
