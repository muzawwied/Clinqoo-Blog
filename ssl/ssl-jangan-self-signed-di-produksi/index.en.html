Do Not Ship a Self-Signed Certificate on Clincoo Production

Self-signed is fine on localhost. It breaks fetch, Secure cookies, and visitor trust.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Local preview on editor.clincoo.buzz may use self-signed. The public host app.clincoo.buzz must not.

Browsers block many APIs on untrusted or insecure contexts: clipboard, service worker, geolocation.

Switch to a public CA or the host auto-issuer. Do not ask users to click Advanced.

Paste the warning screenshot plus hostname to AI. Ask how to replace the certificate, not how to click Bypass.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz uses trusted HTTPS so save and preview flows do not fail quietly.
