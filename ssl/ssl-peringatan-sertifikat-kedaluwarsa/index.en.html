Handle an Expired Certificate Warning on Clincoo

An expired certificate breaks the lock overnight. Check the date before a client opens the site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The browser marks app.clincoo.buzz Not secure if the certificate is past date. Visitors leave before seeing content.

Put the expiry date on the editor.clincoo.buzz release calendar. Renew before 14 days remain.

Do not ask users to click Proceed to the site. That breaks trust and cuts off secure APIs.

Send a screenshot of the warning page to AI with the host name. Write a short runbook on blog.clincoo.buzz.
