Clincoo Preconnect and CDN Hints Must Be HTTPS

A preconnect to http://cdn wastes time and triggers mixed content when fonts load.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Check the head on editor.clincoo.buzz. preconnect, prefetch, and dns-prefetch to the CDN must be https://.

An HTTP preconnect wastes the handshake. The browser then upgrades or blocks the font.

Match the CDN host to the asset URL. Do not preconnect both www and non-www.

Paste the head block to AI. Ask to drop unused hints, not to add five new preconnects.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz keeps hints few and safe so LCP does not slip.
