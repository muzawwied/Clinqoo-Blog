Turn Off Box-Shadow and Animation When Printing Clincoo

Shadows and transitions waste ink and dirty card edges on paper.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Cards on editor.clincoo.buzz use shadow-lg. On paper a gray smear appears around each card.

In @media print set box-shadow: none; animation: none; transition: none.

Do not ask AI for a new theme. Override print only.

Compare before/after PDFs on app.clincoo.buzz. Write the rule on blog.clincoo.buzz.
