Do Not Split CTA Cards Across Printed Clincoo Pages

Price cards and CTA buttons split at the page foot are hard to read. Use break-inside: avoid on important Clincoo blocks.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Visitors still print pricing and terms pages. A tight card grid on screen often splits: the title on page one, the price on page two. In @media print, mark cards, quotes, and CTA blocks with break-inside: avoid.

Do not apply avoid to an entire long section. The browser will push a tall block to the next page and leave a blank gap. Limit it to elements that are about a third of a sheet or shorter.

Hide buttons that only work on screen, but keep the offer text visible. A "Contact" CTA with no number on paper is useless; print the email or phone next to the card when print media is active.

Test with Print Preview from the Clincoo preview, not only DevTools. 100 percent scale on A4 shows cuts that a narrow emulator hides.

If a card still splits, reduce print padding or collapse the grid to one column. Paper has no hover; an intact block matters more than on-screen density.
