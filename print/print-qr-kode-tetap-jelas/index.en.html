Keep a Clincoo QR Code Sharp on Paper

A QR scaled only in CSS looks blurry on paper. Print from a vector or a high-resolution PNG.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, an 80px on-screen QR becomes a muddy square on a 300dpi printer.

Use SVG or a PNG of at least 256px. Do not compress the QR like a hero photo.

Scan the printed page with a phone. If it fails, send the source file to AI, not a screenshot.

Repeat on app.clincoo.buzz. Store the QR checklist on blog.clincoo.buzz.
