Use Landscape Orientation for Wide Clincoo Invoices

Many columns do not fit A4 portrait. Set @page size landscape only on the invoice template.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, an 8-column invoice clips on the right edge of portrait paper.

Add @page invoice { size: A4 landscape; } and the class on that template body only.

Do not force landscape on blog articles. Paste the @page rule to AI.

Preview on app.clincoo.buzz. Note when landscape is required on blog.clincoo.buzz.
