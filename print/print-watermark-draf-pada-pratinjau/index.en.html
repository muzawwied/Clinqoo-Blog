Add a Draft Watermark on Clincoo Print Previews

A test PDF without a draft mark is easy for a client to treat as final.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The editor.clincoo.buzz preview is often shared as a PDF. Without a watermark, a client prints a pre-release version.

In draft mode, insert Draft text with position fixed in @media print. Remove it on release status.

Ask AI for two classes: .print-draft and .print-final. Do not rewrite the invoice.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz. Explain the draft policy on blog.clincoo.buzz.
