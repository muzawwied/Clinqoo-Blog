Enable Background Colors When Printing a Clincoo Page

Browsers often drop background-color on print. Allow background graphics so Clincoo cards stay readable.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, price cards become empty white boxes in Print Preview.

Add print-color-adjust: exact on blocks that must keep color.

In Chrome enable Background graphics. Paste a blank preview screenshot to AI.

Test on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz before a client prints.
