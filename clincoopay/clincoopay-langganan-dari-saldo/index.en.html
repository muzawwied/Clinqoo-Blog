Pay Your Clinqoo Subscription Straight from Your ClincooPay Balance

Subscription plans are paid from your wallet balance, and plan limits are enforced server-side, not just in the interface.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Your ClincooPay balance pays for the Clinqoo subscription. You upgrade from inside the app without re-entering payment details.

Plan limits such as project count, AI quota, and storage are validated on the server. The interface only mirrors those numbers; it is not the source.

When a plan ends or is cancelled, the old limits apply again automatically. Your files and projects remain; only new additions are limited.

Check the next billing date on the subscription page before deploying something big. An insufficient balance will fail the next billing run.

Before a demo or launch, make sure the balance covers one full billing cycle so the plan never drops silently mid-presentation.
