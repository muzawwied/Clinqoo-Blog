Keep Your Clinqoo Transaction History for Balance Audits

Every top-up and transfer leaves an order trail with status and txid. That trail is the deciding evidence when a balance looks wrong.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Do not settle a balance discrepancy from memory. The Clincoo transaction history records the order ID, method, status, and amount of every event.

When a balance bug is reported, sort the history by time. Match each entry against the web wallet mutations using the txid.

A top-up still marked pending is not a bug. Wait for PAID or expiry before concluding the money is lost.

The history also separates top-ups from transfers between accounts. The two are often mixed up during manual counting.

Before a demo, run a quick check: sum the history, compare it with the displayed balance, and confirm the two match.
