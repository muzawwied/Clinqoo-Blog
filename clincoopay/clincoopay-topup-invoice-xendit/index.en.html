Top Up Clincoo with a Xendit Invoice: from QRIS to Virtual Accounts

The top-up flow: type the amount on the keypad, pick a method, pay the invoice, and the balance lands automatically once the status is PAID.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo top-ups use a Xendit invoice. You type the amount on the dedicated keypad in the wallet page, then choose one of the available payment methods.

Supported methods include QRIS, GoPay, OVO, LinkAja, and virtual accounts for BCA, Mandiri, BRI, and BNI. One invoice is issued for the amount you picked.

After the invoice is paid, the wallet page keeps checking its status in the background. When the status turns PAID, the balance lands without a manual refresh.

The invoice opens in a new tab. You can close it after paying; the wallet page finishes the rest.

Every order is stored with its ID, method, and status. If the balance has not arrived after paying, check the order status before topping up again to avoid a duplicate.
