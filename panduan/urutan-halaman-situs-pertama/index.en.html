Page Order for a First Site: Home, About, Contact

Do not start with ten menu items. These three pages are enough for a first site you can share.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

First sites stall because the menu is too big. In Clincoo, plan three pages: a home that says who you are in a few sentences, an about page for context, and a contact page that is easy to find.

Finish home until it looks decent on a phone. Then make about and contact as separate HTML files and link them from the same menu. Matching header and footer on all three makes the site feel whole even when the copy is still short.

Hold the gallery, blog, and shop. Those can follow after the three core pages are live through Deploy. First visitors do not need a maze; they need to know who you are and how to reach you.

If the words are hard, ask the AI for a draft and edit it in your voice. This three-page frame is what gets the project finished, not first-day perfection.
