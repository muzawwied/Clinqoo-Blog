Set Up a Clear Contact Page in Clincoo

A good contact page is more than a form. Say when you reply and which channel you actually watch.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

An empty form in a Clincoo template is not enough. Add one sentence: send the project through this form, or write on WhatsApp if it is urgent. People hesitate when three buttons have no guidance.

Write an honest reply window. 'Replies within two working days' is more trusted than 'we are always online'.

Test the form in preview until a message actually arrives. Also check the email link and the number so they are not leftover dummy text.

A contact page is a promise. Make that promise small, clear, and one you can keep after the site is live.
