Tidy a Clinqoo Project Before You Share the Link

A short checklist before you copy the site URL: tab titles, menu links, broken images, and pages that are not ready for other people.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clinqoo site can have a public URL right after deploy. The next temptation is sharing that link before the project is ready to be seen. Five minutes of tidying is usually worth more than an apology to the first recipient.

Open every menu item. Make sure nothing points at an empty page, a trial file, or a path you already renamed. Check the browser tab title — “Document” or “Page 2” still looks unfinished.

Preview at a phone width. Oversized images, stacked text, and buttons that fall off the screen show up faster on a small device than in the desktop editor.

If a draft page still exists, do not link it from the main menu. Keep drafts in a separate folder or leave them unlinked until the content is ready. Only then copy the URL and share it.
