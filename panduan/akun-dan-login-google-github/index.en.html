Sign in to Clinqoo with Google or GitHub

Learn how Clinqoo login works with Google and GitHub, its benefits, and what happens to your account data.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clinqoo deliberately avoids building its own password system. Just sign in with the Google or GitHub account you already have — one approval click and you are inside the editor. That means the one password you already trust across your digital life is also what protects your Clinqoo account.

It works the industry-standard way: you are redirected to the official Google or GitHub page, pick your account, then return to Clinqoo with a secure login token. Your credentials are never stored by Clinqoo — only your name, email, and profile photo are kept to identify your account.

For GitHub users there is an extra benefit: your GitHub identity plugs straight into project work that involves repositories. For Google users, signing in is a single tap if your browser is already logged in.

Signing out is just as easy: one button in the account menu ends the session. Simple, secure, no forgotten-password drama.
