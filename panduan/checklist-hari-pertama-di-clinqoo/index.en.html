Day-One Checklist in Clincoo: Five Steps Are Enough

Do not chase every feature on day one. These five steps are enough to make the account feel alive.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Day one in Clincoo gets noisy because there are many buttons. Hold on. Five steps are enough: sign in with Google or GitHub, create one project, change the home-page copy, preview at phone width, then deploy once.

Do not start with a custom domain, ten pages, or a complex integration. A short live site beats a grand plan that still lives in a tab.

If you get stuck, ask the AI about one problem only — for example a heading that clips on a phone. Keep the answers that work as a habit, not as an untested pile of chat.

Tomorrow you can add about or contact. Day one succeeds when you have a public link you are willing to open yourself.
