Check Mobile Preview in Clinqoo Before You Publish

Many visitors open sites on a phone. Mobile preview is a required step before deploy.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A site that looks tidy on a laptop often breaks on a phone: titles run too long, buttons are too small, or images shove text aside. In Clinqoo, open preview at a mobile width before you hit Deploy.

Check three things: text stays readable without sideways scrolling, menus can be tapped with a thumb, and buttons are not cramped. If something is clipped, fix the CSS or split a long sentence.

Large images are a common culprit. Compress them first, then make sure they do not force the page width. Preview saves embarrassment — better you see it first than a client.

Make the mobile check a habit, not a bonus. A first site that feels good on a phone already looks more professional.
