Clinqoo Balance: How to Top Up and What It Is For

Understand the Clinqoo balance — how to fill it, where the money goes, and how to read your transaction history.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Inside your Clinqoo account there is a digital wallet called your balance. It works like e-money in other apps: you top it up first using the available payment methods, then spend it on paid features inside Clinqoo.

Topping up happens from your account page: pick an amount, proceed to payment, and within moments the balance lands in your wallet. Every transaction — money in or out — is recorded in the wallet history with date, description, and amount, so you always know where your money went.

The benefit of using a balance over paying per item: transactions become fast because you never re-enter payment details, and spending is easy to track from one page.

If anything looks odd in your transaction history, do not panic — check the details of each row and reach out to support if needed. This transparency is deliberate, so you always stay in full control of your wallet.
