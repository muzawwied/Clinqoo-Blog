How to Choose the Right Clincoo Template for Your First Site

The best template is not the busiest one. Pick a structure close to what your site actually needs.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates speed up the start, but the wrong pick leaves you deleting unused sections. Begin with the goal: portfolio, small business, or an event page.

Look for a template with few pages and typography that is already easy to read. Skip heavy animation. You will change text, color, and images — that is enough to make it yours.

Once the template opens, read the folder structure. Learn which file is the home page, the CSS, and the image assets. Only then change content. That order keeps the site intact.

If you hesitate, pick the simplest template. A clear site is more convincing than a fancy one still full of placeholders.
