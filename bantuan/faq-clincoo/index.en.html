Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to the questions that reach us most often. If yours is not here, see Common Problems and How to Fix Them in the same category.

Is Clincoo free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes the editor, AI chat (50 messages per month), 3 active projects, and 5 web deploys per month. Paid plans only matter once you outgrow those limits. A full comparison is in Plans and Pricing.

How do I create an account?

Open app.clincoo.buzz and sign in with Google, GitHub, or register with email. The full walkthrough is in Welcome to Clincoo.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Ready-made templates let a site come together without writing code, and the AI assistant can write or modify code for you. That said, a little HTML/CSS understanding makes results far better — and this blog exists to teach exactly that, step by step.

Can the AI build my site automatically?

The AI is an assistant, not an automatic site builder. It explains, writes code, and drafts content, but it cannot click buttons or test your site for real. You review and approve every change. Details in The Clincoo AI Assistant.

How long does my site stay online after deploying?

Deployed sites stay online. You can redeploy anytime to publish a newer version, and deploy quotas follow your plan — Starter 5x per month, Pro 25x, Business unlimited.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. The custom domain feature lives in project settings with automatic HTTPS: you enter the domain and set the DNS records as instructed. The free plan includes one domain; additional domains are premium features marked with the crown icon.

How do I upgrade or cancel my subscription?

Everything happens on the Subscription page in your account. Upgrades apply as soon as payment is confirmed; cancelling (dropping to free) applies at the end of the current billing period, with no penalty.

Is my data safe?

Project files and account data are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. How we collect and use data is explained openly in the Clincoo Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and all its data anytime from the danger zone in settings.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. The collaboration feature lets you invite members by email with clear role separation. Limits per plan: Starter 1 collaborator per project, Pro 5, Business 20. The walkthrough is in Team Collaboration.
