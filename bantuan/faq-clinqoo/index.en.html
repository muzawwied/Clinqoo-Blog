Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to the questions that reach the Clincoo team most often. If yours is not here, see Common Problems and How to Fix Them in the same category.

Is Clincoo free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes the editor, AI chat (50 messages per month), 3 active projects, and 5 web deploys per month. Paid plans only matter once you outgrow those limits.

How do I create an account?

Open app.clincoo.buzz and sign in with Google, GitHub, or register with email. The full walkthrough is in Welcome to Clincoo.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Ready-made templates let a site come together without writing code, and the AI assistant can write or modify code for you. That said, a little HTML/CSS understanding makes results far better — and this blog exists to teach exactly that, step by step.

Can the AI build my site automatically?

The AI is an assistant, not an automatic site builder. It explains, writes code, and drafts content, but it cannot click buttons or test your site for real. You review and approve every change. Details in The Clincoo AI Assistant.

How long does my site stay online after deploying?

Deployed sites stay online. You can redeploy anytime to publish a newer version, and deploy quotas follow your plan.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. The custom domain feature is in the project settings with automatic HTTPS. The free plan includes one domain; additional domains are premium features marked with the crown icon.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Subscription page in your account and choose to downgrade. Your plan reverts to free at the end of the current billing period, with no penalty.

Is my data safe?

Project files and account data are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. How we collect and use data is explained openly in the Clincoo Privacy Policy. You can also delete your account and all its data anytime from the danger zone in settings.
