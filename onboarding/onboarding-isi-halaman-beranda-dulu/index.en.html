Fill the Clincoo Home Page before You Add Ten Other Pages

Building many empty pages makes navigation go live earlier than the message you meant to ship.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

A new project has eight menu links and a home page still full of Lorem. Visitors cannot tell what the product is.

Write a title, one benefits paragraph, and one action button in index.html first. Only then split into other pages.

Work in editor.clincoo.buzz until the home page is readable on a phone without wasted scroll.

Ask AI to tighten home-page copy to 80–120 words. Paste your rough draft and the Clincoo brand name.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz is judged from the first page. A finished home page beats ten skeletons.
