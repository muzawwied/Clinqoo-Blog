Check Every Internal Link on Day One of a Clincoo Project

Menus that point to # or missing file.html make preview feel broken from the start.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Templates copied into Clincoo usually ship About, Services, and Blog links. Many of those still point at pages you have not created.

On day one, open preview and click every nav item plus footer links. Note which return 404, which stay on #, and which leave to someone else’s domain.

Fix links to pages that actually exist first. For pages you have not written, point them at home for now or hide the menu item — do not leave dead links for visitors.

Use consistent relative links from the same folder. Avoid absolute paths to localhost or to a template copy on another machine.

Ask AI to scan index.html against the project file list and name links with no target. Verify in preview, not only from the list.

This habit is cheap on day one and expensive if you wait until the production domain is attached.
