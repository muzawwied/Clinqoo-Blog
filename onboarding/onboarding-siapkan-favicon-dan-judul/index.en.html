Set a Favicon and Page Title at the Start of a Clincoo Project

A browser tab without an icon and title makes the site look unfinished even when the copy is ready.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Many Clincoo projects still ship Untitled and no favicon until release day.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, write a title with the brand plus the page. Link a PNG or ICO favicon in the head.

Match og:title to the title. Check the preview tab, not only the canvas.

Ask AI to add one icon link and one title. Do not generate a full PWA pack on day one.

Clincoo ships the head you save. A clear tab makes app.clincoo.buzz feel ready.
