Write the Clincoo Site Goal in One Sentence before You Edit

Without a goal sentence, templates and AI suggestions pull the project in many directions at once.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

A beginner opens editor.clincoo.buzz and accepts every AI suggestion. The page becomes a shop, a blog, and a portfolio in one night.

Write one sentence in the README: who the visitor is and what they must do. Paste that sentence every time you ask AI for help.

Reject features that do not support the sentence. Forms, animation, and extra menus wait until the home page is clear.

Ask AI to test home-page copy against the goal sentence. Do not ask for a full theme redesign.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz is easier to judge when one goal is visible on the first screen.
