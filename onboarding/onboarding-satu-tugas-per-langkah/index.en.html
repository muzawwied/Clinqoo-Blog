One Task per Clincoo Onboarding Step

A first screen that asks for name, theme, and domain at once makes users leave. Split it into small steps.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The wizard on editor.clincoo.buzz fails when three decisions appear together. Users close the tab before preview.

One screen, one action: site name, then palette, then domain. Save a draft after each step on app.clincoo.buzz.

Show an honest Skip button. Do not lock progress behind required fields you will not use that day.

Ask AI to tighten copy for one step. Do not ask for a rewrite of the whole flow.

blog.clincoo.buzz uses the same pattern: enter, read, then act.
