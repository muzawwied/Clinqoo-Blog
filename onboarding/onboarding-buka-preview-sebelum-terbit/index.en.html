Open Clincoo Preview before You Hit Publish

Publishing without preview lets broken links and missing images reach visitors first.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

A page looks tidy in an AI chat, then goes straight to production. Visitors see a menu that does nothing.

Use preview in editor.clincoo.buzz. Click every link, submit a dummy form, and check the browser console.

Test a narrow width and a wide width. Fix what breaks before the publish button.

Ask AI for a five-minute preview checklist. Paste the preview URL and any error screenshot.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz shows what you publish. Preview protects the first visitor.
