Use flex: 1 on Clincoo Columns, Not a Rigid width: 50%

Percent widths plus padding make two Clincoo columns overflow. flex: 1 splits leftover space without manual math.

Indonesia · English

Side-by-side cards in Clincoo are often written as width: 50% plus 16px padding. The default box model adds padding outside the percent, so the total exceeds 100% and the row wraps or grows a horizontal scrollbar on phones.

Make the parent display: flex; gap: 1rem. Children use flex: 1; min-width: 0. flex: 1 means grow 1, shrink 1, basis 0% — leftover space is shared after the gap. min-width: 0 stops long text from enforcing a huge minimum content width.

Do not mix width: 50% with flex-grow. They fight over the same constraint and look different in Firefox and Chrome. If one column must stay narrower, write flex: 1 1 16rem on the sidebar and flex: 2 1 20rem on the content.

Test in the Clincoo preview: shrink the viewport until cards should wrap. If you need wrapping, add flex-wrap: wrap and a sensible basis instead of media queries that recreate percent widths.

flex: 1 plus gap replaces fragile negative margins and calc(50% - 12px) that break when the AI adds padding on a child.
