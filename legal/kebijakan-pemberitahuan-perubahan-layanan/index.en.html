Clincoo Service Change Notice Policy

How Clincoo notices feature, quota, or policy changes that affect an account and a live site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo may add, adjust, or retire features. Changes that affect how you sign in, deploy, or pay will be announced in a reasonable way. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Notice channels

Notices may appear in the editor, the blog, or email tied to the account. Material legal-policy changes are marked on the relevant page with an updated date.

What may ship without a separate note

Bug fixes, performance tweaks, and internal changes that do not alter user rights or duties can ship without a standalone notice.

If you disagree

You may stop using the changed feature or delete the account under the deletion policy. Questions go to halo@clincoo.buzz.

Relation to other policies

This notice policy complements the Terms of Service. The date at the head of a policy is the version that applies.
