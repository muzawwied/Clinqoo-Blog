Put a Version Date on Every Clincoo Policy

Without a date, readers cannot tell which policy applies. Keep older versions that are still linked.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Legal pages on blog.clincoo.buzz are often updated without a date. Users then cannot tell if the preview text is the one they agreed to.

Write the effective date at the top, for example Effective 3 October 2026, and keep the old slug if the link was already shared.

Do not overwrite a policy only in chat. The change must live on the same page with a canonical link to blog.clincoo.buzz.

Ask AI to draft a three-bullet change summary. Paste the old and new text. Do not ask AI to invent legal clauses.

Before deploy from app.clincoo.buzz, check that the date in the title matches the body. Preview on editor.clincoo.buzz before the link is shared.
