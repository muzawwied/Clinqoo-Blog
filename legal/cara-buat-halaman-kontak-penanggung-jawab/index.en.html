How to Add a Responsible-Contact Page

A site that collects a form or sets a cookie should have one page naming who is responsible. Create contact.html in the editor.clincoo.buzz project, then link it from the footer.

Minimum clear contents

A name or business name, an email you actually read, and an operating city are enough for a small site. Do not publish a national ID number or home address unless required. State that forms on this site go to that email.

Separate Clincoo contact from your site contact

A visitor to your site is not automatically a Clincoo user. Point editor bugs to Clincoo help, and point questions about site content to your email. The privacy-policy link should still go to your own page.

Check after deploy

Open the public URL, send a test email, and confirm the footer link is not a 404. If you use a custom domain, test on that domain, not only in preview.
