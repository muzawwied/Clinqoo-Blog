Responsibility for Site Content You Publish through Clinqoo

Clinqoo provides the editor and deploy. The live page content remains the account owner's responsibility.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clinqoo helps you write, review, and publish a site. The platform is not a hidden author behind every sentence on your public page. After you press deploy, visitors read your work — and the law treats it as your publication.

That means: do not ship false claims, material that infringes someone else's copyright, or content banned by the terms of service. The AI assistant can draft; you decide whether that draft is fit to go live.

If you quote a source, credit it. If you use a photo, be clear about permission. If a form collects data, explain why on your own site.

Questions about public site content can go to muzawwied@gmail.com. Fixing the page in the editor and redeploying is faster than leaving a wrong page in circulation.
