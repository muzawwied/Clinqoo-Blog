Clincoo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026. This policy explains how Clincoo handles your personal data. We wrote it in the plainest language possible, because privacy should not be complicated.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and profile picture (basic data from the login provider when you sign in with Google/GitHub).
  • Usage data: in-app activity such as projects created and features used, which keeps plan quotas working correctly.
  • Project content: files you upload and create in the workspace, stored so the service can function.
  • Technical data: IP address and device type for security and aggregate analytics.

How we use data

Data is used to: run the service (storing projects, publishing sites, enforcing quotas), secure accounts (abuse detection), send important notices about your account and subscription, and improve the product with aggregate analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

Where data is stored

The service runs on Cloudflare's network: files and databases live in Cloudflare data centers, and the sites you publish are served through their global network. Some third-party providers (such as transactional email and payment gateway services) process data only as needed for their function.

Cookies and local storage

Clincoo uses cookies and localStorage for login sessions, display preferences (like dark mode), and analytics. Third-party advertising tracking cookies are not used. You can clear them in your browser settings; your session ends and you can simply log in again.

The AI and your content

When you ask the AI assistant a question, relevant project context is sent so the AI can answer accurately. AI requests are processed to produce answers and maintain service quality; we do not sell or share your project content for other purposes.

Sharing with third parties

Data is shared only as necessary with: infrastructure providers (Cloudflare), login providers (Google, GitHub), transactional email providers for service emails, and the payment gateway for subscription transactions. We do not share data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account through the danger zone in settings, account and project data are permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period, except data we must keep by law (such as transaction records). Periodic backups are also removed according to their retention cycles.

Your rights

You can access your personal data (exportable from account settings), request corrections to inaccurate data, and delete your account along with its data. For requests involving your personal data, contact us through the help menu in the app — requests are handled in line with applicable data protection rules.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The latest version always lives here with an update date, and significant changes are announced in the app or by email.
