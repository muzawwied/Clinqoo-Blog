Clincoo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026. This policy explains how Clincoo handles your personal data. We wrote it in the plainest language possible, because privacy should not be complicated.

Data we collect and why

Data typeExamplesPurpose
Account dataName, email, profile pictureRunning your account and authentication
Usage dataProjects created, features usedKeeping plan quotas working correctly
Project contentHTML/CSS/JS files, imagesStoring and publishing your sites
Technical dataIP address, device typeSecurity and aggregate analytics
Subscription dataBilling history, plan statusManaging paid plans

How we use data

Data is used to: run the service (storing projects, publishing sites, enforcing quotas), secure accounts (abuse detection), send important notices about your account and subscription, and improve the product with aggregate analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

Where data is stored

The service runs on Cloudflare's network: files and databases live in Cloudflare data centers, and the sites you publish are served through their global network. Some third-party providers process data only as needed for their function — explained in the sharing section below.

Cookies and local storage

TypeFunctionRemovable?

localStorageDisplay preferences (e.g. dark mode)Yes — via browser settings

Third-party advertising tracking cookies are not used.

The AI and your content

When you ask the AI assistant a question, relevant project context is sent so the AI can answer accurately. AI requests are processed to produce answers and maintain service quality; we do not sell or share your project content for other purposes.

Sharing with third parties

PartyData processedReason
CloudflareProject files, databases, trafficHosting infrastructure and network
Google / GitHubBasic account data at loginLogin providers (OAuth)

We do not share data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account through the danger zone in settings, account and project data are permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period, except data we must keep by law (such as transaction records). Periodic backups are also removed according to their retention cycles.

Your rights

  • Access: your personal data can be exported from account settings.
  • Correction: inaccurate data can be fixed in the profile settings.
  • Deletion: your account and all its data can be deleted anytime from the danger zone.
  • Questions: contact us through the help menu in the app — requests are handled in line with applicable data protection rules.

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The latest version always lives here with an update date, and significant changes are announced in the app or by email.
