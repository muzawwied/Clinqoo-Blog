Clincoo Account and Session Security Policy

How Clincoo login works, the user duty to protect a session, and what to do if an account may have been used by someone else.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo signs you in through identity providers such as Google or GitHub. We do not store those providers passwords. Account safety starts with the device and identity account you use. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Sessions and devices

After sign-in, a session stays active in the browser. Do not leave a session open on a shared computer. Sign out if the device is not yours.

User responsibilities

  • Protect the Google or GitHub account used to sign in.
  • Do not share sessions, screenshots that expose tokens, or uncontrolled remote access.
  • Watch for fake links that imitate the Clincoo sign-in page.

If the account feels wrong

Revoke sessions at the identity provider, then sign in to Clincoo again. Review recently changed projects. Email halo@clincoo.buzz if you see changes you did not make.

Platform action

Clincoo may restrict sessions that look abusive or risky. The goal is to protect other accounts and infrastructure, not to punish without cause.

Relation to other policies

This policy complements the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. By signing in to Clincoo, you agree to treat your session with reasonable care.
