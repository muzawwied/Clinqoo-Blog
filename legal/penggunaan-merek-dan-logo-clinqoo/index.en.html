Use of the Clincoo Name and Logo

When you may mention Clincoo on a client site, what you must not do with the logo, and how to request written permission.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The Clincoo name, logo, and interface are the platform's identity. You may mention Clincoo honestly, but you may not imply that your product is Clincoo. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Allowed without special permission

  • Writing "Made with Clincoo" in a client site footer, as long as the link points to the official site.
  • Mentioning Clincoo in tutorials, reviews, or class materials with accurate facts.
  • Showing editor screenshots for education, not to clone the interface as a new product.

Not allowed

  • Using the Clincoo logo as your business logo or on a misleading domain.
  • Changing the logo's color, proportions, or slogan and claiming it is official.
  • Selling "official Clincoo" templates without a written agreement.
  • Registering a confusingly similar trademark.

Written permission

For partnerships, press materials, or logo use at events, email halo@clincoo.buzz. Describe the context, duration, and a design sample.

Violations

Misleading brand use may be asked to stop. Serious cases may proceed under applicable Indonesian law.
