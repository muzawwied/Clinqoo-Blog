How to Report Abusive Content

Clincoo lets people publish sites, but it does not allow fraud, brand impersonation, or uploads that leak someone else's data. Send the report from the account at app.clincoo.buzz, not as a public comment on docs.clincoo.buzz.

Collect evidence first

  1. Copy the full URL of the site, including the page path.
  2. Note when you saw it and what is wrong: fraud, a threat, or personal data.
  3. Keep a screenshot. Do not download or reshare leaked personal data.

Send the report

Sign in to Clincoo, open account help, and choose report abuse. Explain it in a few sentences. One report per URL. Add a second link only if it is part of the same site.

  • Do not ask the editor AI to delete someone else's account — the assistant cannot act on accounts.
  • If this is a safety emergency, contact local authorities as well as sending the report.
  • Repeated false reports can suspend the reporter's account under the terms of service.

After it is sent

You get a sign that the report arrived. Review does not always reply with details about the other account. If the URL no longer opens, an action may already have been applied.
