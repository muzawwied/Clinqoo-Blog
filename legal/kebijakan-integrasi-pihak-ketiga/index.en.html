Third-Party Integration Policy at Clincoo

How Clincoo uses Google, GitHub, and hosting services without handing over full control of your project.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo relies on identity and infrastructure providers so the editor and deploy can run. Those integrations stay limited to features you turn on. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Sign-in with Google or GitHub

We accept identity from those providers. Their passwords are not stored in Clincoo. Revoke access on the provider side if you stop using the platform.

Hosting and release

When you press deploy, public files go to the publish path. What goes live is the version you chose, not every draft.

Data we do not sell

Clincoo does not sell project contents to ad networks. Integrations exist for login, storage, and publish.

Provider changes

If a partner changes, related policies are updated. Questions go to muzawwied@gmail.com.
