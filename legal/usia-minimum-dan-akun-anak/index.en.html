Minimum Age and Children's Accounts on Clincoo

Who may create a Clincoo account, what applies to users under 18, and how a parent or guardian can request data deletion.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo is intended for users who can understand the terms of service and take responsibility for published sites. This article summarizes age limits and how children's accounts are handled. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Age limit

Clincoo account registration is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 13 may not create an account. If we find an account that clearly belongs to a child under 13 without parental or guardian consent, the account and related data will be deleted.

Users aged 13–17

Teens aged 13–17 may use Clincoo to learn and publish simple projects, provided a parent or guardian is aware of that use. They must still follow the Terms of Service, including prohibited content rules.

Parent or guardian responsibilities

  • Monitor projects the child publishes, especially if a custom domain or public link is used.
  • Do not let a child paste third-party personal data into AI chat.
  • Request account deletion at halo@clincoo.buzz if the account was created without consent.

How to request deletion

Email from a verifiable address, include the account URL or registered email, and explain the guardian relationship. We review requests within a maximum of 14 working days under the Privacy Policy.

Relation to other documents

These age terms complement the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. If interpretations differ, the main legal documents in this category prevail.
