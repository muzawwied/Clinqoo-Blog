Clinqoo User Content Copyright Policy

You keep ownership of the sites you write in Clinqoo. This policy explains the limited license the platform needs to host and back up that work.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Content you write, upload, or deploy through Clinqoo remains yours, provided you have the right to that material. Clinqoo does not claim copyright over a user’s original text, images, or code.

So the site can be published, backed up, and shown in preview, you grant Clinqoo a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to store and serve that content while the account is active. The license is not a transfer of ownership.

Do not upload material that infringes third-party rights: paid photos without permission, licensed code that forbids distribution, or text you did not create. Infringement can lead to takedown or closure of the related project.

If you close the account, Clinqoo stops serving the project under the applicable deletion policy. Technical backups may linger briefly for incident recovery, not for reuse as our own product.
