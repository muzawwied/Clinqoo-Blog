How to Report Abuse of Clinqoo Services

Steps to report a site, account, or community post that may violate the law or Clinqoo policy, plus the details you should attach.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clinqoo does not manually review every page users publish. Community reports help us act on harmful content. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

What you can report

  • Public sites that contain gambling, fraud, malware, or other prohibited content.
  • Accounts impersonating Clinqoo staff or stealing an identity.
  • Community posts with spam, hate speech, or dangerous links.
  • Misleading use of the Clinqoo brand.

How to send a report

Email muzawwied@gmail.com with a clear subject such as "Abuse report — site URL". Include the full link, the date you saw it, a screenshot if it is safe to attach, and a short reason why it violates policy.

What happens after a report

We review reports as soon as we can. If a violation is confirmed, content may be removed or the account restricted under the Terms of Service. We do not always share action details with the reporter because of the other party's privacy.

False reports

Do not use this channel to attack a competitor or settle a personal score. Repeated reports that prove false may be ignored.

Emergencies

If someone is in immediate danger or a crime is in progress, contact the authorities first. Clinqoo is not an emergency channel.
