Cookie Policy

Cookies and localStorage used by Clincoo — no advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo uses a small amount of cookies and local storage (localStorage) so features continue to work. Last updated: 14 September 2026.

Types We Use

  • Authentication (required): session tokens in localStorage ("clinqoo_auth_token" and similar) to keep you signed in. Without these, account features do not work.
  • Preferences (functional): dark/light mode, last selected chat mode, 5-minute cache of certain pages for faster navigation.
  • Local project data (functional): workspace files, chat drafts, and temporary top-up order status, so your work is not lost on page reload.

What We Do Not Use

  • We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell your browsing data.

Managing Cookies

You can clear local storage at any time from browser settings. Do so carefully: deleting authentication tokens will sign you out and local drafts may be lost.
