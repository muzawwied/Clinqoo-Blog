Community Guidelines

Rules for interaction on Clincoo community features: mutual respect, no spam, and consequences of violations.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo community features exist so users can share progress, ask questions, and support each other. These guidelines set expected behaviour. Last updated: 17 September 2026.

Core Principles

  • Respect other users. Healthy, relevant discussion is preferred.
  • Focus on topics around web projects, coding, Clincoo, and learning.
  • Do not spread misleading or harmful information.

Prohibited

  • Spam, repeated promotion, or irrelevant advertising.
  • Hate speech, harassment, threats, or demeaning content.
  • Promotion of gambling, fraud, investment scams, or other illegal content.
  • Sharing malware, phishing links, or attempts to harm systems.
  • Uploading pornography or content involving child exploitation.
  • Impersonating others or posing as Clincoo staff.

Moderation

We may remove posts/comments that violate these guidelines without prior notice. Repeated or serious violations may result in restriction of community features or account closure.

Reporting

If you find violating content, report it through the available community tools or email muzawwied@gmail.com. We will review as quickly as possible.

Relation to Terms of Service

These guidelines form part of the Clincoo Terms of Service. By using community features, you accept these terms.
