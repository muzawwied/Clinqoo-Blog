Clinqoo Activity Log and Audit Policy

What technical logs Clinqoo may keep for security, how long, and your right to ask for an explanation.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clinqoo keeps limited technical logs so the service stays safe: sign-in time, coarse IP, and server error traces. Logs are not a diary of your page copy. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

What is recorded

Typical log events cover account and infrastructure activity, not the text of your pages. Editor contents stay in your project.

Retention

Security logs are kept only as long as needed to trace an incident. After that window they are deleted or anonymized.

Access

The platform team reads logs when abuse or an outage is suspected. You may write to muzawwied@gmail.com if a session feels wrong.

Related policies

This policy complements Privacy and Account Security. Signing in to Clinqoo means you understand technical logs protect the service; they are not used to republish your work.
