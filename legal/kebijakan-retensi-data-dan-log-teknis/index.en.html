Clinqoo Data Retention and Technical Log Policy

How long Clinqoo keeps account data, technical logs, and payment traces — and when data is deleted or anonymized.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Retention spells out how long data stays on Clinqoo infrastructure. Without a time box, technical logs pile up with no purpose. Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Active account data

While an account is alive, profile, project, and preference data stay so you can sign in from another device. Deleting the account triggers personal-data removal within 30 days, except transaction records required by regulation.

Technical logs

Access logs, server errors, and security signals are kept for a short window to diagnose incidents. They are not an ad feed and are not sold.

Transactions

Top-up status and amounts are kept to meet financial record duties. QRIS payment credentials are not stored by Clinqoo.

Asking for an explanation

Retention questions go to muzawwied@gmail.com. We point to the privacy policy when subject-data rights overlap.
