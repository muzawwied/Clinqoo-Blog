License for Templates and Sample Code in Clincoo

Templates and sample snippets are there for your own projects. The Clincoo brand stays with the platform.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Templates and sample code in Clincoo exist so you can build your own site faster. You may change the copy, colors, and structure for your project. What you may not do: copy the Clincoo brand, logo, or interface as if they were your product.

If a template includes third-party images or fonts, check the notes that come with it. Rights in those assets can differ from rights in the HTML frame.

Code you write yourself remains yours. Clincoo only receives a limited license to store and serve the files as needed for the editor and deploy to work.

If you are unsure whether a snippet may be reused outside the platform, ask at halo@clincoo.buzz before shipping a sensitive commercial version.
