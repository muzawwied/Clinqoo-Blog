Compare Clincoo Lighthouse Lab Data with Field Data

A lab score on a laptop is not CrUX. Read both before changing the hero.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Lighthouse audit on editor.clincoo.buzz measures your machine, not real visitors. That is lab data.

Open the field-data panel when it is available. If lab LCP is green but field LCP is red, the hero or font is still slow on real networks.

Do not change the layout only because one lab score rose. Record the URL, date, and throttling you used.

Ask AI to compare the two LCP numbers, lab and field, then name one cause. Reject advice that deletes content.

Link the comparison summary on blog.clincoo.buzz and store the threshold in the app.clincoo.buzz release note.
