Chase Clincoo Page LCP, Not a Perfect Lighthouse Score

The last ten points are often expensive and invisible. A slow LCP element is felt on every visit.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A score of 100 on the editor.clincoo.buzz preview is not the goal. Users wait on the largest image or heading.

In the report, open the LCP element. If it is a banner without width and height, add dimensions and a smaller format.

Defer scripts that are not needed before first paint. Do not lazy-load the element that is actually the LCP.

Ask AI to suggest the LCP candidate from an HTML snippet. Reject advice that removes a heading just to raise the score.

Record the LCP target in the app.clincoo.buzz README and link before-after proof on blog.clincoo.buzz.
