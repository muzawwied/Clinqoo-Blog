Read Clincoo Lighthouse with Mobile Throttling, Not the Desktop Score

A desktop score on a fast laptop hides an LCP that fails on a slow network.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Visitors to app.clincoo.buzz often arrive on a phone. A desktop audit without throttling almost always looks green.

Pick mobile mode and applied throttling in Lighthouse. Record LCP, INP, and CLS, not only the circle number.

Repeat on the editor.clincoo.buzz preview at 390 pixels wide. If the layout shifts, fix that before chasing leftover points.

Paste the metrics to AI and ask for one fix per metric. Reject advice that rewrites the whole stylesheet.

Keep the mobile snapshot on blog.clincoo.buzz so the team does not celebrate a desktop score alone.
