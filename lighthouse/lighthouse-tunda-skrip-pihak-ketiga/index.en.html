Defer Third-Party Scripts That Drag Clincoo Lighthouse

Analytics and chat widgets loaded in the head often block first paint without serving the page task.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the Lighthouse report, open the third-party and render-blocking sections. Record origins that are not app.clincoo.buzz or editor.clincoo.buzz.

Move noncritical scripts to the end of the body or add defer. Do not put chat in the head just because the vendor snippet does.

Load the widget after the first interaction, for example after the user opens help. The preview page stays readable without it.

Ask AI to suggest one script tag that can be deferred. Reject advice that removes all measurement at once.

Document the deferred script list on blog.clincoo.buzz so the next release does not put the tag back in the head.
