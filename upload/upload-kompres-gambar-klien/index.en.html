Compress Images on the Client before Clincoo Upload

An 8 MB camera photo slows the form. Canvas or createImageBitmap slims the file before it is sent.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many people upload raw phone photos to a form in editor.clincoo.buzz. Without compression the progress bar drags and hosting quota vanishes.

Draw the file to a bitmap, redraw it on a canvas at a sensible max width, then canvas.toBlob at quality 0.8. Replace the original File in FormData with the resulting blob.

Keep the aspect ratio. Do not force a square if the product needs a landscape shot. Keep the original filename so server logs stay readable.

Show before and after sizes on the preview. People will wait for a short compress more readily than fail mid-upload.

Ask the AI to use createImageBitmap plus canvas, not a large library. Reject suggestions that upload the original file together with the compressed version.
