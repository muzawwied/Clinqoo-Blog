Disable Clincoo Prev on the First Page

A live Previous link on page 1 triggers an empty request.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The editor.clincoo.buzz pager still links Prev on page 1. A click yields an error or an empty list.

Use a disabled button or aria-disabled and block the click. Hide Next on the last page.

Do not send page=0 to the API. Clamp on the client before fetch.

Ask AI for safe Prev/Next markup. Test on app.clincoo.buzz and write it on blog.clincoo.buzz.
