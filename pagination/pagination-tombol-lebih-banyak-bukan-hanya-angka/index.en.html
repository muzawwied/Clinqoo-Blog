Offer Load More besides Clincoo Page Numbers

Number-only pagers break on a narrow screen. A load-more button stays clear on a phone.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Numbers 1–12 on editor.clincoo.buzz wrap at 360px. Visitors cannot tell how to continue.

Add a Load more button that uses the same cursor. Keep a pager for desktop archives.

Do not double-fetch on a double click. Disable the button during the request.

Ask AI for two controls on one API. Test on app.clincoo.buzz and note it on blog.clincoo.buzz.
