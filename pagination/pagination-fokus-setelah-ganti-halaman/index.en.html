Move Clincoo Keyboard Focus after Changing Page

After a Next click, focus stays on the pager so new cards are not read.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, a Tab user hits Next then remains in the footer. Result cards are not announced.

After new data renders, focus the results heading or a live region. Do not let focus drop to body.

Paste the pager Tab order to AI. Ask to move focus, not a redesign.

Test keyboard-only on app.clincoo.buzz. The checklist lives on blog.clincoo.buzz.
