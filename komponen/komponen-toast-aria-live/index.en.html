Announce Clincoo Toasts with aria-live, Not Animation Alone

Corner notices that only animate stay silent. Use a status live region, a close button, and do not steal focus.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo toasts are often a sliding div after a successful form. The animation is visible, but screen reader users stay on the submit button. The important message never gets spoken.

Keep a role="status" or aria-live="polite" container in the DOM. When a new message arrives, write the text into that container. Do not move focus to the toast unless an action is required. Critical errors may be assertive; ordinary confirmations should stay polite.

Give a clearly labeled close button and enough time on screen. Honor prefers-reduced-motion so the toast does not bounce. Do not stack five toasts; one message per action is easier to read.

Ask AI to add only the live region and a showToast(text) helper. Paste sample success and failure copy. Test in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview with a screen reader or accessibility monitor, and keep focus on the form.

A toast that speaks through a live region feels like feedback, not decoration. Deploy to app.clincoo.buzz after you confirm the message does not cover the primary button on a narrow screen.
