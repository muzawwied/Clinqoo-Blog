Do Not Rely on Hover Alone for Clincoo Tooltips

Native title tooltips vanish on touch and keyboard. Use a focusable trigger, the tooltip role, and Escape to dismiss.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Help icons on Clincoo forms often only have a title attribute. On desktop the tip flashes briefly. On phones and when tabbing, the hint is gone. Users never learn why a field is required or why a button is disabled.

Use a focusable button or link. Point it at a role="tooltip" panel with aria-describedby. Show the panel on focus or click, and close it with Escape. Do not hide required rules only in the tooltip; repeat them in always-visible help text when you can.

Avoid cursor-following tips that cover the input. On a narrow screen the panel can sit under the icon. Ask AI to add focus and Escape handlers only, not a heavy overlay library.

Test in editor.clincoo.buzz: Tab to the icon, hear the description, press Escape, and confirm focus stays put. Tap once to open, tap again or outside to close. Check tooltip contrast against the threshold.

A tooltip that opens without hover is a component, not a guess. Deploy to app.clincoo.buzz after the hint no longer covers the field label.
