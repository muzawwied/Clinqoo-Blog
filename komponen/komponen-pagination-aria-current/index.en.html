Mark the Active Page in Clincoo Pagination with aria-current

Bare page numbers without a nav or current marker confuse keyboards. Wrap pagination, name the links, and mark the current page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo article lists often end with bare 1 2 3 links. Users see a bold number, but screen readers only hear "three" repeatedly. The current page stays clickable and rel prev/next is missing.

Wrap the controls in nav aria-label="Pagination". Each link needs full text such as Page 2, not a lone digit. The active item uses aria-current="page". Previous and next are disabled at the ends of the list, not silently removed.

Keep shareable URLs: ?page=2 or /page/2. Do not rely on a hash if the list should be indexed. When AI writes markup, paste one pagination block and forbid copying ids from the cards above it.

Keyboard-test from the last card link into pagination. Focus must be visible. In the editor.clincoo.buzz phone preview, numbers must not wrap into two rows without touch spacing.

Pagination that states the current page helps people and machines. Deploy to app.clincoo.buzz after the list, title, and numbers agree.
