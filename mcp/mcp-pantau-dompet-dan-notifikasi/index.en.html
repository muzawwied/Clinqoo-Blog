Watch Your ClincooPay Balance and Notifications from an AI Editor

The balance, transaction, and notification tools turn your AI editor into a compact Clinqoo status panel.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

You do not need to open app.clincoo.buzz just to check a balance. get_wallet_balance returns the figure right in your AI editor.

list_wallet_transactions shows the latest history. Use it to confirm a top-up landed before continuing paid work.

list_notifications brings in collaboration invites, quota warnings, and project news without opening a browser.

Combine all three in one prompt: ask for the balance, the last three transactions, and unread notifications.

Remember these tools are read-only. Balance changes must still go through the official Clinqoo flow to stay recorded.
