Send a Minimal Repro before Asking in the Clincoo Community

Do not paste the whole project. Include a preview URL, click steps, the console error, and the relevant file slice.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

'It does not work' posts force people to guess. Without a repro, both AI and humans stall.

Write three things: a URL on editor.clincoo.buzz or app.clincoo.buzz, the click steps, and the full console error text.

Trim the file until the bug still appears. Strip branding, tokens, and client data before you share.

If you ask AI for help, paste the same repro. Do not change the topic mid-thread.

This keeps blog.clincoo.buzz and community threads useful for debugging instead of guesswork.
