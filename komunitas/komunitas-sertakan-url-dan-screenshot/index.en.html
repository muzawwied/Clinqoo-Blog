Include a Preview URL and a Screenshot when Asking for Clincoo Help

A question without a URL forces people to guess. Paste the preview link and one image of the broken state.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo community chats often stall because a bug report is only "the page looks weird". Without a preview URL, nobody can open the same context. Without a screenshot, they cannot tell whether you mean the header, the form, or the footer.

Copy the live preview subdomain URL, not a local file on your laptop. Make sure the link opens without a private login. If the page is protected, explain how to sign in or publish a temporary public copy.

Capture one screenshot that shows the symptom: poor contrast, a covered button, or a layout that breaks at phone width. Do not send ten nearly identical photos. Write one sentence above the image: device, viewport width, and the step you just took.

Add what you expected versus what happened. "I pressed Send, the page reloaded, the success message never appeared" is much faster to act on than "form error". The pattern matches a good GitHub issue report.

Keep a short template in the project channel: URL, screenshot, steps, result. New members copy that format and reviews stay kinder because nobody has to interrogate the reporter.
