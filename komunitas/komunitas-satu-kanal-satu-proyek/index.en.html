One Chat Channel for One Clincoo Project

Feedback scattered across three groups makes design decisions impossible to trace.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Color notes live in a family chat, copy lives in email, and bugs live under a status. At deploy time nobody remembers what was agreed.

Make one channel or thread named after the project. Pin the Clincoo preview URL. All feedback, including we dropped that, stays there.

If someone writes elsewhere, copy one sentence into the official channel and reply there. Do not keep two truths.

A channel is not an eternal archive. After release, move decisions into a short file in the workspace so newcomers do not restart the debate.

A tidy community is not a loud one. Clincoo stores files; the channel stores reasons.
