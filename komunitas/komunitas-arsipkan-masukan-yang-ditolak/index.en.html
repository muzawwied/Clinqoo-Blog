Archive Rejected Feedback so the Debate Does Not Return

A suggestion marked not now needs one line in the Clincoo project notes.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Next week the same person suggests dark mode again. Nobody remembers why it was declined. That loop costs an evening.

In the Clincoo workspace or the project channel, write: idea, date, decision, one-sentence reason. Example: dark mode postponed, brand contrast is not ready.

This is not a rejection of the community. It respects time. The idea can reopen after the next release.

Do not rely on a group admin memory. Newcomers lack that context. A short file outlasts chat scrollback.

Clincoo stores the pages you chose. A rejection note stores why those pages did not change.
