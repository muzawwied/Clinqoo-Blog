Write Down Design Decisions so Collaborators Do Not Replay the Debate

Agreed colors, fonts, and CTAs need a short note in the Clincoo project.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Without a note, every late arrival asks why the button is orange or why the hero has no slider. Old debates restart. The AI also re-suggests styles you already rejected.

Add a short file, for example NOTES.md at the project root. List three to five decisions: palette, heading type, tone of voice, and what must not change. One sentence per item is enough.

When you ask the AI to change a page, paste a snippet of that note in the prompt. "Do not change the primary color." New collaborators read the same file before they edit.

This is not a long proposal. It is a board that stops a Clincoo project from spinning on personal taste.

A community maintains decisions, not only HTML files.
