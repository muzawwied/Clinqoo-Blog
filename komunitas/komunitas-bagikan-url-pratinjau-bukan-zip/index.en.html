Share a Clincoo Preview URL, Not a ZIP File

Reviewers move faster on a subdomain than by extracting an archive on another laptop.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

When people ask for feedback they often send a ZIP. The recipient must download, extract, and open local files that are not the live build. The zip goes stale and paths break.

Clincoo already gives a preview or subdomain URL. Send that link. Reviewers see layout, fonts, and links as visitors will. Comments become concrete: "the hero is too tall on a phone", not "I cannot open index.html".

If the site is still raw, put one sentence at the top: draft, do not share widely. After fixes, deploy again and send the same URL. Do not switch the thread to random screenshots.

A ZIP is still useful as a backup, not as the discussion channel. A community moves faster when everyone sees the same version.

Keep one live link per feedback round. That is the cheapest Clincoo project etiquette.
