Record a Short Demo Instead of Writing a Novel in the Group

Fifteen seconds of the Clincoo preview screen explains layout faster than a long paragraph.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A written note that the hero is too tight is read differently by each person. Someone opens another laptop and thinks you meant the footer.

Record a few seconds of the Clincoo preview: scroll, tap the menu, point at the button. Paste it in the same thread as the URL. Do not replace the live link with video only.

Video is not a changelog. Still write one line about what you want commented. The demo only guides the eye.

If the file is heavy, cut the resolution. A community on phones does not need 4K to see a bad margin.

Clincoo already serves the site. Your job is to point at the part under discussion, not to write an essay.
