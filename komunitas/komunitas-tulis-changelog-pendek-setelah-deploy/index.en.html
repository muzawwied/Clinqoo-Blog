Write a Short Changelog after Deploy so the Team Stops Asking

One paragraph in the team chat or a CHANGELOG file prevents questions about what is new on the subdomain.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After a release, people in the group ask which page changed. The person who deployed already moved on. Context fades within hours.

Write three lines: what changed, which URL to check, and what you deliberately left alone. Paste it in the team channel or in CHANGELOG.md in the Clincoo project.

A changelog is not a novel. If the AI touched many files, ask for a short diff summary and copy that sentence. Reviewers know which page to open.

This habit serves a small community — a cofounder, a client, or a friend helping with copy. They should not have to guess.

A deploy with no note forces people to crawl the whole site. Three lines cost less.
