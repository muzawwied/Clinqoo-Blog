Ask for Feedback on One Page, Not the Whole Site

Reviewers stall when asked to judge ten files at once. Point them at one URL.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A message that says "please check my site" yields vague replies. People do not know whether to start at the hero, pricing, or footer. Time is wasted and you cannot tell what was actually read.

Send one page URL, one question, and one deadline. Example: "Is the CTA on /pricing readable on a phone? Reply before Friday." A narrow question produces an actionable answer.

After you fix it, do not open a new topic in the same thread. Start a second round with the next page. The Clincoo AI can tune copy on that page only, instead of rewriting navigation.

A healthy community rejects reviews without context. The person asking for feedback must narrow the frame.

One page per message protects reviewer energy and feedback quality.
