Credit the Template and Assets Used on a Clincoo Site

Communities notice short attribution. Name photo, icon, and template sources in the footer or about page.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Projects that grow from a Clincoo template or free assets often ship with no credit. Asset authors feel ignored, and visitors cannot tell the image license.

Add one line in the footer or about page: base template, photographer, icon set. If the license asks for a link, put the link there. Do not bury credit in an HTML comment.

If the AI pastes a new asset, ask where the file came from before you deploy. An unsourced asset is better replaced than argued about later.

Short credit is community language. It does not slow the release and keeps the site polite.

A Clincoo site with clear sources is easier to hand to the next collaborator.
