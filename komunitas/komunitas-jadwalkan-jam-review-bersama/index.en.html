Schedule a Shared Review Hour, Do Not Drop Links at Midnight

A community replies more cleanly when there is a short window to open the Clincoo preview.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A preview link sent at two in the morning sinks in the chat. The next day people see an old message and do not know which version is still valid.

Write one short promise: today at seven, open this URL, comment only in that thread. Redeploy before that hour so the Clincoo subdomain cache has settled.

If someone is late, do not send a backup ZIP. Ask them to open the same URL. Mixed versions split the discussion.

A review hour does not need to be formal. Fifteen minutes in the group is enough for the CTA, hero, and spelling. The rest can wait until tomorrow.

A small community lives on rhythm, not random notifications. Clincoo already gives a live URL — also give a shared time to look at it.
