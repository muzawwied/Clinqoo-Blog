Dark mode

Provide a Dark Hero Background Image for Clincoo

A bright photo on a dark hero hides the heading. Ship a second asset or a measured overlay, not a random filter.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo dark themes often swap text tokens only. The hero image stays a bright daytime photo. Heading contrast then falls below the WCAG threshold.

Cleanest approach: two files. One for light, one for dark. Switch with a class on html or a picture element plus prefers-color-scheme. Keep both in the project assets folder in editor.clincoo.buzz.

If the second asset is not ready, add a dark overlay with a fixed opacity such as 0.45, then test text contrast. Do not rely on a random brightness filter that ruins product photos.

Ask AI to add only background rules for .theme-dark. Paste the hero markup. Recheck logos covered in the contrast-icons article.

A hero that stays readable in both themes feels intentional. Deploy to app.clincoo.buzz after you reload with prefers-color-scheme dark in DevTools.
