Do Not Use filter: invert for Clincoo Dark Mode

filter: invert(1) flips photos, logos, and charts. Color tokens and prefers-color-scheme keep the Clincoo brand correct.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Some drafts on editor.clincoo.buzz use filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg) on body. The logo and product photos flip too.

Real dark mode swaps background, text, and border tokens. Images stay intact unless a dark asset was prepared.

invert also breaks focus contrast and form status colors. Success green can become an unrecognizable purple.

If AI offers filter invert as a shortcut, reject it. Ask for CSS variables and a prefers-color-scheme media query.

Compare light and dark preview on app.clincoo.buzz before publish. Note image exceptions on blog.clincoo.buzz.
