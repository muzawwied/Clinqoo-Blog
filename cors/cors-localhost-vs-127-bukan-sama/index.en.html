Treat localhost and 127.0.0.1 as Different Origins in Clincoo CORS Tests

Browsers treat localhost and 127.0.0.1 as different origins. List both or pick one.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A preview of editor.clincoo.buzz from http://localhost:5173 may pass, then fail on http://127.0.0.1:5173. Those are two origins.

Keep the test URL consistent. Add both origins to Allow-Origin only if you actually use both.

Do not ask AI for a large CORS middleware before you copy the exact origin from window.location.origin.

Test the API fetch from app.clincoo.buzz after the local origin is consistent. Record the result on blog.clincoo.buzz.
