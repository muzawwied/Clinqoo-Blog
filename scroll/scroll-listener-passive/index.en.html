Attach Clincoo Scroll Listeners as Passive

A scroll listener without passive blocks gestures. Use passive: true unless you truly call preventDefault.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A reading indicator in the Clincoo preview feels stuttery on a phone. A non-passive scroll listener forces the browser to wait for preventDefault.

Write addEventListener scroll with the passive true option in the editor.clincoo.buzz page script. Do not preventDefault on window scroll.

Compute progress with requestAnimationFrame, not on every raw event. That keeps app.clincoo.buzz responsive.

Paste the listener to AI. Do not ask for a throttle library before the passive option is set.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz can mark status without holding the user's finger on the screen.
