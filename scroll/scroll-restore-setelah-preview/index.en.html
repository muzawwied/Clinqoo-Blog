Restore Scroll Position after Closing Clincoo Preview

Closing a preview overlay often throws the editor to the top. Save scrollY before the overlay opens.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, closing preview makes you lose the line you were editing. The overlay changes overflow and then resets position.

Store window.scrollY or the container scrollTop before the overlay. After it closes, restore that value on app.clincoo.buzz.

Do not rely on history.scrollRestoration alone if the overlay is not a full navigation. That only helps page changes.

Paste the overlay open-close sequence to AI. Do not ask for an editor rewrite before position is restored.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz keeps reading context; the editor should keep editing context.
