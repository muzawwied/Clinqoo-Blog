Scroll to the Clincoo Form Error Field, Do Not Only Color the Border

An error message above the fold is invisible when the bad field sits below. Combine focus, scrollIntoView, and error text.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A long form on editor.clincoo.buzz rejects submit, then the user cannot see which field failed because the viewport stays on the button.

Focus the first invalid input, then scrollIntoView with block center. Show error text on app.clincoo.buzz, not only a red border.

Do not scroll to the top of the form. That hides a mid-form field that actually failed.

Paste the form markup and validation messages to AI. Do not ask for a toast library before focus and scroll are tested.

Clincoo on blog.clincoo.buzz explains steps; a form should move the eye to the step that failed.
