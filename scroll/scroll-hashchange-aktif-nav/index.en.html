Mark Active Nav from Hash and Scroll Position on Clincoo

A table of contents that ignores reading position is confusing. Intersection Observer plus hashchange keeps the active link without measuring offsets every frame.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Long Clincoo pages often have a side table of contents. If the active link only changes on click, manual scrolling leaves the marker wrong.

Watch headings with Intersection Observer and a rootMargin that accounts for the sticky header. When a heading enters the zone, add an active class to the matching link.

Sync the URL with history.replaceState, not repeated location.hash writes, so browser history stays clean. Listen to hashchange for table-of-contents clicks.

Do not use a scroll listener that calls getBoundingClientRect on every heading. An observer is cheaper and enough for editor.clincoo.buzz.

Test jumping in from an external hash, refreshing mid-article, and fast scrolling. The marker should stay stable and not flicker between neighboring headings.
