Stop Scroll Chaining with overscroll-behavior on Clincoo

Gestures inside a modal or sidebar can move the page behind it. overscroll-behavior cuts the scroll chain without fragile preventDefault code.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On Clincoo sites, a long list inside a dialog often “leaks”: when the list ends, the page behind it moves. The iOS rubber-band effect can also dismiss an overlay by accident.

Set overscroll-behavior: contain on the overflowing container. contain keeps the scroll chain on that element. none suppresses the bounce at the edge.

Combine this with locking the body while a modal is open. overscroll-behavior does not replace overflow: hidden on the body, but it stops leftover gestures from propagating.

Test in Chrome, iOS Safari, and Firefox. Edge-of-viewport behavior differs across engines. editor.clincoo.buzz preview is enough for desktop; also check a real phone.

Do not apply contain on html globally without a reason. The main page still needs native overscroll. Limit the rule to panels, drawers, and chat panes.
