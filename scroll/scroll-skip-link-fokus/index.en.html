Skip Links That Actually Move Focus on Clincoo

A Skip to content link that only changes the hash without moving focus fails keyboard users. Combine href, tabindex, and scrollIntoView.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo site with a long nav needs a Skip to content link. If main cannot take focus, the next Tab returns to the menu instead of the article.

Give main an id="konten" and tabindex="-1". tabindex -1 lets script focus the target without putting it in the regular Tab order.

After the skip link is clicked, call focus() on main. Some browsers do not move focus from a hash alone. scrollIntoView helps align the viewport with a sticky header.

Keep a visible focus ring. Do not remove outline on main:focus. Keyboard users must see where they landed.

Test by Tabbing from the top of the page in editor.clincoo.buzz preview and on a phone with an external keyboard. This pattern follows WAI and MDN practice.
