🚀 Deploy

Preview First, Deploy Second

The Clincoo editor preview is your last safety net. Check desktop and phone before the site actually goes live.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The deploy button feels tempting the moment a page looks tidy enough. Hold on. Preview in the Clincoo editor exists so you see the site the way visitors will — not the way you see it in a code tab.

Open preview, scroll top to bottom, click every link and button. Watch for clipped text, oversized images, and forms that do nothing. Then shrink the window or use phone mode: many layout issues only appear on a small screen.

If something looks off, fix it in the editor, save, and preview again. Repeat until you would be fine with a stranger opening it. Then deploy. Five minutes of checking is cheaper than explaining to a friend why the button does not click.

Treat preview as a dress rehearsal. Deploy is opening night. You can always deploy again later, but a visitor's first impression only happens once.
