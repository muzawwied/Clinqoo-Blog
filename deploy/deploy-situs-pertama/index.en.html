Deploy Your First Site to the Internet in Minutes

From editor to an address anyone can open — here is how publishing works through the Clincoo Deploy menu.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Every beginner’s favorite moment: seeing their own site online, openable by anyone anywhere. In Clincoo that moment needs no complicated setup — deploying is simply part of the editor workflow.

The flow: make sure your changes are saved, open the Deploy menu, then publish. Clincoo processes your project files and serves them as a static site on a global network — so visitors from different regions load your site from the location nearest to them.

Once live, save your site’s address and share it. Later code changes? Just deploy again — the new version replaces the old. There is no starting over; deploy is a repeat button you can press anytime.

One last tip: before deploying, open the preview and check how it looks on a phone-sized screen. Most of your first visitors arrive on mobile, and you want the best first impression.
