Check the HTTPS Redirect after a Clincoo Release

HTTP that does not jump to HTTPS mixes assets and triggers browser warnings.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

After a release from editor.clincoo.buzz, open a fresh tab and type the URL with http://, not https://.

The response should be 301 or 308 to the same host on https. Do not accept a 200 on HTTP just because the internal preview passed.

In DevTools, Network panel, confirm the document, CSS, and JS are all https. An http asset triggers mixed content and may be blocked.

Also test the URL with and without a trailing slash. Both should land on one canonical.

Ask AI to draft the checklist of URLs, not to rewrite the server. Paste only the status and the Location header.

Record the result on blog.clincoo.buzz, then open app.clincoo.buzz once more from a network other than the office Wi-Fi.
