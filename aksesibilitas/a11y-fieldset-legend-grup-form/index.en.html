Group Clincoo Form Fields with fieldset and legend

Radios and checkboxes without a fieldset lose context in screen readers. Wrap the group with a clear legend.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In the Clincoo editor, booking or quiz forms often show a row of “Plan A/B/C” radios with no group title. Sighted users see a heading above; screen readers only hear “Plan A, radio, not checked” without the original question.

The fix is simple: wrap related options in a fieldset and put the question or group name in a legend. Do not replace the legend with a styled heading alone if those options are one decision.

One fieldset per decision. Do not wrap the entire form in a giant fieldset; split “Plan”, “Payment”, and “Shipping” so form navigation stays short.

Test in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview with Tab and, if possible, a screen reader. Ask Clincoo AI to add fieldset and legend only, not rewrite form CSS.

This semantic markup renders as-is on the public Clincoo site. Clear groups cut mis-taps and speed up mobile forms.
