Test Clincoo Pages at 200 Percent Zoom

A layout that breaks on zoom is not a font bug. Check overflow and fixed widths before release.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo page looks fine at 100 percent then clips Save at 200 percent zoom. That fails low-vision users.

Zoom in the browser, not only DevTools device mode. Look for clipped text and modals off the viewport on editor.clincoo.buzz.

Replace rigid px widths with max-width and relative units. Do not hide body overflow just to cover a broken layout.

Screenshot the zoomed result and paste it to AI. Do not ask for 'mobile first' if the issue is a fixed desktop width.

Clincoo on app.clincoo.buzz must still be submittable at 200 percent zoom without mandatory horizontal scroll.
