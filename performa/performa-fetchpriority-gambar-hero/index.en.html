Set fetchpriority high on the Clincoo Hero Image

The hero image is often the LCP candidate. Mark fetchpriority="high" and never lazy-load the above-the-fold image.

Indonesia · English

Clincoo pages often put loading="lazy" on every img, including the hero. Lazy on LCP delays the largest image and leaves the headline hanging.

web.dev recommends fetchpriority="high" for the LCP image. Put it on one hero only. Do not mark five images as high.

Keep intrinsic width and height so CLS stays low. fetchpriority does not replace reserved space.

Measure in preview and on the public URL. If LCP shifts to a logo or a font, the hero may be too heavy or hidden by CSS.

Clincoo does not rewrite network priority. The attributes you save in HTML are what the browser sees.
