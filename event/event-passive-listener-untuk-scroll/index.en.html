Attach Scroll Listeners as Passive

A non-passive scroll listener forces the browser to wait for preventDefault and makes swipes feel stuck.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In editor.clincoo.buzz find addEventListener for scroll, wheel, or touchmove. If the handler never calls preventDefault, add the passive true option.

Do not mark it passive and still call preventDefault. The browser ignores the call and the console warns.

To close a menu on scroll, set state. You do not need to cancel page scrolling.

Test on a phone at app.clincoo.buzz. The swipe should follow the finger, not wait for the next frame.

Ask AI to mark listeners that are safe to make passive. Paste the handler function, not the whole bundle.

Note exceptions on blog.clincoo.buzz, such as a canvas that really must consume the wheel event.
