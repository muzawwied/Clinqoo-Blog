Use noindex on Test Pages in the Clincoo Head

A drafted page that gets indexed competes with the official URL. Mark tests with robots noindex.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

A test folder in a Clincoo repo sometimes ships without a robots meta tag. Search then ranks the draft above the official page.

Add a robots meta noindex, nofollow on test pages in editor.clincoo.buzz.

Do not leave noindex on a production page. Remove the tag when the draft becomes the canonical URL.

Ask AI to add the robots meta on the test file only. Paste the draft head, not the production index.

Clincoo ships files as saved. noindex keeps drafts from competing with links on blog.clincoo.buzz.
