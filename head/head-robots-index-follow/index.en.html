Set Meta Robots to index, follow in the Clincoo Head

A leftover noindex from a draft keeps a finished page out of search results.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo draft pages sometimes use noindex so they stay out of search. The tag is easy to leave behind after you publish.

For public pages use . Reserve noindex for internal previews, thank-you pages, or URLs that must stay out of the index.

Do not mix a head noindex with a robots.txt block on the same folder without a reason. Google Search Central explains how both signals work together.

MDN lists valid robots values. One robots tag per document is enough; duplicates confuse crawlers.

Before you deploy from editor.clincoo.buzz, search the project for noindex. Remove leftovers and confirm in View Source.
