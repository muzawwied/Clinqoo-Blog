Fill og:title and og:image in the Clincoo Head

Without Open Graph, shared chat links show a random title and no image.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

When a Clincoo URL is shared in chat or social apps, crawlers read og:title, og:description, and og:image. Missing tags produce an empty or ugly link preview.

Copy the page title into og:title, the description into og:description, and point og:image at a 1200×630 graphic or your existing logo. og:type article and og:site_name Clincoo keep the brand consistent.

Do not point og:image at a local file that is not deployed. Use an absolute https://your-domain/logo.png URL so crawlers can fetch it.

The Open Graph protocol and MDN meta property docs describe the property attribute. web.dev treats social previews as part of the HTML document.

After you save the head in editor.clincoo.buzz, share the link to yourself. Social caches may lag a few minutes if the card looks stale.
