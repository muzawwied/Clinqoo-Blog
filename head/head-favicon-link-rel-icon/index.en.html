Add a Favicon with link rel=icon in the Clincoo Head

Without a favicon the browser tab shows a blank icon and the site brand is harder to recognize.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

New Clincoo projects often forget to link a favicon. The browser tab then shows a generic document even though a logo file already sits in the root folder.

Add in the head of every HTML page. The path must match a file that actually exists in the project.

A single 32×32 or 192×192 PNG is enough for tabs. Apple touch icons and a manifest can wait; do not let AI inject dozens of sizes at once.

MDN documents rel=icon and the MIME type. web.dev recommends a simple mark that stays readable on a tiny tab.

Check the Clincoo preview: the icon should appear on the tab. If it 404s, fix the href before you publish from editor.clincoo.buzz.
