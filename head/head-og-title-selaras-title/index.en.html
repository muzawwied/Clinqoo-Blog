Keep og:title Aligned with the Clincoo Page Title

Open Graph that differs from the title makes a share preview feel like another site.

This article is also available in: Indonesia · English

AI adds a generic og:title while the page title is already specific. The share card then mismatches the tab.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, copy the title text into og:title, or shorten it only if the card needs a tighter line.

Also match og:url to the canonical. Do not point og:image at a file you have not committed.

Ask AI to tidy three Open Graph tags only. Paste the head, do not rewrite the body.

Clincoo renders the tags you save. A consistent share card keeps the brand intact on blog.clincoo.buzz.
