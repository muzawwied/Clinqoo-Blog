Write One Tagline and Reuse It in Clincoo

Different taglines in the hero, meta, and footer confuse visitors. One sentence anchors the brand.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo hero says "build sites fast", the footer says "visual HTML editor", and the meta title uses another slogan.

Write one tagline in brand.md in editor.clincoo.buzz. Copy that sentence into the hero, og:description, and footer.

Do not let AI rewrite the slogan every time you ask for page copy. Tell AI to use the established tagline.

Paste the official tagline when you ask for help. Reject suggestions that change the product promise.

Clincoo shows the text you type. One tagline keeps the message consistent on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz.
