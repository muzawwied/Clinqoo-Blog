Use a Fixed Spacing Scale so Clincoo Layouts Feel on Brand

Random margins make pages look improvised. A 4/8/16/24 scale keeps brand rhythm.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo cards use 13px padding, sections 22px, and the footer 18px. Visual rhythm breaks.

Define --space-1 through --space-6 on :root in editor.clincoo.buzz. Use those tokens for padding and gap.

Do not let AI insert "just right" px values. Ask it to snap to the nearest token.

Paste :root and one stray card. Ask AI to replace only the wild numbers.

Clincoo does not unify spacing for you. A fixed scale makes the brand feel tidy on app.clincoo.buzz.
