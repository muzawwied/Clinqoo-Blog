Set One Display Font and One Body Font in Clincoo

Too many type families make a brand look random. Two font roles are enough for a Clincoo site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo site uses Inter on home, Poppins on about, and Georgia on the blog. Visitors never hear one visual voice.

Pick one display font and one body font in editor.clincoo.buzz. Load them once, then use .display and .body across pages.

Do not accept an AI suggestion that adds a third font "to feel more alive". Life comes from size hierarchy, not a Google Fonts catalog.

Ask AI to replace only the stray font-family rules. Paste the @font-face block and one wrong heading.

Clincoo renders the fonts you link. A fixed pair keeps identity tidy on app.clincoo.buzz.
