Check Text Contrast on Clincoo Brand Colors

Brand buttons and badges often fail contrast. Measure light text on the brand color before you publish.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

In editor.clincoo.buzz open a page that uses the brand color as the background of a button, badge, or banner. Do not judge it by eye on a bright screen only.

Open DevTools, select the text on the brand color, and read the contrast ratio. A common target is 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text or UI components.

If it fails, do not override the token with an inline hex. Darken or lighten the color token in one place, then preview again on app.clincoo.buzz.

Also check hover and disabled states. A lighter hover often drops contrast exactly when the user is focused.

Write down the failing pairs, then ask AI to adjust the token only. Do not ask for a full palette rewrite. Keep the note on blog.clincoo.buzz.
