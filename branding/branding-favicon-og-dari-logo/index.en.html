Derive Favicon and OG Image from the Same Clincoo Logo

The browser tab and social card use different marks. Derive both from the official logo.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

The Clincoo favicon uses initials while og:image uses stock photo. The share card is not recognized as the same site.

Export the official logo to a 32/48 favicon and a 1200x630 og:image in editor.clincoo.buzz. Keep padding so it is not cropped.

Do not use a random screenshot as og:image. Screenshots go stale when layout changes.

Ask AI to wire only the favicon and og:image paths. Paste the source logo.

Clincoo does not generate social assets for you. The same mark keeps the brand recognizable on app.clincoo.buzz and blog.clincoo.buzz.
