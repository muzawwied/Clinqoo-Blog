Keep Photo Ratios Consistent so Clincoo Galleries Look on Brand

Mixing 4:3 and 16:9 photos breaks a grid. One ratio protects the brand face.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo gallery shows portraits, landscapes, and squircles in one row. Cards jump unevenly.

Pick an official ratio such as 3:2 in editor.clincoo.buzz. Crop every photo to that ratio before upload to assets.

Do not rely on object-fit alone without a cropped source. AI often adds CSS crops that clip faces.

Ask AI to add aspect-ratio on the container only. Paste one gallery card as the reference.

Clincoo displays the files you upload. A fixed ratio keeps galleries tidy on app.clincoo.buzz.
