Keep a Clincoo Dropdown to One Level

Nested submenus are hard to tap on a phone. One level is enough for a small site.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo templates sometimes nest a submenu inside another submenu. A finger on a small screen often misses the second item.

If you need a group, use one shallow dropdown or split it into a list page. Avoid hover-only menus without a button.

Test in the editor.clincoo.buzz preview at 360px width. Every item must open with a tap, not only hover.

Ask the AI to flatten the menu to one level. Refuse a mega-menu plugin.

A Clincoo site rarely needs a navigation tree. A short list is safer.
