Write Clincoo Menu Labels That Name the Destination

Click here does not explain the page. Use words like Services or Contact.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A Clincoo menu that says Click here or Read more forces visitors to guess the destination.

Replace every label with the page name: Home, Services, About, Contact. One or two words is enough.

Check in editor.clincoo.buzz that the link text matches the destination title. Do not use an icon without text.

Ask the AI to tidy the text in one menu file. Refuse an automatic label generator.

Clear Clincoo navigation can be read without opening the link.
