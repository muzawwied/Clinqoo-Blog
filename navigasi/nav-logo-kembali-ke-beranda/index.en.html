Make the Clincoo Logo a Link Back Home

Visitors click the logo to go home. Wrap the image with a link to index.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Many Clincoo templates leave the logo as a plain img. Visitors deep in the site click it and nothing happens.

Wrap the logo with a link to / or index.html. Use alt text that names the site, not company logo.

Test in editor.clincoo.buzz from the Contact page: click the logo, return Home. Do not point the logo at social media.

Ask the AI to change one header. Refuse a script that rewrites the whole navigation.

On a static Clincoo site, the logo is a home link, not decoration.
