Close the Clincoo Hamburger Menu after a Visitor Picks a Link

A mobile menu that stays open covers the destination. Close the panel on link click or Escape.

This article is available in: Indonesian · English

Clincoo hamburger menus often use a checkbox or an .open class. If that class is not removed after a link click, the overlay still covers the new page on a single-page site, or stays open when the hash changes.

Add a click listener on links inside nav. After navigation, remove the open class and set aria-expanded="false" on the trigger button.

Support Escape and a click outside the panel. Keyboard visitors must close the menu without reloading the editor.clincoo.buzz preview.

Do not rely on hover to close. Phones have no reliable hover; open state must be explicit.

Test the path: open the menu, choose Contact, confirm the overlay is gone and focus moves to the content. Then test again with the skip link.
