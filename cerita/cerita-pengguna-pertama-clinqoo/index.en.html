Clincoo’s First Users: The Start of a Living Ecosystem

How the first sign-up on 5 September 2026 marked Clincoo as a real platform used by real people, not just an experiment.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Clincoo started with one independent developer, then was tested by the first person willing to sign up on 5 September 2026.

First users open the editor, try a template, hit deploy, and see a site appear on a .clinqoo.biz.id subdomain.

Clincoo keeps the path short: template or blank start, edit, ask the AI when stuck, then one-click deploy to Cloudflare Pages.
