The Story Behind Clincoo: Muzawwied Ilman Al-Fauza

Clincoo is built by Muzawwied Ilman Al-Fauza, an independent full-stack developer from Indonesia. He built the platform from scratch, on his own, focused on web development, cloud automation, and products that are genuinely user-centered.

An unusual learning path

His educational background is unusual. He attended formal school up to the 3rd grade, then continued his education at an Islamic boarding school until the age of 14. From that point on, his learning path shifted to hands-on practice: writing code, designing systems, and building products other people can use.

How Clincoo was born

The story of how Clincoo was born is less dramatic. Muzawwied built it purely out of curiosity: he had no projects at the time, so he tried focusing on web development — learning by doing, guided consistently by AI. In a little over a month, that casual experiment turned into a complete platform: a code editor, a built-in AI assistant, a ready-made template gallery, and one-click deploy with a free subdomain address. That point was reached one month before his 16th birthday.

The first user registered on September 5, 2026. Since then Clincoo has kept growing — team collaboration, the MCP Server, plugin integrations, and an ever-expanding template gallery — all with the same goal: making websites feel close to everyone.

Vision and mission

  • Vision — making website creation feel as easy as typing and as fast as one click, and affordable for everyone in Indonesia.
  • Mission — being a complete no-code + AI platform that stays free to start, surrounded by a creator ecosystem that shares templates.
