The Story Behind Clinqoo: Muzawwied Ilman Al-Fauza

Who founded Clinqoo, how the platform was born, and the vision that drives it — from a simple idea to a site anyone can build.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Muzawwied Ilman Al-Fauza, an independent full-stack developer from Indonesia, built Clinqoo from scratch on his own. His focus sits on web development, cloud automation, and products that put users first.

His education path was unconventional. He attended formal school through third grade, then left and continued at a pesantren (Islamic boarding school) until the age of 14. From there, learning shifted to practice: writing code, designing systems, and building products other people could actually use.

Clinqoo began with a simple question: why does building a website have to feel complicated and expensive? The answer became a platform where anyone — without deep coding skills — can create a professional site. It includes a code editor, a built-in AI assistant, a template gallery, and one-click deploy to Cloudflare Pages, plus a free .clinqoo.biz.id subdomain.

The first user signed up on 5 September 2026. Within two weeks, Clinqoo already had active users and kept growing. One of the latest steps is the community template gallery program: user work can be shared and reused by others, so the creator ecosystem strengthens itself.

Clinqoo’s vision is clear: making website creation feel as easy as typing and as fast as one click, and keeping it affordable for everyone in Indonesia. Its mission is to be a complete no-code + AI platform that stays free to start, with a creator ecosystem that shares templates with one another.
