Community Template Gallery: When User Work Becomes a Starting Point

Why Clinqoo opened a community template gallery — so a good site does not stop in one account, but can be reused and improved by others.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A blank page feels intimidating. Templates ease that. On Clinqoo the starting point can come from other users.

Work is shared, then text, color, and images are swapped. The code still opens in the editor.
