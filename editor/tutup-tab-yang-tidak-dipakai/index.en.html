Close Editor Tabs You Are No Longer Touching

Piled-up tabs split your focus. In Clinqoo, keep only the files you are changing open.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

Every file you open in the Clinqoo editor gets a tab. That is handy when comparing HTML and CSS. It is not handy when ten tabs from yesterday are still sitting there.

Close what you have finished. If you are unsure, reopen from the file explorer — that is faster than hunting a tab with a similar name. Save before you close so nothing floats unsaved.

Keep two or three working tabs: the page you are changing, its stylesheet, and maybe a script. More than that usually means you switched tasks without closing the loop.

A lean workspace makes preview and AI chat feel close. Focus is not an editor feature; it is what remains after you decide which tabs may stay.
