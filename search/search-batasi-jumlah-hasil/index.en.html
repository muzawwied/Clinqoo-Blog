Limit How Many Clincoo Search Hits Render at Once

Rendering hundreds of rows at once makes the page heavy. Slice the list, state the remainder, and offer show all.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

A static Clincoo index can hold hundreds of titles. A client-side filter that appends every match to the DOM overwhelms scroll and screen readers.

In editor.clincoo.buzz, after filtering, slice the array to 8–12 items. Show There are N results, showing the first M plus a show all button that renders the rest without another fetch.

Skip complex pagination when the data is already in memory. One cap plus a button is easier to test. Rank exact title matches before body snippets.

Ask AI to slice results before innerHTML. Paste the loop that currently appends every item. Test with a one-letter query that hits many pages.

Clincoo runs the script you save. A short list keeps preview and app.clincoo.buzz fast.
