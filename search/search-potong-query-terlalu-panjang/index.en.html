Trim an Oversized Clincoo Search Query to a Safe Length

Pasting a whole article into the box blows the URL and the API. Cap length before fetch.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, pasting 4,000 characters into search breaks the URL and returns 414.

Clamp the query on the client, for example 80–120 characters. Show that search uses the prefix. Do not silently drop the rest.

Ask AI for one clamp plus a message. Do not request a new backend.

Test a long paste on app.clincoo.buzz. Record the cap on blog.clincoo.buzz.
