Focus the First Clincoo Search Hit when Enter Is Pressed

Enter without a target submits the form and reloads. Move to the first result or let the list take focus.

This article is available in: Indonesia · English

On editor.clincoo.buzz, Enter in the search box submits the form and reloads a blank page.

preventDefault on submit. If there are hits, focus the first item or open its link. If empty, leave the empty message readable.

Ask AI for an Enter keydown plus submit handler. Do not request a full combobox library.

Test the keyboard on app.clincoo.buzz. Record the focus order on blog.clincoo.buzz.
